# Studies of pkdb_data

`studies/hydrochlorothiazide/Patel1984` and `studies/hydrochlorothiazide/Nilsen1989` (with scatters) are copies of the studies of study format 2 of [pkdb_data](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkdb_data) (MIT license, Copyright (c) 2025 Matthias König) without their publications; their figures and tables are blank images of one pixel. The tests of `pkpdsim.data` read them with the library of PK-DB.
