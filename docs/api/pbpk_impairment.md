# pbpk impairment

The impaired organ function of the library: the factors of the Child-Pugh classes, the CKD stages and the NYHA classes on the physiology, the binding and the proteins of the liver, the factors of cirrhosis from the consensus of the parameter database (`child_pugh_factor`, `child_pugh_protein`) and the mapping of a MELD score onto the severity of cirrhosis (`cp_severity_of_meld`, `meld_of_cp_severity`, `meld_anchors`), see [Impairment](../pbpk/impairment.md).
