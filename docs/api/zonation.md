# zonation

The zonation engine, see [Zonated liver](../zonation.md): the settings of a zonated liver, the geometry of its sinusoid, the gradients along the lobule, the transport of a substance, the flow through the sinusoids, the cell model per zone, the observers, the build, the id scheme and the figures. The engine writes plain SBML with the [sbml](sbml.md) kit and imports no other library of pkpdsim: a caller (a zonated liver of a body model, a perfused organ) hands over its boundary and its cell model.
