Model controls and the time of the surgery

The gain of the buffer response is the fraction of a lost portal inflow the hepatic artery of that lobe compensates, the growth constant sets how fast the anatomy follows, and the time of the surgery decides whether the intervention is applied before the simulation or to the running model. A named procedure at the default controls is a precomputed reference run; anything else runs the compiled model here.

2 · The five days which follow

day 0

The area of the whole liver is the mass which is there, so a hepatectomy shrinks it and the regrowth fills it again; the area of a block is the lobe inside it. Click a block to cycle its state.

The models, the solver and the licenses of the runtime are those of sbmlsim-core; this page runs the same compiled SBML as the documentation does, it holds no second model of a liver.
