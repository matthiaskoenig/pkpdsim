The anatomy of the simulation

The lobes of the liver

The area of a block is the share of the liver the lobe holds at the shown time, so the atrophy of a deportalised lobe and the hypertrophy of its neighbours are a change of area. Click a block, or focus it and press Enter, to cycle intact, portal vein ligated, hepatic artery ligated and resected. A resected lobe holds no mass, so it leaves the liver and is listed below it, one click from coming back.

The procedures of the literature and the model choices

Presets and controls

A preset at the default controls is a precomputed reference run; moving a control runs the compiled model in a Web Worker, which takes a moment. The gain of the buffer response is the fraction of a lost portal inflow the hepatic artery of that lobe compensates, the growth constant sets how fast the anatomy follows, and the time of the surgery decides whether the intervention is applied before the simulation or to the running model.
