# Context of use and credibility

The fit metrics of a model pool its validation data: one GMFE and one percentage within 2-fold for doses between the fitted doses, interactions, held-out subjects and processes the model is known not to describe. A number like that answers no question. What the model is credible for is not written down anywhere. This page describes how a model declares what it is used for, which evidence supports each use and when the use is accepted, and how `run_credibility` assesses the declaration against the adopted fits of the model.

The credibility is the one of sbmlsim (`sbmlsim.credibility`, the kind `credibility` of `python -m sbmlsim.run`): the declaration, the selection of the evidence, the assessment, the verification, the parameter uncertainty, the predictive check, the lock, the report and the scorecard. pkpdsim adds what is specific to the PBPK models of pkdb_models (`pkpdsim.credibility`), which every model registers by its credibility settings (`pkpdsim.model.CREDIBILITY_SETTINGS`): the metadata of its fit mappings (`PKPDMappingMetaData`) with the fields a domain claims (`DOMAIN_FIELDS`), the evidence extensions of the ratio pairs ([Ratios](#ratios)), the temporal validation ([Temporal validation](#temporal-validation)), the equivalency of the inputs ([Equivalency of the inputs](#equivalency-of-the-inputs)) and the study data ([Study data](#study-data)), and the PK battery as the battery hook ([Battery of the builds](#battery-of-the-builds)). The evidence of an extension is a section of the record of a context or of the assessment by the name of the extension (`ContextResult.section("ratios")`, `CredibilityAssessment.extensions`); the records of pkpdsim 0.17 to 0.19, which have it as fields of their own, are read as these sections.

## The frame, the declaration, the evidence and the verdicts

The frame of ASME V&V 40, the ladder of the contexts of use `C1` to `C5`, the model risk with its default acceptance and minimum of points, how a model declares its contexts of use (`Credibility`, `ContextOfUse`, `QuantityOfInterest`, `Domain`, `Evidence`, `Unclaimed`), the checks of a declaration, the roles of the evidence, the kinds from the records, the verdicts and their order are described in [Credibility](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim/credibility/) of sbmlsim, which also describes the verification of the predictions, the generic battery, the cross-validation, the propagation of the parameter uncertainty, the variability and the predictive check, the locked splits and the report. This page keeps what pkpdsim adds to them.

A model package gets a module `credibility.py` with the object `CREDIBILITY`, a `Credibility`, and references it in its model object like the experiments and the fit definitions, `credibility=".credibility:CREDIBILITY"` (see [Models and their commands](models.md)); it is resolved when a command uses it. The example of the declaration is the module of the test package `tests/pkpdsim_credibility`, a decay with an intravenous dose.

The `Domain` of a context of use claims values by the fields of the metadata of the fit mappings of pkpdsim (`route`, `health`, `genotype`, `phenotype`, `coadministration`, `dosing`, `fasting`, `application_form`, `tissue`; members of the enums of `pkpdsim.metadata`, the `DOMAIN_FIELDS` of the credibility settings). The dose is not a field of the metadata, so a dose range is a statement only.

When the model loads it (`load_credibility`, `sbmlsim.project.Project.load_credibility`), the declaration is checked against the credibility settings of the model as well: the claimed metadata values are members of the enums of the fields of `DOMAIN_FIELDS`, a setting of `extensions` names an extension of the model and a pairing of a ratio quantity is claimed by one (a `Ratio` by the extension `ratios`).

`run_credibility` runs the kind `credibility` of sbmlsim and the commands `check_declaration(model)` and the lock work as described there; `--lock` writes `credibility.lock.json` into the folder of the model, to be committed and reviewed with the declaration, and `--no-verification` skips the verification and the battery.

## Ratios

A question of a special population is a ratio: how much higher is the exposure in renal impairment than in the healthy subjects of the same study? A fold criterion on the absolute predictions is too lenient for it near a ratio of 1, two predictions within 2-fold each may give a ratio which is 4-fold off. A ratio quantity with a `Ratio` compares the ratio directly (`pkpdsim.credibility.ratios`, the evidence extension `ratios`):

```python
# not executed
from sbmlsim.credibility import QuantityOfInterest

from pkpdsim.credibility import Ratio, Summary
from pkpdsim.fit import metadata_filter
from pkpdsim.metadata import Health

QuantityOfInterest(
    "auc_ratio_renal",
    "the AUC of HCTZ in renal impairment over the healthy one",
    ratio=Ratio(
        observable="Carm_hctz",
        numerator=metadata_filter(health={Health.RENAL_IMPAIRMENT}),
        denominator=metadata_filter(health={Health.HEALTHY}),
        summary=Summary.AUC,
    ),
)
```

- The arms are paired in the decisive points of the COU: per fit and study (the experiment) every mapping of the observable which `numerator` selects with the one mapping which `denominator` selects. A study without its reference arm gives no pair; a study with more than one mapping of the denominator is an error of the selection, so the reference arm is part of the decisive evidence (e.g. a validation evidence of the healthy arms of the studies). The observable of a ratio restricts the evidence of its COU like an absolute quantity.
- The summary of an arm is the same for the data and the predictions: `Summary.AUC` the area under its points by the trapezoid (at least two points with a finite data value and prediction), `Summary.VALUE` the value of its single point (a PK parameter). The observed ratio is the one of the data, the predicted ratio the one of the predictions.
- The limit of Guest et al. (2011) of an observed ratio `R` (its inverse below 1) is `(delta + 2 (R - 1)) / R` with `delta` 1.25: 1.25 at a ratio of 1, 1.625 at 2 or 0.5, towards 2 for a large ratio; a pair is within when the predicted ratio lies between the observed ratio divided by and times the limit.
- The rule `GuestAcceptance(minimum)`, the percentage of the evaluable pairs within the limits, is the setting of the extension of a COU, `ContextOfUse(extensions={"ratios": GuestAcceptance(80)})`, by default `DEFAULT_RATIO_ACCEPTANCE` of the risk: 50 % for a low, 67 % for a medium and 80 % for a high risk. It joins the verdict like a rule of the points: not met is `not_supported`, no evaluable pair `insufficient_evidence`; it is the last row of the acceptance of the COU, with the extension `ratios`. The verification and the parameter uncertainty evaluate it on the alternative predictions as well.
- The factor relevance of the quantities of interest is `assessed` for a COU with an assessed ratio, by its pairs and the coverage of its ratios.

`ratios.tsv` has one row per COU and pair (`context`, `quantity`, `fit`, `experiment`, `numerator`, `denominator`, `observed`, `predicted`, `limit`, `within`), the report and the release page a table of the pairs per COU and the rule of the pairs (`Guest`) in the acceptance, which `contexts.tsv` has as the columns `Guest` and `Guest_limit`. The pairs are the section `ratios` of the record of a COU with an assessed ratio, a COU without one has none (pkpdsim 0.19 wrote an empty list); a record of 0.18 or earlier has no pairs.


## Verification

The three checks of the verification of the predictions (`tolerances`, `integrator`, `setup`) are those of sbmlsim, see [Verification](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim/credibility/#verification). The experiments of pkpdsim run on roadrunner through sbmlsim; the battery below compares the builds with AMICI, an engine of sbmlsim.

## Battery of the builds

The checks above ask whether the predictions a verdict is decided on are the solution of the model. The battery asks whether the model itself is sound, without any data. It is the battery of sbmlsim (`sbmlsim.credibility.battery`, `run_battery`) on the variants of the model, its builds (`model_paths`, the male build and a female one), with the PK battery of pkpdsim as its hook (`pkpdsim.credibility.battery`, `PK_BATTERY`): every build is simulated with roadrunner at `rtol 1e-10` and `atol 1e-16` for two days on a 10 min grid (`HORIZON`, `STEPS`), without a dose and with a dose of 10 mg of every route of every substance (an infusion over 60 min), and the results are checked against what every PBPK model must fulfil; `mass_balance`, `zero_dose` and `superposition` are the checks of the PK battery, the others the generic ones of sbmlsim:

| check | factor | what is checked |
|---|---|---|
| `dose_states` | use error | every state in mg which a reaction reads is a dose state the battery knows (`dose_prefixes` of the module, the qualifier of a submodel stripped, one case per build), so no dose of that shape is simulated by nothing; a state which is neither is a dose state or a depot of a dose nothing simulates, and the check names it with the prefix to add and the known failure to declare instead of leaving it out in silence. The detector is a heuristic in both directions and no guarantee that no dose is skipped: it finds a parameter with a rate rule, in mg, which a reaction reads, with no false positive over the tracked builds of pkdb_models, and it does not find a dose state in another unit, one which is a species or one which is a constant without a rate rule (none of the three occurs in the tracked builds). The mass balance keeps its arithmetic meaning and does not fail for an unknown state |
| `mass_balance` | numerical code verification | the amount of the substances in the model, in its sinks and in its dose states (in mmole, a dose state in mg divided by the molecular weight) changes by the infusion and the flows of the reactions which read no dose alone, at most `1e-6` of the largest term (`MASS_LIMIT`): a dose enters with its molecular weight, and nothing appears or disappears outside of the reactions (a rule or an event on a species of a substance is a problem of its own) |
| `non_negativity` | numerical code verification | no species becomes negative beyond `1e-6` of its largest value in any simulation of the battery |
| `zero_dose` | numerical code verification | without a dose the species of a substance which starts at zero (a drug, not an endogenous substance) stay zero |
| `steady_state` | numerical code verification | without a dose every state but the sinks (the states nothing drains) stays within `1e-6` of its start (`STEADY_LIMIT`): the physiology and the endogenous substances start at their steady state, which the PK battery declares for every model (`BatterySettings.steady_state`) |
| `superposition` | numerical code verification | twice a dose of 0.01 mg gives twice the response and two doses at 0 and 12 h the sum of their responses (the response is the difference to the simulation without a dose), within `0.01` of the largest response: the model is linear and time invariant in the limit of a small dose |
| `cross_simulator` | numerical code verification | AMICI, an engine of sbmlsim with its own compilation of the SBML, gives the results of roadrunner without a dose and with every dose of 10 mg (a bolus and the infusion over 60 min), within `0.01` |
| `plausibility` | use error | the default individual has finite values, positive compartments and species which are not negative, and no value of an adopted fit lies within `1e-3` of the range of a bound (in the scale of the fit, `BOUND_MARGIN`): a value at a bound is decided by the bound, not by the data |
| `rebuild` | software quality assurance | the factory of the model builds its models again (`Project.create_models` into a temporary directory), equal but for the dates of the model history; with the current pkpdsim, so the builds are those of the current library |

The battery reads the conventions of the models of pkdb_models from the flat SBML (`FlatBuild`): a substance has its molecular weight `Mr_<s>`, its doses are the dose states of `dose_prefixes` (mg, the qualifier of a submodel stripped, so a dose state of a flattened submodel is a dose state) and the infusion rate `Ri_<s>` (mg/min): the dose states of the PBPK library (`pkpdsim.pbpk.equilibration.DOSE_PREFIXES`, `IVDOSE_<s>`, `PODOSE_<s>`, `SOLDOSE_<s>` and the depots `IMDOSE_<s>`, `SCDOSE_<s>`, `RECDOSE_<s>`, so a route the library gains is a dose of the battery) and `LEGACY_DOSE_STATES`, the dose states of the models which are not built with the library, each with the model which has it (`INHDOSE_<s>` inhaled, `BUDOSE_<s>` buccal, `REDOSE_<s>` rectal before `RECDOSE_<s>`, `SLDOSE_<s>` sublingual, `POSOLUTION_<s>` an oral solution); a state in mg which a reaction reads and which is neither is a dose state or a depot of a dose the battery does not know, which the check `dose_states` reports (`FlatBuild.unknown_doses`) instead of leaving it out in silence, so it is given its prefix or the check is declared as a known failure with its reason, while the mass balance keeps its arithmetic meaning and does not fail for it, and a species belongs to the substance its id (the part after the last `__`) ends with, `_<s>`, or else starts with, `<s>_`. A parameter in mg whose rate rule reads a dose (the drug in the stomach of some models) is a state of the dose while a reaction reads it; a sum of the given doses is none. A reaction is an input when it reads a dose of a substance it forms; a secretion which a dose of another substance stimulates is a flow. A check without what it needs is not applicable and assesses nothing: the checks of the PK battery without a substance with a molecular weight, the mass balance and the superposition without a dose, the zero dose without a substance which starts at zero, the second simulator without AMICI (the extra `amici`: `pip install pkpdsim[amici]`, the extra `amici` of sbmlsim, which builds AMICI from source and compiles every build with a C++ compiler, about a minute and a half for a PBPK model, cached by sbmlsim; a build which AMICI does not compile within 15 min, `BatterySettings.compile_timeout`, e.g. the creatinine model of pkdb_models with its large nested formulas of the starts, which did not compile within 45 min, is not compared and named in the statement of the check, as is a build AMICI does not simulate as roadrunner). The non-negativity, the second simulator and the plausibility of sbmlsim run on every build; the steady state leaves a build without a substance with a molecular weight out (`PKBattery.not_applicable`, not applicable with that reason when no build has one, e.g. the decay of the test model), and a model which is not meant to start at its steady state switches it off in its credibility settings, `CREDIBILITY_SETTINGS.model_copy(update={"battery_settings": {"steady_state": False}})`.

The mass balance records the fate of every dose (`Fate`): at the end of the two days, relative to the simulation without a dose, the amount in the body, in the urine and the feces (the unabsorbed part of a rectal dose among them, which the library sends into `Afeces_<s>`), in the loss of an injected depot (what it does not absorb), not yet applied, and the amount every reaction which reads no dose moved (negative for an elimination, e.g. a metabolism to products the model does not describe, positive for a production, e.g. of a metabolite by the fraction of a branch).

A failed check of the battery makes every context of use `not_verified`. Its checks are rows of the verification of every context, and the factors numerical code verification and software quality assurance are `assessed` with their platform statement followed by the results of the battery. A model whose build fails a check for a reason it accepts declares it with the reason, `CheckSettings(battery={"steady_state": "..."})`: `sbmlsim.project_checks.check_battery` with the battery of the model (`sbmlsim.credibility.battery.run_battery`, the test of a repository of models over its models) then passes, fails when the known failure no longer applies, and an assessment shows the reason and still fails the check.

## Cross-validation

`run_fit <package> --cross-validation <fit id>` refits the problem of an adopted fit once per training study without that study and stores the result with the fit; `run_credibility` reads the cross-validations of the adopted fits, see [Cross-validation](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim/credibility/#cross-validation).

## Temporal validation

A study which the model collected after the day an adopted fit ran cannot have shaped the fit, neither as training data nor by the choices of the modeller who looked at it. Its decisive points test whether the acceptance holds on data which did not exist for the model when it was fitted, the closest an existing model gets to a prospective validation (`pkpdsim.credibility.temporal`, the evidence extension `temporal`).

The day of the collection is recorded where the data are: `collect_tsv_files` writes beside the tables of a study the record `.<study>_study.json` (`pkpdsim.data.StudyRecord`) with the day the model first collected the study, kept by every later collection, and the provenance of the tables, see [Study data](data.md). The release date of PK-DB is no such day, since a model can collect a study from a checkout of pkdb_data before its release, and a release repository receives all data in one commit. The studies a model collected before the records existed were dated once from the git history of pkdb_models (`scripts/backfill_study_records.py`, the dating `git`): the earliest trace of the study, the first commit with a table of the study in its folder (following the renames of the folders) or with a mention of the study in the python code of the model, since a model read many studies from pkdb_data before it kept them and an experiment is written after its data. Such a day is an upper bound, so it is moved back to the day of an adopted fit which was committed with the study present: a model imported with its data and its fits in one commit, or a fit adopted after the study arrived, counts as having known it. A study dated from git is thereby new for a fit only when the history has the fit before any trace of the study. A study without a record has no day (`collected` is `None`) and is never new.

`run_credibility` reads the records of the study folders of every data path of the model and the day of every adopted fit, the date of its id (`chronology`, `sbmlsim.fit.result.fit_date`). A decisive point of a COU above C1 is new when its study was collected after the day of its fit; the same day is not after. Per COU the record has the new studies with their fit, the days of the fit, the collection and the release and their points, the acceptance rules of the COU on the new points, how many decisive studies were collected before their fit and which have no day (the section `temporal` of the record of the COU, `TemporalRecord`; none for a C1 COU, which is decided on calibration data, and for a record of 0.18 or earlier). The new points are decisive points, so they are part of the acceptance already, and the verdict does not change; the statement of the factor output comparison names the acceptance on them, and fewer new evaluable points than the minimum of the COU. `temporal.tsv` has one row per COU and new study, the report and the release page a table per COU.

## Parameter uncertainty

`run_credibility --uncertainty` propagates the uncertainty of the adopted fits to the decisive predictions as described in [Parameter uncertainty](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim/credibility/#parameter-uncertainty); the draws are those of the records of the fits (see [Uncertainty of the fitted parameters](uncertainty.md)).

## Equivalency of the inputs

The evidence of a COU supports it for the inputs its simulations had. An experiment simulates the subjects of its study, the body weight and the height its simulations set (or those of the model when they set none), and its doses; the study reports its subjects and its interventions in the tables of the collection (`TabGroups`, `TabIndividuals`, `TabInterventions`, see [Study data](data.md)). Every assessment compares, for every mapping of the adopted fits, the simulated inputs with the reported ones of the groups, the individuals and the interventions of the rows of its data, without a simulation (`pkpdsim.credibility.inputs`, `input_checks`, the evidence extension `inputs`):

- a covariate of `Credibility.covariates` (the target of the model and the measurement type of PK-DB of its reported value; a declaration without covariates checks `DEFAULT_COVARIATES`, `BW` against the `weight` and `HEIGHT` against the `height`, a model with other ids or further covariates declares its own, an empty mapping checks none) is `equivalent` within 10 % of the reported mean, median or geometric mean of the group, or of the value of an individual (`COVARIATE_TOLERANCE`), `in_range` within the reported range of the group (the minimum and the maximum, else the mean with two standard deviations, `SPREAD`, the SD from the SE and the count, or the geometric mean with its GSD), else it `differs`; a group without the measurement reads the one of its parent, an individual without it the one of its group;
- a dose (a target with a prefix of `dose_prefixes`, the dose states of the battery (the library's and the legacy ones, the infusion rate excluded), in the pre-initialization changes or in a change, also a dose added to its state, `PODOSE_x + 25`) is `equivalent` within 2 % of a dose of the reported interventions of the data (`DOSE_TOLERANCE`; a dose per body weight times the simulated body weight), else it `differs`, e.g. a dose of a salt which the experiment converts to the base;
- an input without a reported value is `not_reported`; a target which a fit fits is no input and not checked.

The values are compared in the unit of the target in the model. A simulation of a scan is checked with the inputs of its base simulation. Per COU (the section `inputs` of its record, `InputsRecord`) the checks of the decisive mappings are summarized per input: the mappings, the range of the simulated values, the inputs the evidence covers, and the counts of the statuses, with every check which differs. The verdict does not change. The factor equivalency of inputs is `assessed` when a decisive mapping has a checked input: the statement names the statuses, the simulated ranges and the first checks which differ (`inputs_statement`); the variability of the subjects, the rest of the factor, is not assessed yet. The scorecard has the column `inputs_differ`. `inputs.tsv` has every check (`fit`, `experiment`, `mapping`, `target`, `kind`, `measurement`, `unit`, `simulated`, `source`, `reported`, `low`, `high`, `reported_by` joined by `;`, `status`, `ratio`), `input_ranges.tsv` one row per COU and input (`context`, `target`, `kind`, `unit`, `n_mappings`, `low`, `high` and the counts); the report and the release page show both per COU. A record of 0.18 or earlier has no checks of the inputs.

## Variability and the predictive check

A model declares the variability of its subjects with `Credibility(variability=Variability({...}))` and `run_credibility --variability [--subjects 64] [--studies 16]` runs the predictive check, see [Variability and the predictive check](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim/credibility/#variability-and-the-predictive-check). For hctz a CV of 30 % of the uptake into the kidney, 40 % of the absorption and 15 % of the body weight of the subjects alone puts 33 % of the 676 evaluable decisive observations of the description (461 of them means of groups) inside their 90 % bands; with a CV of 30 % of both between the studies (8 studies of 16 subjects) 64 %, with 78 % of the plasma and 84 % of the urinary excretion of hctz, while the sodium, chloride and urine volume of the diuresis stay at 16 % or less: the description is `narrow`, the dose and the interaction contexts (86 % and 92 %) `consistent`.

## Study data

An assessment names the data it was computed on (`pkpdsim.credibility.studies`, the evidence extension `studies`): for every study with points in the adopted fits, the first folder of the data paths which has it (the one the experiments read), the fits and the points of the study, and its record of the collection: the days of the first and of the latest collection, the dating of the first, the release in PK-DB (`released`, `pkdb_id`), the source digest of PK-DB (the version of the study in the database), the commit of the checkout of pkdb_data and whether its study folder had uncommitted changes, and the tables of the record with those which differ from it now (`changed`, a table changed by hand after the collection, `-` for a removed and `+` for an added one; `None` for a record without the digests of its tables). A study without a folder (an experiment which generates its data) or without a record is listed as such (the section `studies` of the record of the assessment, `StudyData`, `study_data`; none in a record of 0.18 or earlier). `studies.tsv` has one row per study, the report and the release page a table; the verdicts do not depend on it.

## Across models: scorecard and qualification

Every model is assessed on its own. The scorecard reads the records of many models (`credibility.json`) into one table, one row per model and COU with the level, the risk, the verdict, the points, whether it is verified, the failed checks, the evaluable points of the studies collected after their fit and whether every rule is met on them (`temporal_points`, `temporal_met`) the inputs of the decisive mappings which differ (`inputs_differ`) and the value of every acceptance rule (`sbmlsim.credibility.scorecard`, `scorecard`, with the extensions of pkpdsim, `pkpdsim.credibility.EXTENSIONS`, whose columns follow the ones of sbmlsim). Stored as a TSV it is the baseline of a repository of models: a change of the shared library, of sbmlsim or of the data which makes a COU worse shows across all models at once (`compare`): a COU of the baseline which is missing, a worse verdict (supported, supported with extrapolation, insufficient evidence, not verified, not supported), a COU which is no longer verified, a COU whose rules were met on the studies collected after their fit and no longer are, and a rule whose value got worse by more than the tolerance (5 points of a percentage, 5 % of a GMFE, an AFE 5 % further from 1; a value which is no longer evaluable). A new model, COU or rule is no regression.

The qualification pools the ratio pairs of all models by a value of the metadata of their numerator arm (`sbmlsim.credibility.scorecard.pool` of the extension `ratios`, `RatiosExtension.pool`, by default the field `health`): per value, e.g. `RENAL_IMPAIRMENT` or `CIRRHOSIS`, the evaluable pairs of every model and of all models, how many lie within the limits of Guest et al. and the GMFE of the predicted over the observed ratios. The renal and hepatic scaling of `pkpdsim.pbpk.impairment` is shared by the models; it is qualified for a population when the ratios of the drugs are predicted across drugs, not only for one. A pair counts once even when two COUs of a model share it, and a numerator whose value is not reported (`NR`) counts for no value.

```bash
pkpdsim-verify scorecard results/ --out scorecard.tsv --qualification qualification.tsv
pkpdsim-verify scorecard results/ --baseline scorecard.tsv
```

The command prints the verdicts, the pooled qualification and the regressions against `--baseline`; it exits with 1 for a regression and 2 for a record or baseline it cannot read. `--field` pools by another field of the metadata (`genotype`, `coadministration`), `--tolerance` sets the tolerance.

## Running and reading the report

```bash
run_credibility pkdb_models.models.hctz
run_credibility pkdb_models.models.hctz --no-verification
run_credibility pkdb_models.models.hctz --from-json results/credibility/credibility.json
```

The command runs the kind `credibility` of sbmlsim (`python -m sbmlsim.run credibility '{"project": "pkdb_models.models.hctz:MODEL"}' --out OUT`, which writes the outputs into `OUT/credibility/`) and moves its outputs up into `--output_dir` (`results/credibility` of the model by default) next to the records of the run (`run.json`, `spec.json`, `summary.json`, `progress.jsonl`); an earlier assessment in the directory is replaced, a directory with other files is refused. `--from-json` is the input `record` of the kind, the outputs written again with the current report. The console shows a table of the COUs, their verdicts, what decided them and the evaluable points as `evaluable/minimum`, and below it how many COUs are verified; see [Commands](commands.md). `index.html` of the report (one page, no network) has:

- the **overview**: the COUs with level, risk, verdict (in words and in a colour), the role of the evidence which decided it and the decisive and evaluable points with the minimum, and the limitations of the model;
- a **section per COU**: question, rationale, the evaluable points with their minimum, quantities with their coverage and points (a ratio without a pairing marked as such), the ratio pairs with their limits, the temporal validation (the acceptance on the studies collected after their fit and the studies), the claimed domain with its coverage and the statement (not assessed), the evidence table (role, points, mappings, studies, GMFE, AFE, the percentages of the criteria, the points removed by limitations and outliers), the acceptance table (value, limit, met), the checks of the verification (points, largest difference and its point, whether the acceptance changed, the result and the problems), the inputs of the decisive mappings against the reported ones (see [Equivalency of the inputs](#equivalency-of-the-inputs)), the coverage of the predictive check with the variability of the subjects (see [Variability and the predictive check](#variability-and-the-predictive-check)), the table of the credibility factors and the extrapolation text;
- the **limitations**: their metrics, explicitly outside of any acceptance;
- the **unclaimed validation data**: the validation points per fit which no evidence and no limitation selects (held-out data which answers no declared question);
- the **verification**: the settings of the integrator of every adopted fit and of its alternatives, the issues of the builds, the battery of the builds with the statement of every check and the fate of every dose;
- the **study data**: every study with points in the adopted fits, its folder and points, the days of its first and of its latest collection and how the first is known (`git` for a day of the backfill), its release in PK-DB, the commit of pkdb_data of the collection and whether the tables are the ones the collection wrote (see [Study data](#study-data));
- the **provenance**: the versions of sbmlsim, sbmlutils, libroadrunner and pkpdsim (the package of the extensions), the commit of the model repository and whether the folder of the model had changes (`git status` of the model folder), the adopted fits and the date. A release passes the commit of its source repository (`--source-commit`), the assessment then records it and leaves the dirty state unknown.

The factors of V&V 40 are listed per COU with a status: `assessed` (output comparison, test samples, test conditions and the relevance of the validation activities, from the metrics, the evidence and the coverage; the discretization error, the numerical solver error and the use error from the verification, the numerical code verification and the software quality assurance from the battery after their platform statement), `platform` (a statement which holds for the whole stack, e.g. the code verification by the SBML Test Suite and the PEtab suites of sbmlsim) or `not_assessed` with the step which will assess it, so a reader sees what is missing. Step 1 checks that every claimed quantity which is no ratio has decisive points in the claimed domain (the quantity coverage, under the relevance of the validation activities); whether the quantities answer the question, e.g. the ratios of a special population, is the relevance of the quantities of interest of step 3.

The record `credibility.json` holds everything, and the tables `contexts.tsv`, `evidence.tsv`, `domain.tsv` and `quantities.tsv` are one row per COU, per COU and evidence, per COU, field and value and per COU and quantity, `ratios.tsv` one row per COU and ratio pair (see [Ratios](#ratios)), `temporal.tsv` one row per COU and study collected after its fit (`context`, `study`, `fit`, `fit_date`, `collected`, `released`, `pkdb_id`, `n_points`, `n_evaluable`, see [Temporal validation](#temporal-validation)), `studies.tsv` one row per study with points (`study`, `folder`, `fits` joined by `;`, `n_points`, `recorded`, `collected`, `dating`, `updated`, `released`, `pkdb_id`, `source_digest`, `pkdb_data_commit`, `pkdb_data_dirty`, `n_tables`, `changed` joined by `;`, see [Study data](#study-data)), `inputs.tsv` one row per mapping and input and `input_ranges.tsv` one row per COU and input (see [Equivalency of the inputs](#equivalency-of-the-inputs)), `variability.tsv`, `variability_quantities.tsv` and `vpc.tsv` (see [Variability and the predictive check](#variability-and-the-predictive-check)) and `verification.tsv` one row per COU and check (the applicable checks of the battery included, the same for every COU), `battery.tsv` one row per check of the battery (`check`, `factor`, `status`, `cases`, `largest`, `at`, `statement`, `problems`) and `fates.tsv` one row per build, dose and part of its fate (`build`, `dose`, `substance`, `amount`, `part`, `value` in mmole and its `share` of the dose). `contexts.tsv` has the decisive and the evaluable points, the minimum (`minimum_points`), the uncovered quantities (`uncovered_quantities`, the ids comma separated, empty without) and whether the COU is verified (`verified`, empty without verification); `quantities.tsv` has the columns `context`, `quantity`, `description`, `ratio`, `coverage` (empty for a ratio without a pairing) and `n_points`; `verification.tsv` the columns `context`, `check`, `factor`, `n`, `max_difference`, `at`, `acceptance_changed`, `passed` and `problems` (joined by `; `), no rows without verification. The record has the evidence of the extensions by their name in the field `extensions` of a COU and of the assessment. A record written by pkpdsim 0.17 to 0.19 is still read (`--from-json`): one of 0.17 has no minimum (read as 0), no quantity coverage and no verification; one of 0.18 has no verification and no ratio pairs; one of 0.19 has the evidence of the extensions as fields of its own, which are read as their sections. A release of a model repository carries the assessment, see [Release of a model repository](release.md).
