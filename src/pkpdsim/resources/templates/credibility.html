Overview

The contexts of use of the model, their model risk from the influence of the model on the decision and the consequence of a wrong decision, and their verdict on the decisive evidence (decided on): the calibration data, the data the model was fitted to, for a description (C1), else the validation data. The evaluable points are the decisive points which the fold criteria can evaluate; a verdict needs at least the minimum of them, by default 5, 10 and 20 for a low, medium and high risk.
