# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.8.0

pkpdsim 0.8.0 runs on sbmlsim 0.10, whose fits record their provenance and write a report of their methods, and gives that report the context of the model.

## New features

### The report of a fit names its model and the fixed fits
`run_fit` passes the context of the model to the report of the fit (`sbmlsim.fit.methods.ReportContext`): the title of the model and the fit (`"<model title>: fit <subset>"`), the description of the fit definition, the descriptions of the fit parameters and a note naming the adopted fits of other subsets which the fit kept fixed. The provenance of the fit records the arguments of the command, the ids of those fixed fits (`fixed_fits`) and their values, without the parameters the fit optimizes itself.

### The report of an adopted fit
- `run_report <package> --adopted` (`pkpdsim.reports.adopted_reports`) writes one report per adopted fit of `MODEL.fits` from its record into `results/report/<fit id>/`; its `report.typ` is the one of `run_report <package> --subset <subset> --name <fit id>`.
- A report rebuilt from a record (`run_report <package> --subset X parameters.json`, `--adopted`) has the same context; the note on the fixed fits comes from the provenance of the record, a record without provenance gets no note.
- `adopt_fit` keeps the methods report with the record: `report.pdf`, `report.typ`, `references.bib`, `references.csl` and the SVG figures `report.typ` references, so the Typst source compiles in the record (about 1.6 MB for the PK fit of HCTZ; no LaTeX sources or PNG copies).
- `fit_subset(fit_id)` reads the subset of a fit id (a subset may contain `_`).
- The Fisher information of a record is read with `FisherInformation.from_dict` of sbmlsim, which keeps `step` and `rank_tolerance`.

## Dependencies
- sbmlsim 0.10 (`sbmlsim>=0.10.0,<0.11`), which brings `typst` and `latex2mathml` for the reports of the fits.

## Fixes
- `pkpdsim-verify` compares the figure settings of sbmlsim 0.8 with those of sbmlsim from 0.9.6, which add `legend_scope` (the comparison failed with sbmlsim 0.9.6 and later).
