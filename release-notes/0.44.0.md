# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.44.0

The fit problems of a model are written as PEtab v2, a training and a validation problem per fit subset with a README of what PEtab does not express (`run_pipeline`'s step or `run_petab <package>`), the forkserver of a pool preloads the builds of the equilibration, and pkpdsim moves onto sbmlsim 0.52.0, sbmlsim-core 0.38.0 and pkpdutils 2.0.1 without matplotlib.

## Features

- **`run_petab <package>`** (#275) writes the fit problems of a model as PEtab v2 (sbmlsim#708): for every fit subset a training problem with the data the fit optimizes and a validation problem with the validation data at the adopted values, each with its `README.md` (the fit and the values, the data written and left out by name, every gap of PEtab with its kind and what it means for another tool, the measurements at the time of a change, how to read it back). A mapping PEtab cannot express is skipped and documented instead of failing the export; `--no-split` writes the single problem with every kind.
- **The forkserver preloads the builds of the equilibration** (#272). `PBPKSimulationExperiment.preload_in_forkserver` loads the build of every sex an experiment class simulates in the forkserver of a pool, so a fresh worker inherits it instead of parsing and compiling the flat model again (1.1 to 2.5 s per worker before).

## Changes

- **sbmlsim 0.52.0, sbmlsim-core 0.38.0 and pkpdutils 2.0.1** (#274, #276): the global sensitivity analysis in sbmlsim-core, PEtab v2 in rust, error caps as the capsize of matplotlib; the browser examples (cardiovascular, lobes) are exported again from them.
- **No matplotlib in pkpdsim** (#273): matplotlib, seaborn and SALib leave the lock; the tests of the outlines test a point in polygon of their own.

The floors are `sbmlsim>=0.52.0`, `sbmlsim-core>=0.38.0` and `pkpdutils>=2.0.1`.
