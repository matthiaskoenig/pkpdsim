# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.6.0

pkpdsim 0.6.0 adds `pkpdsim.uncertainty`, the uncertainty of the fitted parameters of a model.

It also adds bands of the predictions over draws of the fitted parameters. Without the new options, models, runs and figures are unchanged.

## New features

### Adopting a fit
`fit_<model> --adopt <fit id> [--force]` copies the record of a finished fit into `PKPDModel.fits_dir`:
- the parameters;
- the problem with its bounds;
- the profile likelihood;
- the Fisher information;
- the metrics and the report.

`PKPDModel.fits_dir` is `fits` next to the output of the factory, field `fits_path`. `PKPDModel.fits` lists the adopted fits.

### The fitted values in the factory
`PKPDModel.fit_records().parameter(pid, unit=...)` gives the value of a fitted parameter for a factory `Parameter`. It comes with a distrib uncertainty of the SBML parameter:
- the confidence interval of the profile likelihood, or of the Fisher information without profiles;
- the standard error of the Fisher information;
- the distribution of the draws;
- the provenance, in the notes.

The distribution is:
- a lognormal or normal around the value, whose spread follows the closed sides of the interval, truncated to the bounds of the fit;
- uniform for a profile which is open on both sides.

`check_fit_parameters` is part of `testing.check_model`.

### The run over the draws
`run_<model> --uncertainty [--draws 100] [--seed 1234] [--quantiles 0.05 0.95]` runs the experiments over draws of the fitted parameters into `simulation_uncertainty`:
- every simulation becomes a scan over the draws;
- every curve over the time becomes the band of the quantiles with the median;
- a curve over another variable draws the simulation with the fitted values.

`PKPDModel.run_experiments(..., uncertainty=UncertaintySettings())` does the same from a script. An extra action gets `options.uncertainty`.

### Figures an experiment draws itself
`PKPDSimulationExperiment.uncertainty` gives the draws to the figures an experiment draws itself. `pkpdsim.scan.point_band` and `scan_dimensions` read the bands of a scan.

The draws are independent per parameter: distrib states one distribution per parameter, and the correlations of the Fisher information stay in the record. See the page [Uncertainty of the fitted parameters](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/pkpdsim/uncertainty/).
