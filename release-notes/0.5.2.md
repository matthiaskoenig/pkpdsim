# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.5.2

pkpdsim 0.5.2 adds the runner of the sensitivity analyses of a script to `pkpdsim.sensitivity` and makes the names of the units of the data of an experiment public. It changes no behavior of a model of pkdb_models: the hctz script on the runner writes the tables and figures of sbmlsim 0.8.5 under their names, and every file which its own copy of the runner writes as well is the same, byte for byte.

## Features

- `pkpdsim.sensitivity.SensitivityRunner` runs the sensitivity analyses of the script of a model: a script gives its model object, a function which gives its parameters, its outputs (`SensitivityOutput`, the id of the tables and files and the id of the observable), its observables and its analyses by name (`LocalAnalysis`, `SamplingAnalysis`, `FastAnalysis`, `SobolAnalysis`, each with its groups, `SensitivityGroup`, and its `delta` or number of samples), and runs them with `SENSITIVITY.main()` from its command line or with `run(names, path=..., subfolder=..., simulator=...)`, with the simulator of the model and `n_workers=None`; an analysis varies the `free_parameters` of its groups, `PKPDSIM_SENSITIVITY_N` replaces its number of samples. Why: the port of the models onto sbmlsim 0.9 copied the same runner (about 180 lines) into the script of every model, hctz, apixaban, rivaroxaban and losartan so far, where a change of a table or a figure would have to be made in every copy. The runner serves eleven of the twelve sensitivity scripts of pkdb_models: simvastatin keeps its own design (the central differences of its parameters), and the two further files of icg, `sensitivity_debug.py` and `sensitivity_parameters.py`, stay outside.
- A run writes the table of the parameters into `results/sensitivity` of the model and one result per quantity, the tables and figures of sbmlsim 0.8.5 with their names and columns, into the folder of an analysis: local `local_d0.01_nvar3_<k>_<group>.tsv` and its heatmap, the mean of the normalized sensitivities over the designs of `delta / 3`, `2 delta / 3` and `delta` as the steps of sbmlsim 0.8.5; sampling `sampling_N<n>.tsv` with the uncertainty `U` and its classification, `sampling_N<n>_compact.tsv`, `sampling_N<n>.tex` and the box and violin figures of the samples and the outputs of every group (`SensitivityGroup.color`); fast and sobol the heatmaps of `S1` and `ST` and the indices of every output, `<prefix>_sensitivity_<k>_<group>_...png`. The heatmaps label the parameters `uid: name` and the outputs by name. What sbmlsim 0.8.5 wrote no file of is written per group, into `<analysis>/<group>`: the samples of the sampling (`samples.tsv`) and the tables of `S1` and `ST`. Why: the sensitivity analyses of the models keep their parameters and outputs across the port; the numbers differ by construction, the tables and figures a manuscript uses must not. Not reproduced: the `.pkl` caches of sbmlsim 0.8.5 and its separate samples per group (the sampling shares one design for every group). The run of hctz with the sizes of a smoke run (`local` complete, `sampling` and `sobol` with 16 samples, `fast` with 65) writes the 33 files hctz's script wrote with sbmlsim 0.8.5 but its caches, and the 50 files its copy of the runner writes as well (the samples, the tables of the indices, the table of the parameters) are those of the copy, byte for byte.
- A model with several simulations (dulaglutide) has a runner per simulation, reads its simulations with the analyses from the command line (`parse_command_line(argv, choices=...)`) and runs them with `run_subfolders`: every simulation into a subfolder, the one table of the parameters into `results/sensitivity`, written and printed once. Why: dulaglutide wrote one table and the analyses of a simulation into `sensitivity/<simulation>`.
- A configuration of the runner which cannot run is a `ValueError` when it is created, and what depends on the run is checked when it starts, before anything is written: a number of samples which the design refuses (`FastAnalysis` more than 64, `SobolAnalysis` a power of two), also the one of `PKPDSIM_SENSITIVITY_N`, a `delta` outside of (0, 1), an analysis which varies no parameter or one without valid bounds, an unknown or repeated name. Why: sbmlsim refuses these only when the analysis builds its design, which in a run of several analyses can be hours after the start.
- `PKPDSimulationExperiment.data_unit_names()` gives the names of the units of `data_units` (read-only, defining the units on its first call), for a table which a model reads itself, e.g. the data of the figure of a scan: `rename_units(df, self.data_unit_names())`. The private `_data_unit_names()` is removed, without an alias. Why: the scan of the parameters of apixaban called the private method.

## Fixes

- A heatmap of which no parameter reaches its cutoff shows every parameter, and an undefined sensitivity or uncertainty (an output which is zero at the reference) or a negative uncertainty (an output with a negative median, e.g. a change of the bodyweight) is classified by its magnitude or left without a class. Why: `plot_heatmap` and the classifications of sbmlsim refuse them, which ended a run in the middle (the classifications of sbmlsim 0.8.5, the same functions, failed on them as well).
