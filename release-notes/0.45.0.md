# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.45.0

A refresh of the floors onto the newest releases of sbmlsim, sbmlsim-core and sbmlutils: pkpdsim moves onto sbmlsim 0.54.0, sbmlsim-core 0.42.0 and sbmlutils 0.19.0, and the browser examples are exported again from them. Nothing of pkpdsim's own behaviour changes and no number of a model moves.

## Changes

- **sbmlsim 0.53.0, sbmlsim-core 0.39.0 and the fit of the engine** (#278): the browser examples (cardiovascular, lobes) exported from sbmlsim-core 0.39.0, and a fit of the engine reports no traces.
- **sbmlsim 0.54.0, sbmlsim-core 0.42.0 and sbmlutils 0.19.0** (#279): sbmlsim reads and evaluates every problem of the PEtab benchmark collection in sbmlsim-core 0.40.0; sbmlsim-core flattens `comp`, fits a PEtab problem in a page (0.40.0), compiles the batched kernel (0.41.0) and holds the statistics of the analyses of sbmlsim (`sbmlsim_core.stats`, 0.42.0); sbmlutils writes the ODEs with the generator of sbmlsim-core and no longer depends on sbmlode. The browser examples (cardiovascular, lobes) are exported again from sbmlsim-core 0.42.0.
- **No sbmlode in the records of a pipeline**: the archive writer of sbmlsim writes the ODEs of a model with `sbmlsim_core.OdeSystem`, so `sbmlode` is no longer installed with pkpdsim; `pkpdsim.pipeline` no longer records its version (it was always empty) and the docstrings of the two archive writers name the generator they use.

The floors are `sbmlsim>=0.54.0`, `sbmlsim-core>=0.42.0` and `sbmlutils>=0.19.0`.
