# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.23.1

The creator of the models carries the complete affiliation and the email of Matthias König at the University of Lübeck.

## Differences users see

- `pkpdsim.templates.creators` names the University of Lübeck, the University Hospital Schleswig-Holstein (Campus Lübeck, First Department of Medicine) and the Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin (Institute of Theoretical Biology) as the organization of Matthias König, instead of the Institute for Theoretical Biology of the Humboldt-University Berlin alone. The email of the creator is `matthias.koenig@uni-luebeck.de` instead of `koenigmx@hu-berlin.de`. The model history of every model which takes the creators of pkpdsim changes accordingly.

## Dependencies

- No changes; sbmlsim 0.18 (`>=0.18.0,<0.19`).
