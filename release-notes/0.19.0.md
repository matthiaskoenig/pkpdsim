# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.19.0

pkpdsim 0.19.0 completes the credibility assessment of a model with steps 2 to 4 of the verification, validation and uncertainty quantification ([pkdb_models#476](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkdb_models/issues/476)):
- step 2, the verification of the decisive predictions and the battery of the checks of the builds;
- step 3, the validation by ratios, cross-validation and temporal validation, the scorecard across models and the locked splits;
- step 4, the parameter uncertainty and the sensitivity of the decisive predictions, the equivalency of the inputs and the visual predictive check with the variability of the subjects.

Results which change: an assessment verifies its decisive predictions by default, and a context of use whose verification fails is `not_verified` (`run_credibility --no-verification` skips it). The other verdicts of a declaration of 0.18.0 are the ones of 0.18.0. Records of 0.17.0 and 0.18.0 still read with `run_credibility --from-json`.

## Features

- **Verification of the decisive predictions** ([#114](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/114)).
  - Every adopted fit is evaluated again with the tolerances divided by 100 (discretization error) and with the BDF of CVODE of at most order 2 (numerical solver error); the builds are checked for consistency and units (use error).
  - A check passes when the decisive predictions differ by at most 1 % and the acceptance does not change. A failed check gives the verdict `not_verified`.
  - `verification.tsv`, the verification section of the report and the release page.
- **Battery of the checks of the builds** ([#115](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/115)).
  - Without data: mass balance with the fate of every dose, non-negativity, zero dose, steady state, superposition, AMICI as a second simulator (the optional extra `amici`), plausibility of the values and the rebuild of the factory.
  - A known failure is declared with its reason (`CheckSettings.battery`) and stays failed.
  - `battery.tsv`, `fates.tsv`.
- **Ratio quantities with the limits of Guest et al.** ([#116](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/116)). `QuantityOfInterest(ratio=Ratio(...))` pairs the arms of a study (e.g. a renal impairment over its control) and accepts the predicted ratio within the limit of Guest et al. 2011 (`GuestAcceptance`); `ratios.tsv`.
- **Scorecard and qualification across models** ([#117](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/117)). `pkpdsim-verify scorecard RECORDS --baseline` gives one row per model and context of use and exits with 1 for a regression against a baseline; `--qualification` pools the ratio pairs by a field of the metadata.
- **Locked splits** ([#118](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/118)). `run_credibility --lock` writes `credibility.lock.json`, the mappings of every evidence fixed for review; an assessment whose evidence differs from the lock is an error.
- **Leave-one-study-out cross-validation** ([#119](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/119)). `run_fit <package> --cross-validation FIT_ID` refits an adopted fit without each study and compares the held-out predictions; the factor model form is assessed; `cross_validation.tsv`.
- **Temporal validation** ([#120](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/120)). The decisive points of the studies a model collected after the day of its fit are evaluated on their own; `temporal.tsv`.
- **Provenance of the study data** ([#121](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/121)).
  - `collect_tsv_files` writes the record `.<study>_study.json` beside the tables of a study (`StudyRecord`): the day of the first collection, the latest collection, the release and the source digest of PK-DB, the commit of pkdb_data and the sha256 of every table.
  - `scripts/backfill_study_records.py` dated the studies collected before from the git history of pkdb_models.
  - An assessment lists the data of every study it was computed on and the tables changed since their collection; `studies.tsv`.
- **Parameter uncertainty of the decisive predictions** ([#122](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/122)). `run_credibility --uncertainty [--draws 128]` evaluates every adopted fit at correlated draws of its parameters (the Fisher correlation as a Gaussian copula) and decides with anytime-valid statements whether every rule is met with a probability of at least 0.9; the factor model inputs is assessed; `uncertainty.tsv`, `uncertainty_quantities.tsv`.
- **Sensitivity of the quantities of interest** ([#123](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/123)). The first-order index of every fitted parameter for every quantity and rule, estimated from the same draws without further simulations; `uncertainty_sensitivity.tsv`.
- **Equivalency of the inputs** ([#124](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/124)). The simulated body weight, height and doses of every mapping against the groups, individuals and interventions its study reports, without a simulation (`Credibility.covariates`); the factor equivalency of inputs is assessed; `inputs.tsv`, `input_ranges.tsv`.
- **Visual predictive check with the variability of the subjects** ([#125](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/125)). A model declares the variability of its subjects (`Credibility.variability`, with `between` the variability between the studies), and `run_credibility --variability [--subjects 64] [--studies 16]` compares a mean of a group of N subjects with the means of virtual groups of N subjects; the coverage of the 90 % bands with its Wilson interval and its agreement per context of use; `variability.tsv`, `variability_quantities.tsv`, `vpc.tsv`.

The verdict depends only on the acceptance at the adopted parameters, the coverage, the minimum of points and the verification. The validation, uncertainty and variability results are recorded, reported and assess their factors of V&V 40, and the scorecard compares them across models.

## Dependencies

- **sbmlsim 0.16** (`>=0.16.0,<0.17`), for the evaluation of a fit problem with the factors of a subject and its predictive check (`sbmlsim.fit.variability`).
- **The optional extra `amici`** (AMICI 1.2.0 or newer, with cmake and ninja), the second simulator of the battery.
