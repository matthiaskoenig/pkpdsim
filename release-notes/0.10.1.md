# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.10.1

pkpdsim 0.10.1 supports the scans of simulations which output the steps of the integrator instead of a grid of times ([#75](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/75)), which the models of pkdb_models use once they drop the hard-coded `steps` of their simulations (matthiaskoenig/pkdb_models#381). A scan of such simulations is ragged: every point keeps its own time points over `(*dims, _point)`, padded with `NaN`.

## Features

- **The start and the end of every point of a scan.** `pkpdsim.scan.start_values(res, key)` and `end_values(res, key)` are the first and the last value of a timecourse at every point, over the dimensions of the scan with their coordinates, on a grid and ragged alike. The end of a ragged point is its last finite time, a failed point is `NaN`. They replace the idiom `res[key].isel(time=0)` and `isel(time=-1)` of the models.
- **Bands of draws with their own time points.** `point_band` interpolates draws with different time points onto the time points of a figure (`plot_times` of sbmlsim 0.12, a few hundred, which follow the density of their steps, with the times of the changes) instead of refusing them; draws on one grid are stacked as before.
- **The non-compartmental analysis of a scan at once.** `pkpdsim.pk.scan_nca(res, key, *, dose, route, substance, lloq_fraction=None, options=None)` analyses a timecourse at every point of a scan as one batch with `pkpdutils.nca`: the dose of every point, the limit of quantification relative to the maximum of every point, also for a substance without a dose. The parameters are those of `nca_single` per point to 1e-12; 200 points of the HCTZ renal scan take 0.06 s instead of 2.5 s. `nca_frame(result)` gives a row per point, with the position along every dimension (the index along a dimension with labels), every parameter with `<name>_unit` and `nca_flags`, the rows the models built point by point.

## Dependencies

- pkpdutils (`pkpdutils>=1.3.0`) is a dependency of pkpdsim, for `pkpdsim.pk`; sbmlsim installed it before.
