# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.42.0

`run_pipeline` runs the independent steps of the analysis of a model side by side, the links of a staged run name the folder of its result, the scans of a run are the choice of sbmlsim, and pkpdsim moves onto sbmlsim 0.47: the profiles of a fit, the runs over the draws, the credibility and the web app of a model are faster and the outputs of a run smaller with the same numbers, a fit simulates the adopted fits it keeps fixed, and the reductions and the PK parameters of an experiment are a fit of the engine.

## The pipeline

A step of a `Pipeline` may declare the cores it is given (`cores`), its peak memory (`memory_gb`) and the steps whose output it reads (`after`). `run_pipeline` starts a step as soon as its steps `after` ended and its cores and its memory fit into what the running steps leave of the budget, `--cores` and `--memory`, so steps which leave most of the machine idle run beside each other; `--jobs` caps the steps which run at the same time and `--jobs 1` times every step alone (#266).

- A step without `cores` is given the whole budget and runs alone, so a pipeline which declares no `cores` runs its steps one after the other as before.
- `cores` of a step is `{cores}` of its arguments and the CPUs its processes see (`PYTHON_CPU_COUNT`), so its pools size themselves by it.
- The CPU time and the peak memory of a step are the ones of its own processes, also beside other steps (`os.wait4` and `/proc`; Windows measures neither); `timings.tsv` gains the start of a step in the run and the cores it was given, `pipeline.json` the budget, the needs of the steps and the sum of their times.
- After a failed step no other step starts; with `--keep-going` only the steps after it are skipped. Ctrl-C, `SIGTERM` and `SIGHUP` stop the process groups of the running steps.

## Changes

- **The links of a staged run** (#267, sbmlsim#669). The commands which write into a staging folder and move it into its place print the links of the folder the result ends up in (`display.relocated` of sbmlsim 0.45.0), `run_fit` the folder of the fit id it moves up; the `results` links which repeated the report after the move are gone.
- **The scans of a run are the choice of sbmlsim** (#268). `PKPDModel.run_experiments` and `run_simulation_experiments` default to `scan_workers=None`, so a worker of the engine core simulates its scans on its share of the CPUs instead of one thread.
- **sbmlsim 0.47.0 and sbmlsim-core 0.33.0**: the chart builder of the renderer of sbmlsim-core 0.33.0 (sbmlsim 0.47.0). sbmlsim 0.46.0: a fit simulates the values of the adopted fits it keeps fixed, whatever the SBML holds (sbmlsim#663); the reductions and the PK parameters of an experiment are a fit of the engine on sbmlsim-core 0.32.0 (sbmlsim#625); the runs over draws read one array per figure and draw the bands at once. sbmlsim 0.45.0 (#267): the points of a profile of the engine stop at the tolerances of a point, the figures carry only the points which can be seen, the workers of a pool simulate on their share of the CPUs, a credibility assessment initializes every fit problem once and the export of the panels reads its models once. Before, sbmlsim 0.43.0 with sbmlsim-core 0.30.0 (#264) and sbmlsim 0.44.0 with sbmlsim-core 0.31.1 (#265).

The floors are `sbmlsim>=0.47.0` and `sbmlsim-core>=0.33.0`; the browser examples (cardiovascular, lobes) are exported again from them.
