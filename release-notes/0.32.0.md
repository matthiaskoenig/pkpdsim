# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.32.0

A model of the PBPK library states, in its own SBML, what values each of its quantities may take: its domain, and the body figure of its app is built by pkpdsim itself rather than by every package which shows one. A tool which scans, samples, fits or checks a model asks the model instead of carrying its own copy of that knowledge, which is what [#207](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/issues/207) was: a sensitivity analysis varied a fraction absorbed of 0.94 up to 1.081 and asked for more than the whole dose absorbed. Beside it the liver lobe explorer of the documentation became a tool rather than a figure: a virtual model is built in the first section, the individual and the operation it is planned for, and every change of it is simulated at once and played from its first day in the second.

## Features

- **The body figure of a model app is built by pkpdsim** ([#222](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/issues/222)). `pkpdsim.webapp.body_figure(svg, sbml, *, simulation, title, credit, substances, knobs, readouts)` turns the drawing of a model and its readouts sidecar into the panels of an app: it restricts the ids of the sidecar to the entities of the flat build (read with libsbml, no compile), puts the knobs of the app on every panel it builds, and labels the curves as the panels of an app label theirs, the compartment alone where a panel names one substance and the short name of the substance beside it where it draws several. It returns the panels by their key, the figure and the keys whose readouts no build has, which stay decorations of the drawing. The three steps were a copy in every model package which showed its figure, which is what a model package may not share (a model folder has to be exportable on its own), and the restriction belongs here in any case: pkpdsim writes both the figure and the model, so it can read the ids back instead of asking a package to keep a hand-maintained list of what its model lacks. The page is `docs/webapp.md`.
- **The domain of a quantity** ([#207](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/issues/207)). `pkpdsim.sbml.domains` is the contract: `Domain` with the named domains `FRACTION`, `NONNEGATIVE`, `REAL`, `BOOLEAN` and `integers(lower, upper)`, written next to the quantity as a `distrib` uncertainty named `domain` and read back with `domains(model_path)`. A domain is what the model accepts, which is neither the uncertainty of a value nor the posterior of a fit, and each of the three is its own element on the element it describes. Silence means the model says nothing about that quantity and never a default. A kind of the annotation table states its domain once and every element of the kind gets it, so no list of ids decides what a quantity is; a `ModelParameter` may state its own, which wins over its kind. The page is `docs/pbpk/domains.md` with the generated table of the domains by kind.
- **The bounds of a sensitivity analysis respect the domain**. `library_parameters(..., clip=True, unclipped=())` intersects the bounds of every parameter with its domain, logs each bound it moves, counts the parameters which state none in one warning, and raises for a value its own domain does not admit. A fraction absorbed of 0.94 at the default span of 0.15 now stops at 1.0 instead of 1.081, and the severities are varied over their domain rather than around their value. `clip=False`, or an id in `unclipped`, keeps the span.
- **The explorer plans a procedure and reads its run two ways** ([#223](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/223)). The procedure, the lobes it acts on as clickable chips, the named procedures of the parameter database, and a line which says what the plan costs, the share of the liver mass it acts on and the remnant it leaves. The area of the whole liver is the mass which is there, measured against the intact liver of the same individual, and within it every lobe is a block of its own colour whose area is the share of the mass it holds at the displayed time; what was done to a lobe is a shading of that colour. Three vessels belong to a lobe, each as wide as the square root of its flow, and a ligated branch is cut across. Beside the liver stands the animal of the run, the anatomical figure of the library with its liver picked out, and the sex of that individual is a switch: the parameter database has the female human and the male alone of the rat and of the mouse, and the female human is a second individual with its own model, its own precomputed runs and its own compiled liver. A switch above the dashboard reads one run as volumes or as pressures and flows, and runs nothing, because both are columns of one result.
- **The explorer runs as it is changed and plays what it ran** ([#224](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/224)). There is no run button any more. A change of the plan, of the physiology or of the settings of the simulation runs the model at once and the course which comes back is played from its first day, which is what an intervention looks like; the player pauses and resumes it and the slider scrubs it by hand. The page is two sections: the virtual model is built in the first, where the species is picked by its animal with the sex as a choice under it, the operation is one row of the intact liver and the three states a lobe is put in, each with the shade its lobes are drawn in, and the named plans sit under the operation they are, labelled with what is left of their name once that operation is said.
- **The settings of a run** ([#224](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/224)). The days simulated, the time points of the output and the dose of the marker whose exposure the lobes are read with are settings of the second section and not parameters of the model: they are the `end`, the `output` and the `dose` of the request of the contract, so a reader who asks for ten days gets ten days of the same liver, run here. Their defaults are the ones `scripts/lobes_examples.py` precomputed the reference courses with, so a named plan at the defaults is served from the committed data and appears at once.
- **A page of its own takes the window** ([#224](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/224)). Opened on its own the explorer is a column which fills the window: the dashboard is the part which grows, the four plots are a square of two by two and the liver is as tall as the room allows. As a figure of the documentation it is in a frame which follows the height it posts, where a viewport height is the height of that frame, so there it stays as tall as its content.

## Changes

- **A `ModelParameter` states its domain.** `PBPKDefinition.validate` and `check_custom_parameters` report a parameter which states none, naming the field to set. A model package adds `domain=` to each of its parameters and rebuilds, which in pkdb_models is 22 call sites across six packages (creatinine, rapamycin, torasemide, lenvatinib, glimepiride, hctz); the named domains are re-exported from `pkpdsim.pbpk` next to `ModelParameter` itself.
- **`pkpdsim.sensitivity.SEVERITIES` is a `frozenset[str]`** of the ids of the severities, where it was a mapping of each id to the top of its range. The tops come from the domain the model states.
- **A model built before this release is not refused.** Such a model states no domain for any quantity, so a sensitivity analysis of it logs one warning and uses the plain relative span, including for the severities; rebuilding the model gives the clip.
- **The range of a fitted fraction is a fraction**. `fraction_range` intersects the support of the draws of a fit with the unit interval, and `check_products` now accepts a product fraction whose fit is an unbounded distribution, whose support says nothing about intent, while it refuses a finite support bound outside the unit interval, which states that a fraction may be 1.5 and contradicts the domain.
- Four pre-existing mis-annotations were found by the contract and fixed by splitting the kind rather than widening the domain, so no written annotation moved: a body weight change annotated as a body weight, a plasma to tissue protein ratio as a tissue fraction, a zonal abundance as a share, and a signed body composition change. The row `depot_loss_amount_rectal` is gone, because a lost rectal dose leaves through its reaction as feces and needs no amount sink, so nothing was ever annotated with it.
- The twelve reference models and the precomputed assets of the documentation were rebuilt for the annotations. Nothing a solver reads changed: across the regenerated datasets the largest relative difference over 748,279 numbers is exactly 0, the only key which moved anywhere is `sbml_sha256`, and the browser models of the lobes are byte identical.

## Fixes

- **The lumen key of a model figure reports the drug of every segment** ([#222](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/issues/222)). The summed dissolved amount `Alumen_<s>` is a readout of the library which a model requests with `PBPKDefinition.readouts`, so a model which does not request it has no such id and the lumen key of its figure proposed the one id it had: a consumer which checks the sidecar against its build dropped it and clicking the intestine of an oral model reported nothing at all. The key now names the species of every lumen compartment of the segmented intestine (`GU__Clumen_du_<s>`, whose plain id is its amount in `mmole`, labelled in the names of the segments, the transit of the drug through the gut) or the one species of a single lumen, and keeps the summed amount where the model declares it. `ModelContent` carries the `lumen_segments` of the intestine and the `readouts` the model requests, and the new tests check the ids of the lumen against a compiled model rather than against the id scheme, which is what let the gap through. The urine and the feces rates stay proposals of the sidecar, dropped where a model does not declare them, and their amounts are unaffected; `docs/pbpk/figures.md` says which ids a model has to ask for.
- **A committed case is recognised whatever order its changes were written in** ([#223](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/223)). A plan whose parameter changes the generator wrote in another order than the lobe table ran the solver although its reference run was on the page, and the legend drew the intact state as a hatch.
- **A block of the liver too narrow for a value carries none** ([#224](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/224)). A value of nine characters is about 50 px wide and was drawn over the edge of a caudate lobe of 6 % of the mass. The colour of the block and the legend still name it, and the token stays.
- **The drawing of a body is compared the way its numbers are** ([#223](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/223)), its structure exactly and its coordinates to a tolerance: the body of the human is geometry written with one decimal whose halves round differently on another platform.
