# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.12.1

pkpdsim 0.12.1 completes the collector of the studies of format 2 with what the ports of the models to pkdb_data needed: geometric means with their calculation type and their unit, output labels which are unique across the sources of a study, and the documentation of the rows and columns of the tables. The simulations are unchanged. The data a model reads can change, and with them the fits and the figures: a curve of geometric means is now converted into the units of the model, an output label which several sources of a study share gets its source appended, and a row of geometric means has the calculation type `geometric mean` instead of `sample mean`.

## Changes

- **Geometric means** ([#87](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/87)).
  - **Before.** PK-DB gives every measurement of a group without a calculation the calculation type `sample mean`, also a row with a `gmean` and no `mean`. sbmlsim's `DataSet.from_df` reads the `unit` of a row only for `mean`, `value` and `median`, so a curve of the geometric means (`yid="gmean"`) had no unit and was not converted.
  - **Now.** A row with a `gmean` and no `mean` has the calculation type `geometric mean`, with its spread in `gsd` (from a geometric error bar or the `gcv`) and `gcv` (a fraction, format 2 writes it in percent). `load_dataframe` and `load_dataframes_by_substance` add the column `gmean_unit` (`add_gmean_unit`), so a curve of the geometric means has the unit of its rows and is converted.
  - **Models.** An experiment which selects rows by `calculation_type == "sample mean"` no longer gets the geometric means; a fit to a curve of geometric means now compares values in the units of the model.
- **Unique output labels** ([#87](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/87)).
  - **Before.** An output got the label generated from its measurement, substance, tissue, interventions and subject, which another source of the study could have as well: sorafenib/Fucile2015 had the same label for the trough of visit 8 in `outputs_Fig1.tsv` and the troughs of patient 1 in `outputs_Tab3.tsv`.
  - **Now.** A generated label which another source of the study has as well gets its source appended in every source which has it (`concentration_sorafenib_plasma_SOF400_1_Fig1`, `..._Tab3`), so the datasets of an experiment can be keyed by the label. A label which only one source has stays as it is; a source added to a study can change the label of another source.
  - **Models.** An experiment which selects such an output by its old label selects nothing and has to use the new label.
- **The rows and columns of the tables are documented** ([#87](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/87)).
  - The new section "The rows and columns" of `docs/data.md` describes what the experiments have to know about the tables collected from format 2; the behaviour is the one of 0.12.0:
    - a row below the limit of quantification has `NA` in `value`, `mean`, `median` and `gmean`, with the LOQ in `max`, so an experiment drops it with `df.dropna(subset=["mean"])`;
    - `intervention` holds the interventions in the natural order of the format 2 cell (`GEM,SIM`), a selection of format 1 in another order (`SIM,GEM`) selects nothing.
  - What format 2 does not store, the tables do not have either: no collection intervals (no `tstart` and `tend`; PK-DB does not store them yet, [pkdb#1032](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkdb/issues/1032)), so the experiments take them from the publication until then, and none of the columns which the curators of format 1 added to a single table (`mean_se`, `dose`, ...).
- **Vocabulary** ([#87](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/87)).
  - New members `Health.NAFLD` (non-alcoholic fatty liver disease, apixaban/Tirona2018) and `Coadministration.NAPROXEN` (apixaban/Frost2014a).
