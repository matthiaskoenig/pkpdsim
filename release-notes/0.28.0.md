# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.28.0

Every model has a default web app: a model which declares no panels shows the pages of its doses and dosing protocols, of the diseases it is studied in and of its scans, each of small panels of the readouts its experiments compare with data, and a reader switches between its builds. The factory writes it with the models (sbmlsim 0.28.0), so the web app of every model of pkdb_models is built from what the model and its experiments state.

## Features

- **The default web app of a model** ([#200](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/200)). `PKPDModel.default_app` (`pkpdsim.webapp`, the override of `Project.default_app` of sbmlsim) builds the app from the experiments of the model with data: the readouts (the observables of their fit mappings, at most 8), the page `Dose` (a knob per studied route and substance with the studied doses as its stops), the page `Dosing protocol` (the doses repeated once, twice or three times daily for up to six days, `PanelDosing`), a page per disease the model is studied in (the classes of `renal_map`, `cirrhosis_map` or `cardiac_map` of the base of its experiments on the parameter of the model) and a page per other scan of its scan experiments (`scan_map`), every page of small panels, and the variants of the model. The docs page `webapp.md` documents it.
