# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.39.0

The libraries of the day: `sbmlsim>=0.41.0` and `sbmlutils>=0.18.0`. A profile likelihood of a model is walked at the scale of its parameters, so the identifiability analysis of a fit needs fewer re-optimizations and the confidence intervals of a refit move in their fourth digit, and the report of a large fit draws its figures in a pool. Nothing of pkpdsim's own code changed since 0.38.0.

## Dependencies

- **`sbmlsim>=0.41.0`**, on the floor of the engine `sbmlsim-core>=0.26.0`, which walks the profiles of a request from the first steps sbmlsim computes.
- **`sbmlutils>=0.18.0`**, which concentrates the package on SBML: the interpolation, Cytoscape and cobra integrations are gone (the interpolation is `sbmlsim.interpolation` now), none of which pkpdsim uses, and the output is printed on a rich console instead of logged at INFO.

### A profile starts at the scale of its parameter (sbmlsim 0.41.0)

`run_fit <package> --identifiability` profiles every fitted parameter. A scan started at one step for every parameter whatever its scale and halved a step which raised the cost too much, discarding the re-optimization which measured it, up to four times per point. The first step is now the one the Fisher information of the optimum gives for the parameter, and a step which is walked again is corrected by the curvature of the profile in one go. On the PK subset of the HCTZ model of pkdb_models the analysis takes 71 re-optimizations instead of 83.

**The profiles have other points, so a bound moves a little.** The confidence interval is the interpolated crossing of the threshold between two points, and those two are no longer the same two. A model package whose factory writes the interval of an adopted fit into its SBML as a `distrib:uncertainty` gets a diff in that file at its next refit: a numerical one, not a scientific one. Compare the classification and the width of an interval, not its digits. A profile which is flat to its bound has no crossing and does not move; a shallow crossing moves the most.

### The figures of the fit mappings are drawn in a pool (sbmlsim 0.41.0)

The two figures of every fit mapping are almost the whole cost of the report of a fit. They are drawn in a pool of workers now, one per 16 figures, so a small report stays in its process: on the adopted PK fit of HCTZ, 160 mappings, from 57.8 s to 28.4 s on 12 physical cores.

### Smaller changes of sbmlsim 0.41.0

- A column of study data in one unit is answered from its unit column, which takes the unit handling of `DataSet.from_df` from 1.35 s to 0.10 s over the 1230 frames of HCTZ and shortens the setup every command of a model pays.
- A change whose formula reads the time, or which is linear in the target of a fitted parameter, is a form the engine evaluates, so it no longer folds the forms of its plan and sends a fit to python.

## Unchanged

- `pymetadata>=0.9.0`, `pkpdutils>=1.7.1`, `sbmlode` (through sbmlutils) and `libroadrunner>=2.12.0.dev714` are unchanged, each is still the release pkpdsim was tested against.
