# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.9.8

pkpdsim 0.9.8 runs on sbmlsim 0.11, the release of sbmlsim for libroadrunner 2.11, and compares the values at the end of a scan relative to their scale in `pkpdsim-verify compare`.

## Dependencies

- pkpdsim requires `sbmlsim>=0.11.0,<0.12` (was `>=0.10.0,<0.11`) and `sbmlutils>=0.17.0` (was `>=0.16.0`), and the lock of the development environment has sbmlsim 0.11.0 and sbmlutils 0.17.0. sbmlsim 0.11 requires libroadrunner 2.11 and compiles a model with LLJIT, three to five times faster than before ([release notes](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/releases/tag/0.11.0)). pkpdsim uses none of the API which sbmlsim 0.11 removes (`sbmlsim.model.roottol`, `sbmlsim.simulator.worker.quiet_sundials`, `SUNDIALS_LOGS`).
- With sbmlsim 0.11 the fast tests of the models of pkdb_models run in half the time; the experiments of hctz, glimepiride and torasemide give the results of sbmlsim 0.10 with libroadrunner 2.10 within a relative difference of 1e-10 (`pkpdsim-verify compare`, the tolerances `--atol 1e-16 --rtol 1e-12`).

## Fixes

- **`pkpdsim-verify compare` compares a numeric array without a time relative to its scale.** The values at the end of the points of a scan, e.g. a clearance per renal function, have a dimension of the scan and no time. They were compared exactly, so a difference of 1e-11 between two versions of the simulator counted as an infinite difference. They are now compared like a timecourse, max|new - old| / max|old| on the same dimensions; an array of other dimensions is a difference, which the exact comparison did not see.
