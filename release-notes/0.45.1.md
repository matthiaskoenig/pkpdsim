# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.45.1

A fix of the annotations of a library model: a term of pymetadata in the annotations of a substance is written as the url which resolves it, no longer as its bare identifier.

## Fixes

- **A term of pymetadata in the annotations of a substance** (#282): `annotate` turned the resources of the annotations of a substance into a plain `str`, so a term of `pymetadata.ontologies`, e.g. `(BQB.IS, PBPKO.MOLECULE)` of the hctz model of pkdb_models, lost its type and was written as its bare identifier, `<rdf:li rdf:resource="COB_0000013"/>`, with an error of pymetadata for every element ("Resource `COB_0000013` could not be split in collection and term"). The resources are passed as given, and pymetadata 0.9.1 writes a term as the url which resolves it, `https://bioregistry.io/COB:0000013`.
- `pkpdsim.pbpk.annotation.pbpko` takes the url of a PBPKO term from pymetadata (`OntologyTerm.url`) instead of building it; the resources it writes do not change (`https://bioregistry.io/PBPKO:00558`), and the constant `PBPKO_URL` is gone.

The floor is `pymetadata>=0.9.1`.
