# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.14.0

pkpdsim 0.14.0 completes the collector of the studies of format 2 with the follow-ups of the sync of the 47 models of pkdb_models with pkdb_data (pkdb_models#416 to #462). The simulations are unchanged. The labels, sources and columns which the models read stay; what the next collection of a model changes:

- **Every `TabGroups`** has the new columns `cv`, `gmean`, `gsd` and `gcv` (the geometric mean of a characteristic was lost before, captopril/MacFadyen1991).
- **Counts are integers**: `count`, `group_count` and `doses` are written as `53`, not `53.0`, in a column with a missing value (tirzepatide/Coskun2018 `TabGroups`).
- **Scatters** have the new columns `x_time`, `x_time_unit`, `x_intervention` and the same for `y`, and a point of a group counts the group (inulin/Prescott1991 Fig4, apixaban/Frost2014 Fig4) instead of 1.
- **Curves of geometric means** lose their curator 0 (the mean of 0 at the time of the dose, aliskiren, apixaban/Chang2016, apixaban/Upreti2013 and the edoxaban studies of apixaban), and a point below the LOQ of such a curve has the calculation type `geometric mean` instead of `sample mean`.
- **Warnings** (`CollectionWarning`) name what the curation should look at: curated labels which are no SId, the same output in two sources of a study, the dropped curator 0, scatters with partly repeated points of a group.

## Changes

- **Integer counts** ([#93](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/93)).
  - **Before.** A column with a missing count was a float column, written as `53.0`.
  - **Now.** `count`, `group_count` and `doses` of all tables are nullable integers (`Int64`, `INTEGER_COLUMNS`), written as `53` and `NA`.
- **Rows below the LOQ and the curator 0 of geometric means** ([#93](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/93)).
  - **Before.** A row below the LOQ (no central value) had the calculation type `sample mean` which PK-DB gives every measurement of a group, also in a curve of geometric means; a curve of geometric means kept the mean of 0 which the curation adds at the time of the dose.
  - **Now.** A row below the LOQ has the calculation type of the other rows of its curve (the rows of a source with the same label). The mean of 0 of a curve of geometric means cannot be a geometric mean: it is left out with a `CollectionWarning`.
  - **Models.** A curve of geometric means selected by its label has one row less; an experiment which selects the LOQ rows by `calculation_type == "sample mean"` no longer gets those of the geometric means.
- **Scatters** ([#93](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/93)).
  - **Before.** A point had the time of `y` (else of `x`), the interventions of both axes joined in `intervention` and the count 1.
  - **Now.** Each axis has its time and interventions (`x_time`, `x_time_unit`, `x_intervention`, `y_...`); `time` and `intervention` stay as before. A point of an individual counts 1, a point of a group the count of the group; several points of a scatter with the same group, interventions and times are anonymous subjects of the group and count 1 (empagliflozin/Sarashina2014 Fig2), a group with both kinds of points is a warning (inulin/Prescott1991, two points of the patients at 1.5 hr). `x_substance` was written before; a measurement without a substance (the eGFR of Sarashina2014) has `NA` there.
  - **Models.** An experiment which weights a scatter by `count` weights the means of groups by the group.
- **Geometric statistics of the groups** ([#93](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/93)): `TabGroups` has the columns `cv`, `gmean`, `gsd` and `gcv` of format 2 (`GROUP_COLUMNS`). Format 2 has `median`, `min` and `max` but no quartiles or confidence intervals, so the tables do not have them ([pkdb#1044](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkdb/issues/1044)).
- **Warnings for the curation** ([#93](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/93)). `CollectionWarning` (`warnings.warn`) reports, without changing the data: a curated label which is no SId (`corr_creatine_cl-pravastatin_cl`), the same output in two sources of a study (the same measurement, substance, tissue, subject, interventions, time and value, naming both sources; such a duplicate makes a generated label get its source). A generated label is always an SId, a leading digit gets a `_`.
- **`collect_tsv_files`** ([#93](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/93)): `studies=[...]` collects further study folders beside those of `source_dir`, e.g. `studies_dir() / "midazolam" / "Chung2006"` for a model of another substance; two studies of the same name are an error. `keep_files=True` keeps the tables of a study in the data folder which the collection does not write (removed by default), e.g. tables a model keeps beside the ones of PK-DB.
- **`load_dataframes_by_substance`** ([#93](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/93)) creates no empty frame for the rows without a substance (the bile flow of cholesterol/Loria1993); they are in the table of `load_dataframe`.
- **Helpers of the experiments** ([#93](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/93)): `pkpdsim.data.mean_of_individuals(df, by=None)` calculates the mean, SD and count of the individuals of a table at every time (the `mean_of_individuals` of the acetaminophen model), `pkpdsim.data.sampling_times(df, times, start=None, by="label")` moves digitized times to the sampling times of a protocol, in their order and with the least total shift (the helper of the atorvastatin and glimepiride models).
- **Vocabulary** ([#93](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/93)): `Tissue.ARTERIAL_PLASMA` (`arterial plasma` of the vocabulary of PK-DB).
- **Documentation** ([#93](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/93)): `docs/data.md` says that `cv` and `gcv` are fractions (not percent), that a curve without errors has an all-`NA` `sd` (`mean_sd` in sbmlsim) and is tested with `df["sd"].notna().any()`, not with the existence of the column, and how to pivot the long `TabIndividuals`.
