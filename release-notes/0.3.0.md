# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.3.0

## New features

- `pkpdsim.model`: `PKPDModel` describes a model once, in the `__init__.py` of its package: its paths, its factory, the registry of its experiments and its fit definitions (references resolved on use), the settings of its simulator and figures, and the actions it adds to its commands (matthiaskoenig/pkdb_models#95)
- `pkpdsim.run`: the simulation experiments of a model run with its settings, the figure settings are restored after a run
- `pkpdsim.cli`: the commands `run_<model>`, `fit_<model>` (with the identifiability analysis of the fitted parameters), `report_<model>` and `identifiability_<model>`
- `pkpdsim.fit`: the fit settings the models share, the metadata check and the observable of a fit mapping
- `pkpdsim.testing`: the tests of a model as functions
- `pkpdsim.verify`: the command `pkpdsim-verify`, the verification of a change of a model (from `scripts/verify_model.py` of pkdb_models), with the new subcommand `definitions`

## Dependencies

- `sbmlsim>=0.8.5`
