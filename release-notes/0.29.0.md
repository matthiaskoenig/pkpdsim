# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.29.0

The default app of a model shows every build with its own panels: the female build has the classes of a renal impairment at its own healthy GFR, as its experiments simulate a group of women. With sbmlsim 0.29.0, which brings the engine core of sbmlsim-core as a fourth simulation engine.

## Features

- **The renal classes of each build in the default app** ([#202](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/202)). `PKPDModel.default_variant_panels` (`pkpdsim.webapp.default_variant_panels`, the override of `Project.default_variant_panels` of sbmlsim 0.29.0) gives the build of each other sex the panels of its disease pages with the classes at its own healthy GFR (`ScaledRenalMap.of_sex`), and `default_app` takes the classes of the sex of the default build.

## Changes

- sbmlsim 0.29.0, the floor and the lock.
- `test_the_assessment_gives_the_verdicts_of_pkpdsim_0_19` expects the verdict of the cross simulator of the PK battery from whether AMICI is installed.
