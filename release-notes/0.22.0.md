# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.22.0

The PBPK library `pkpdsim.pbpk` gains the permeability-limited mucosa of the intestine, the turnover of a protein with its stabilisation by a substance, the intramuscular, subcutaneous and rectal routes, the fed state and the enteric coat of a tablet and the enzymes CES1, CYP2E1, CYP3A5, UGT1A3 and UGT2B15; the battery of the checks of the builds knows the dose states of all models of pkdb_models, not only the library's. pkpdsim 0.22.0 runs on sbmlsim 0.18, like 0.21 and 0.20.

## Features

- **Permeability-limited mucosa of a substance**. `SegmentedIntestine(mucosa_ps={"<s>": value})` and `SingleLumen(mucosa_ps=...)` make the mucosa of the substances they name permeability-limited: the enterocytes exchange with the mucosal blood through the permeability-surface product of their basolateral membrane on the unbound concentrations (`fu*(C_blood - Cmu/K_mu)`, the convention of `ps` of an organ), which leaves the hybrid flow of the Qgut model (Yang 2007, Gertz 2010), `Qgut = Qmu*carrier*fu*PS/(Qmu*carrier + fu*PS)`, in place of the mucosal blood flow of the two flows of a mucosa segment, while the rest of the mucosal blood passes the enterocytes from the artery into the portal vein. Two forms: `kps_mu_<s>` per litre of mucosa, and `PS_mu_ref_<s>` of the whole mucosa of the small intestine with `mucosa_ps_per_volume=False`. The flow-limited mucosa, the default, clears systemic drug with about `Qmu*E_G`, which a drug whose total clearance is far below that flow cannot do; with a small `PS` the gut availability and the systemic clearance of the gut are separated, and with a large one the model returns to the flow-limited flows. Docs page `intestine.md` with the tested equations and the limitations, `tests/pbpk/test_mucosa.py`.
- **Turnover of a protein and its stabilisation by a substance**. `PBPKDefinition(turnover={"<protein>": value})` sets the degradation rate constant of a protein in every organ which has its turnover state (1/hr with a source, or a fitted `Value` in 1/min), instead of the values of the organ (`[turnover]` of `database/tables/proteins.toml`). `Substance(stabilises=(Stabilisation("CYP2E1", ki),))` declares that the bound substance protects a protein from its degradation (ethanol and CYP2E1, Roberts 1995): the degradation becomes `kdeg*E/(1 + sum I/Kstab)`, so the amount of the protein approaches `(1 + induction)*(1 + sum I/Kstab)`, with the constant `Kstab_<protein>_<s>` of every stabiliser. `validate` refuses a protein without a turnover state and `stationary.py` a substance with a generated steady state which changes a turnover state; without either option the SBML is unchanged (`turnover_reference` of `tests/pbpk/data/`).
- **Intramuscular, subcutaneous and rectal routes**. `Route.im`, `Route.sc` and `Route.rectal` (`Depot(ka, fraction, portal, application)` of a model, else the defaults of `database/tables/routes.toml`) apply the dose state `IMDOSE_`/`SCDOSE_`/`RECDOSE_<s>` with the half-life `ti_<k>_<s>` into the depot `Aim_`/`Asc_`/`Arec_<s>`, a species of the top model in the compartment `Vim`/`Vsc`/`Vrec`, absorbed with `ka_<k>_<s>`: the fraction `f_<k>_<s>` into `Cve_<s>`, of the rectal dose `f_rec_portal_<s>` of it into `Cpo_<s>`, the rest into `Aloss_im_`/`Aloss_sc_<s>` or the feces. A second dose adds to the dose state and so to the depot, and the depot doses join `DOSE_PREFIXES`, so the equilibration moves them to the start. The defaults are the absorption rates 1/hr, choices of the library which are not verified, the fraction 1, and the rectal portal fraction 0.45 from the lidocaine availabilities of de Boer 1979. A definition without a depot route builds the same SBML.
- **Fed state of the dose-dependent dissolution, and the enteric-coated tablet**. `DoseDependentDissolution(k, dose50, fed=, enteric=)` takes the fed factor `f_dis_fed_<s>` on its saturating rate, as `FirstOrderDissolution` does, and both take `enteric=True`: an enteric-coated tablet has no dissolution `J_po_<s>` in the stomach, it empties undissolved with the transit time of the stomach of the condition and dissolves from the duodenum on, and its saturation amount is the dose outside the stomach (the single lumen refuses the coat, naming the substance). The figure of a model names the dissolution, the fed factor and the coat of every tablet.
- **CES1, CYP2E1, UGT2B15, UGT1A3 and CYP3A5 in the protein table**. The five enzymes of the hand-written models of pkdb_models are proteins of the library with their UniProt and NCIt annotations, their hepatic and intestinal expression (CYP3A5: Paine 2006, Lin 2002, Kuehl 2001) and the consensus of cirrhosis of the parameter database; none of them has a factor of CKD, which Yoshida 2016 tabulates for CYP2D6 and the CYP3A4/5 model drugs together only (a limitation of `impairment.md`).
- **Dose states of the models in the battery and in the equivalency of the inputs** ([#115](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/issues/115), [#124](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/issues/124)). The PK battery and the equivalency of the inputs enumerated the dose states of a model by a hard-coded list written before the depot routes, so a depot dose was no dose of the mass balance, of the superposition, of the fates and of the inputs. Both now read one list, `pkpdsim.credibility.battery.dose_prefixes()`: the dose states of the library (`pkpdsim.pbpk.equilibration.DOSE_PREFIXES`, so a route the library gains needs no change here) plus `LEGACY_DOSE_STATES`, the dose states of the models which the library does not build, each naming its model (`INHDOSE_` inhaled, `BUDOSE_` buccal, `REDOSE_` the rectal spelling before `RECDOSE_`, `SLDOSE_` sublingual, `POSOLUTION_` an oral solution). The qualifier of a flattened submodel is stripped, so a dose state of a submodel is a dose state and a qualified dose is added to its state instead of replacing it. `Aloss_<kind>_<s>`, what an injected depot does not absorb, is the part `loss` of a fate instead of a part of the body, and the unabsorbed part of a rectal dose is in `feces`.
- **The new check `dose_states` of the battery**. A state in mg which a reaction reads and which is no dose of any prefix is a dose state or a depot of a dose the battery does not know, which nothing simulated and nothing reported. The check `dose_states` (factor use error, one case per build, first of the battery) names such a state with the prefix to add and the known failure to declare, instead of leaving it out in silence, while the mass balance keeps its arithmetic meaning and does not fail for it. The check needs no simulation and no molecular weight, so a build without a substance has it as well. The detector is a heuristic in both directions: it finds a parameter with a rate rule, in mg, which a reaction reads, with no false positive over the tracked builds of pkdb_models, and it does not find a dose state in another unit, one which is a species or one which is a constant without a rate rule.

## Differences users see

- A model package which sets `mucosa_ps` of its intestine variant, `turnover` of its definition, `stabilises` of a substance, a depot route, `enteric` or the `fed` factor of a dose-dependent dissolution gets new SBML elements; a definition which sets none of them builds the SBML of 0.21, line by line but for the dates (the stored references of `tests/pbpk/data/`).
- `run_credibility` writes one more row in the battery of every model, the check `dose_states`, so the battery of a model has one applicable check more than in 0.21 and the records of 0.21 have no result of it. A model with a dose state the battery does not know fails the new check: of the models of pkdb_models the patch and ring depots of ethinylestradiol (`Askin_ee`, `Askin_lng`, `Avag_ee`) are such states, and a model declares them as a known failure with its reason (`CheckSettings.battery`) or gives them their prefix.
- The mass balance, the superposition and the fates now cover the dose states the list had missed, so a model with a depot, inhaled, buccal, sublingual or oral-solution dose state is checked on more cases and may fail where nothing was checked before: the inhaled dose of albuterol fails the mass balance at 0.279 of the dose, the fraction the model drops without a species. The fate of a dose has the part `loss` where an injected depot does not absorb its whole dose, which the table of the report and the release page name.
- The equivalency of the inputs checks the doses of the depot routes and of the legacy dose states as well, so a COU can gain input checks; `pkpdsim.credibility.inputs.DOSE_PREFIXES` is gone, `inputs.dose_prefixes()` takes its place.
- `pkpdsim.pbpk.ids` gains the ids of the depot routes (`depot_dose`, `depot`, `depot_compartment`, `depot_application_time`, `depot_absorption_rate`, `depot_fraction`, `rectal_portal_fraction`, `depot_loss`, `depot_application`, `depot_absorption`, `depot_loss_reaction`), of the permeability-limited mucosa (`mucosa_kps`, `mucosa_ps_ref`, `mucosa_ps`, `mucosa_qgut`) and of a stabilisation (`kstab`).

## Dependencies

- No changes; sbmlsim 0.18 (`>=0.18.0,<0.19`).
