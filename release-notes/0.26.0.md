# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.26.0

The archive of a model carries its interactive panels: sbmlsim 0.24.0 declares the panels a reader plays with in an annotation of the SBML of a model, and `create_archive` writes them into the flat models of the builds before the archive is written, so the archive of a release holds the models with their panels.

## Features

- **The archive of a model carries the interactive panels of its builds** ([#192](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/192)). `create_archive` of `pkpdsim.pbpk` and of `pkpdsim.perfusion` takes `panels=`: one list of `sbmlsim.panel.Panel` for every build or the panels of each build by its sex. The panels of a build are written into its flat model with `sbmlsim.panel_sbml.write_panels` before the ODEs, the antimony and the archive are written, so the archive is written once. Before anything is written, `check_panels` refuses a key which is no built sex, an object which is no panel and an id repeated within a build. Without `panels` nothing changes. The factories of the model packages which wrote the panels themselves and the archive a second time become one call.

## Dependencies

- `sbmlsim>=0.24.0`, which declares the interactive panels of a model (`sbmlsim.panel`, `sbmlsim.panel_sbml`) and serves them (`python -m sbmlsim.serve`, the extra `web`), and compiles a formula of PEtab math with its numbers as the doubles of their text and its operations as written.
