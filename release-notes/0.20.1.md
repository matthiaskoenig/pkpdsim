# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.20.1

The waist circumference as the covariate of the visceral adipose tissue, generated figures of a model and of its individuals, and a cleanup of unused functions and keywords. pkpdsim 0.20.1 runs on sbmlsim 0.18 (the lock takes sbmlsim 0.18.2, whose parallel scans pass large results through shared memory).

## Features

- **The waist circumference** ([#161](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/161), [#154](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/issues/154)). Every build with the adipose option carries the waist circumference, predicted from the sex, body weight, height and age of the population (`WAIST_base`, NHANES 2013-2018 anchored on Haueise 2023) or measured (`f_waist`, the measured waist over that prediction, `pkpdsim.pbpk.waist_factor`), the waist-to-height ratio `WHtR`, and the visceral share of the adipose tissue follows the waist (`f_vat` with the sensitivity of Haueise 2023).
- **Generated figures of a model** ([#163](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/163), [#159](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/issues/159)). The anatomical figure of the male and the female build (`anatomy_figure`), the schematic with icons of the organs and the routes (`pkpdsim.pbpk.figure`), the bodies of individuals stretched by their age, waist and body mass index (`body_strip`), interactive pages with hover cards (`interactive_figure`) and the publication figure on one A4 page (`publication_figure`, PDF with the extra `pdf`). The drawings come from the anatomograms of the EBI Expression Atlas (CC BY 4.0, `resources/anatomy/NOTICE.md`); `create_archive` also writes the body figure `<sid>_body.svg`. Docs page `pbpk/figures.md`.

## Cleanup

- Removed ([#165](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/165)): `pbpk.populations.bw_median` and `database.integration.prediction_interval`, which nothing used, and the keywords `fits_path` and `factory_path` of a model package, which no model of pkdb_models uses (the other keywords of 0.19 are still accepted).
- The documentation of the verification no longer describes the layouts of sbmlsim 0.8 and 0.9, and the installation states pkpdsim 0.20 on sbmlsim 0.18.

## Dependencies

- `contourpy` is a direct dependency (it came with matplotlib before); the new extra `pdf` installs cairosvg for the PDF of the publication figure.
