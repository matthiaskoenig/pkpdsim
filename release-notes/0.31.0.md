# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.31.0

The body figure of a model is interactive: every clickable place of the drawing declares the timecourses of the model it stands for, and those declarations travel in the COMBINE archive of a release, so a web application builds a panel per organ, per sample site and per process without knowing the id scheme. Next to it the whole-body cardiovascular circulation of 0D pressures and resistances, and the lobes of the liver with the surgical interventions of portal hypertension. With sbmlsim 0.32.0, whose `panels_from_figure` reads the new sidecar and whose app checks every curve against the compiled model.

## Features

- **The readouts of a model figure** ([#216](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/216), [#218](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/218)). Every key of the interactive body figure declares what the model reports at that place: `readouts(content)` gives one `ReadoutGroup` per key, each `Readout` naming the selection, its label in words, its unit and its kind (`concentration`, `amount`, `rate` or `flux`), and `write_readouts(content, svg, *, vein="arm vein")` writes them as `<svg stem>.readouts.json` beside the drawing under the contract `figurereadouts/1`. A key which reports nothing says why, so a consumer can tell a decoration of the drawing from a gap in the declaration. The sidecar is an entry of the COMBINE archive of a model release, which is what lets an application present the figure as a map of its panels; the ids are those of the id scheme and are not checked against a compiled model, so the consumer filters what its model does not carry.
- **The whole-body cardiovascular circulation** ([#208](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/issues/208)). `Variants(circulation=PressureFlow())` derives steady organ perfusion from pressures and vascular resistance; `DynamicHeart()` adds compliant reservoirs, time-varying ventricular elastance and valve flows. Both retain the portal circulation and the hepatic dual supply, support resistance interventions and pharmacodynamic feedback, and use the core engine of sbmlsim with SUNDIALS IDA. Fractional perfusion remains the default.
- **Liver lobe simulations** ([#209](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/issues/209)). `LobedLiver` is an organ variant with two spatial axes, the lobes in parallel and the zones of a lobe in series, with the Couinaud segments of the human and the anatomical lobes of rat and mouse in the parameter database. The interventions are parameter maps (`PortalLigation`, `ArterialLigation`, `Resection`, the named procedures of a species in `scenario_changes(species)`), so a surgery happens before a simulation or between two phases of one. On `PressureFlow` every lobe gets its own node with the three resistances of its branches, so a ligation raises the portal pressure, which prescribed flows cannot show, and the hepatic arterial buffer response of Lautt is available for a deportalised lobe. The pages are `docs/pbpk/lobes.md` and the browser explorer `docs/pbpk/lobes_examples.md`.
- **The interactive examples of the circulation and the lobes in the documentation**. The cardiovascular explorer is a compact dashboard with the male and female bodies of the physiology figures and their sex-specific simulations, shared resistance sliders, heart rate and contractility controls, live IDA integration in the browser, flow-width schematics with adjustable scaling, synchronized plot markers, playback and CSV downloads, and it is embedded in the cardiovascular documentation alone. The examples include the hepatic resistance changes relevant to portal hypertension. Their adult-human parameters are illustrative and require calibration for a specific application.

## Fixes

- **A concentration readout of the sidecar names the selection which gives a concentration** ([#219](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/219)). The bound forms of a substance are species with `hasOnlySubstanceUnits="false"`, whose plain id gives the amount in `mmole`, so their readouts are the bracket selection (`[LI__Cpl_api]`) and no longer declare `mmole/liter` for an amount; the unbound forms and the three observers of the blood sample are parameters which already hold a concentration and keep their plain id, as do the amounts and the rates of the urine, the feces and the lumen. An application of a PBPK figure could not be built before this, since sbmlsim refuses a curve whose unit is no unit of its values.
- **Every text file and every subprocess is read as utf-8** ([#220](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/220)). `_git`, which reads the provenance of a build, ran git with `text=True` and no encoding, so its output was decoded with the codec of the locale and raised `UnicodeDecodeError` under a locale which is not utf-8 on the first commit whose metadata is not ascii. The tests carried the same omission in 37 `read_text()` calls and six `subprocess.run` sites, where the windows code page returns mojibake instead of raising, so a comparison failed on a character its message never named.
- **The core engine initializes a model with algebraic rules**. PBPK initialization, renal-flow validation and the endogenous balance checks use the core engine when a model contains algebraic rules, so a pressure-driven model initializes its generated stationary concentrations consistently and retains its baseline after a later intervention.
