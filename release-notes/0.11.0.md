# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.11.0

pkpdsim 0.11.0 runs on sbmlsim 0.13, which makes the simulation experiments of a model faster. `run_simulation pkdb_models.models.hctz --action simulate --experiments all` (28 experiments, 128 figures) takes 16 s instead of 28 s with sbmlsim 0.12.1 on the same busy machine, and 12 s instead of 21 s on an idle one. The simulations and the fits are unchanged.

## Dependencies

- pkpdsim requires `sbmlsim>=0.13.0,<0.14` (was `>=0.12.0,<0.13`), and the lock of the development environment has sbmlsim 0.13.0 ([release notes of 0.12.1](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/releases/tag/0.12.1), [of 0.13.0](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/releases/tag/0.13.0)).
  - **sbmlsim 0.12.1** fixes the memory of a run:
    - The units of a model are read without the leak of libsbml, which was about 13 MB per model.
    - A figure is measured without being drawn dozens of times. The HCTZ run took 37 s and peaked at 12 GB with sbmlsim 0.12.0.
  - **sbmlsim 0.13.0** brings the speed:
    - The experiments of a run run in a pool of worker processes.
    - A figure is saved without a second drawing.
    - A formula is parsed without importing petab and torch.
    - The PK observables of the steps of the integrator are analysed in batches.

## Changes

- **The experiments of a run run in a pool of processes.**
  - **When.** With the default of a model, serial scans (`n_workers=1`, `--workers model` or `--workers 1`), sbmlsim runs the experiments of `run_simulation` and `model.run_experiments` in a pool: a worker per four experiments, at most one per CPU, and a few experiments serially. Every experiment runs with its scans in its worker. With a pool of the scans (`--workers N` or `auto`) the experiments run one after the other, so the scans keep their pool.
  - **What it costs.** A worker of a PBPK model needs about 330 MB. The 28 experiments of HCTZ run on 7 workers with about 3.2 GB of all processes instead of 0.5 GB.
  - **Unchanged.** The outputs, the figures, the report and the order of the messages are the same.
  - **Documentation.** The help of `--workers`, the commands and the settings of a model describe this.
