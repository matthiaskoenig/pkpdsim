# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.9.3

pkpdsim 0.9.3 draws the uncertainty of the fitted parameters in the figures of a run over the draws (`run_simulation <package> --uncertainty`) also for the curves whose x is not the time ([#57](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/57)).

## Features

- A curve over the values of a dimension of its scan (`x = <dimension>.<target>`, e.g. the net sodium at 12 hr over the scanned renal function of HCTZ Knauf1995 Fig3) becomes the band of the quantiles of the draws with the median, as a curve over the time. The values of a dimension are the same in every draw; such a curve drew only the simulation with the fitted values before.
- A curve over another variable of its task, which differs between the draws (e.g. the blood pressure over the HCTZ concentration of the dose dependency), has no common x for the quantiles of a band. It keeps the curve of the fitted values (`<simulation>_fitted`, its name gets "(fit)") and draws a thin translucent line of every draw in its colour below it, without a legend entry and with an opacity of `min(0.3, 10 / draws)`, so the area of the draws is denser where more draws lie. The lines of all curves of a plot come first, below the fitted values and the data. A curve with a line per point of a dimension keeps only the fitted values.

The figures of a run over the draws change where they have such curves; the SVG of a figure with many lines of the draws grows (the multiple dose figure of the HCTZ dose dependency from 15 to 23 MB with 100 draws).
