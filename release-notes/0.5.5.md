# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.5.5

pkpdsim 0.5.5 drops the workarounds of the sensitivity runner for [sbmlsim#311](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/311), which sbmlsim 0.9.5 fixes, and requires sbmlsim 0.9.5. The written tables are the same.

## Fixes

- The runner takes the classes of the sensitivities and uncertainties from `sbmlsim.sensitivity.classification` (`sensitivity_classification`, `uncertainty_classification` and their symbols), which since sbmlsim 0.9.5 give `None` for `NaN` and classify a negative uncertainty by its magnitude, and writes an empty class for `None`; the private `_sensitivity_class` and `_uncertainty_class` are removed. The floor of sbmlsim is `>=0.9.5,<0.10`. Why: the workaround was to go when sbmlsim fixed #311. The tables (`sampling_N<n>.tsv`, `_compact.tsv`, `.tex`, the local tables) of a run with a zero output (`NaN`) and a negative output (negative `U`) are byte for byte those of 0.5.4. One difference: an infinite value (`U` of an output whose median is zero but not its spread) is `high` with its symbol, where the workaround left it empty.
