# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.13.0

pkpdsim 0.13.0 simulates with sbmlsim 0.14 ([sbmlsim 0.14.0](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/releases/tag/0.14.0)). The simulations of a model are unchanged. What changes comes with sbmlsim:

- **Fits.** `run_fit` takes the step of the finite differences of sbmlsim 0.14, the square root of the relative tolerance of the integrator (`1e-3` for the default `1e-6`), instead of `0.05`; the re-optimizations of the profiles of `run_identifiability` take it as well. On the HCTZ fit of sbmlsim's example both steps reach the same best cost, the step of the tolerance a slightly lower one (28.404 against 28.414). A fit gives other parameters than with 0.12, within the precision of its optimum.
- **Fisher information.** It takes central differences with the cube root of the relative tolerance (`1e-2`) instead of `1e-6`, at which its standard errors were up to 30 times too small. The intervals of the Fisher information of a new fit are wider; the adopted fits keep their records.
- **Fits simulate the times of their data**, with the selections of their mappings only, and the report of a fit draws the curves of the simulation instead of lines between the times of the data.
- **Figures** write the short units of pint, `time [h]` for `hr`, and the error of data as ` ± SD`.
- **A run of the experiments** of `run_simulation` shows its progress and a table of the seconds every experiment took per phase on an interactive terminal. The timing messages of the log are gone.
- **Results** are quantities first: `res.q[...]`, `res.to(...)`, plots in units with `sbmlsim.plot.label_axes`.

## Changes

- **sbmlsim 0.14** ([#89](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/89)): pkpdsim requires `sbmlsim>=0.14.0,<0.15`.
- **`pkpdsim-verify compare` across versions of sbmlsim** ([#89](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/89)). The label of a curve is its name, which sbmlsim 0.14 keeps out of the style arguments (`kwargs.label`), and the error of the label of data is ` ± SD` as sbmlsim 0.14 writes it. A figure of an earlier version compares without these two differences.
- **A replay of the sampling profiler which fails** (`--profile`, python 3.15) reports the output of the profiler instead of a bare `CalledProcessError` ([#89](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/89)).
- **The profiler tests on python 3.15.1** expect a failure: the sampling profiler of CPython 3.15.1 crashes now and then while it writes a sample of the binary profile (`UnicodeEncodeError` in `binary_collector.write_sample`), which 3.15.0 does not.
