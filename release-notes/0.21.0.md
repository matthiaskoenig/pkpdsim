# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.21.0

The machine perfusion library `pkpdsim.perfusion`, which builds the model of an ex vivo machine perfusion circuit with a mounted organ, and grey organ icons in the figures of a model. pkpdsim 0.21.0 runs on sbmlsim 0.18, like 0.20.

## Features

- **Machine perfusion models** ([#169](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/169), [#146](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/issues/146)). `pkpdsim.perfusion` builds one SBML model of a perfusion circuit from a `PerfusionDefinition`: reservoir, pump (total pump flow `Q_pump`), oxygenator (O2 towards a target pO2, CO2 towards the pCO2 of its sweep gas), inflow and outflow tubing and the temperature with an Arrhenius factor. The organ is a zonated liver (zones in series along the sinusoid, zonal profiles, bile), a kidney (filtration, reabsorption, urine) or a generic organ, each zone with a cellular core of energy metabolism, glutathione, necrosis releasing the enzymes of the organ and potassium, and the bicarbonate buffer and pH of the perfusate; the constants are in the parameter database (`database/tables/perfusion.toml`). Observers give every quantity at every site, pO2, pCO2, pH, the enzyme activities in µkat/l, bile per mass and urine. `PerfusionSimulationExperiment` runs the experiments with medium changes (`medium_changes`) and the flow, organ mass and temperature of a perfusion (`perfusion_changes`); `create_archive` writes the model, its figures A (the organ in its circuit) and B (the schematic), the ODEs and the COMBINE archive. The vocabulary of the fit mappings has `Tissue.PERFUSATE`, `Route.EX_VIVO`, `Dosing.PERFUSION` and the sampling sites `SamplingSite.RESERVOIR`, `INFLOW` and `OUTFLOW`. Docs page `perfusion.md` with tested equations and its limitations.
- **Grey organ icons** ([#167](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/167)). The organ icons of the anatomical figure (A) and the schematic (B) are greys, one per organ (`palette.ORGAN_GREYS`), so the colours of a figure are those of the substances and the tissues. The docs show the figures A and B of every model a page creates as interactive pages.

## Differences users see

- The citation of `ModelChoice` names both libraries, the PBPK and the machine perfusion library, so the notes of every SBML model of the PBPK library change in that text.
- The title of a PBPK schematic wraps when the name of the model is longer than one line of the legend, so the archive figure of such a model changes; the credit line of the interactive pages names what is drawn.

## Dependencies

- No changes; sbmlsim 0.18 (`>=0.18.0,<0.19`).
