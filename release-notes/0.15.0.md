# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.15.0

pkpdsim 0.15.0 adds `pkpdsim.pbpk`, one modular library from which the PBPK models of pkdb_models are built ([#78](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/issues/78)), with a consistent and complete annotation including the PBPK ontology ([#63](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/issues/63)), and the components which the migration of the first seven models (hctz, glimepiride, apixaban, torasemide, inulin, gdeobdtpa, creatinine) needed. The code shared with the other models gains builds per sex, the sampling site of the data, the optimization level of a model, the start of a fit and a common output grid of `pkpdsim-verify`. It contains the changes of 0.14.1, which was released from `release/0.14.x`. A model which does not use `pkpdsim.pbpk` simulates as before; what it sees:

- **Regenerated SBML has more annotations**: the compartments of `pkpdsim.annotations` gain terms (UBERON among others), so a model whose factory runs again changes the annotations of its SBML, not its equations.
- **The sampling site** is a field of the metadata of every fit mapping: the overview of the data of a fit has the column `sampling_site`, `arm vein` for plasma, serum and blood unless a study states another site.
- **`run_fit` refuses bounds which make a remainder negative**: a fit whose fitted fractions enter an initial assignment `1 - a - b - ...` and can sum above 1 at their upper bounds.
- **`pkpdsim-verify compare`** compares the rows of a time which only one dump has by interpolation instead of counting them as differences.
- **New runtime dependencies**: `sbmlode`, `pymetadata` and `scipy`.

## Changes

- **The PBPK library `pkpdsim.pbpk`** ([#94](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/94)). A model is a `PBPKDefinition`: substances with their flags (protein binding, erythrocytes, urinary and fecal excretion, tissue distribution), the chemistry defined once per organ, routes, organ variants, PD components, the population and the sex. `build(definition, directory)` writes the comp model and the flat SBML.
  - **Physiology.** The ICRP 89 reference man and woman, scaled to body weight and height as in Huisinga 2012; adipose tissue is the remainder of the body weight, the flows are renormalised to the cardiac output. Populations `european_icrp` (default), `white_american_nhanes`, `black_american_nhanes`, `mexican_american_nhanes` (from httk) and `east_asian_tanaka`.
  - **Standard organ.** Plasma, blood cells, interstitial and intracellular parts, collapsed per substance by its `Mode`: `PERFUSION` (flow-limited), `TISSUE` (merged tissue with finite exchange, the default) or `PERMEABILITY`. Kp is the whole-tissue to plasma coefficient in every mode, one per substance with organ overrides or computed by Rodgers & Rowland (`KpMethod`).
  - **Blood.** Erythrocytes none, at equilibrium (`bp` or `kbc`) or kinetic; free concentrations and the binding to the plasma proteins (`Binding`, `PROTEINS`).
  - **Chemistry.** `Metabolism` and `Transport` with `FirstOrder` (per litre of organ, `per_volume=False` for a whole-organ clearance), `MM` and `Hill`; competitive, noncompetitive, uncompetitive, mixed and mechanism-based inhibition and induction (enzyme turnover); `CustomReaction` for a mechanism without a class.
  - **Variants.** The same chemistry placed by volume into the parts of a variant: `ZonatedLiver`, `SegmentedIntestine` (the 12 lumen segments of Thelen 2011 from the stomach to the rectum, mucosa, dissolution, fed state) and `NephronKidney`.
  - **Impairment.** `CP_severity` (0 to 3), `f_renal_function` and `HF_severity` (0 to 4); their effects are initial assignments, a parameter listed in `dynamic` changes during a run.
  - **Observers.** The arm vein is the default sampling site, `Carm_<s>` (preset `pksim_default`, 0.7 skin and 0.3 muscle; further presets `levitt2004`, `musther2015`, `arterialised`, chosen by `PBPKDefinition.arm_vein`). Further observers: hepatic vein, free and blood concentrations, unbound tissue concentrations, the urinary excretion rate `Rurine_<s>`, and the `Observer`s of a definition (e.g. the sum of metabolites).
  - **Composition.** PD models (`PDComponent`) coupled through INPUT/OUTPUT ports; `PBPKDefinition.combine` couples several substance packages in one physiology, so an inhibition across packages works.
  - **Annotation.** Every generated element carries an SBO term and PBPKO, UBERON, NCIT or UniProt terms; a PBPKO term is written as its bioregistry url (`https://bioregistry.io/PBPKO:nnnnn`), which resolves, since identifiers.org does not register PBPKO.
  - **Data.** Every value has a source in `pkpdsim/pbpk/data/sources.toml`; a value not read in its primary source is a `ModelChoice` marked "not verified"; no value comes from the PK-Sim database (GPL) or from a source under a non-commercial license. The acidic phospholipids of the tissues and the composition of the erythrocytes of the Rodgers & Rowland coefficients are the rat values of the tissue composition of Utsey 2020 (CC BY 4.0), corroborated by Ye 2016 ([#101](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/101)).
  - **Documentation.** The section "PBPK library" of the documentation (`docs/pbpk/`): overview, a tutorial which runs, physiology, standard organ, substances, chemistry, variants, intestine and routes, impairment, observers, annotation and a migration guide, with diagrams, data tables generated from the TOML files (`scripts/pbpk_tables.py`) and equations checked against a built model; API pages of the public modules of the library.
- **The components of the first seven models** ([#97](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/97)), what their migration needed, as components of the library instead of code in the models.
  - **Definition.** Top parameters which formulas and custom reactions read (`ModelParameter`, with provenance; a formula may read the constants of its organ), shared rates and product fractions (`REST`), the starts of the species (`starts`), an endogenous substance at its baseline, oral absorption per substance, the fed state and the formulation scaling the dissolution (one table of the dissolution parameters of a tablet).
  - **Kidney.** The glomerulus filters the afferent plasma and the plasma leaving the kidney carries the plasma flow less the filtration; saturable secretion and reabsorption (`ThresholdReabsorption`); the validity of the kidney outflow, bounded by the filtered plasma water and simulated only for a model which filters (`kidney_outflow`, `KidneyOutflow`); `healthy_gfr` of the reference individual.
  - **Distribution.** The extracellular distribution, a slow tissue pool fed from the tissue or the plasma of an organ (`SlowPool`), `interstitial_share(organ, fu, sex)`, the CKD profile of the binding per protein, the CKD floor of the cell partition per organ (`ckd_floor={organ: reason}`, `KCellFloorWarning`).
  - **Intestine.** Conversions in the lumen (`LumenConversion`), the regional absorption profile (`pkpdsim.pbpk.regional`: `derive_regional_absorption`, `release_auc_ratios`), the transit time of the stomach of the condition (`T_st_eff`), the readouts on request `Aabs_<s>` (amount absorbed) and `Fa_<s>` (gross absorbed fraction).
  - **Steady state.** The steady state of the organs as formulas and the generated steady state of custom reactions (`pkpdsim.pbpk.stationary`).
  - **Builds per sex.** A female build next to the male one from one definition (`sex_definition`), chosen by the experiments (see below).
  - **Equilibration.** The drug-free steady state of the condition of a simulation (`pkpdsim.pbpk.equilibration`, `equilibrated_simulation`, also outside an experiment), used by the experiments, the fits and the sensitivity analysis (`SensitivityGroup(prepare=pkpdsim.pbpk.Equilibration(resets=...))`).
  - **Readouts.** Of organs, the intestine and constants; the change of an observer over its start (`deltas`, `<id>_delta`); the urinary excretion rate net of a reaction from the urine.
  - **Experiments and archive.** `PBPKSimulationExperiment`, the base of the experiments of a library model: healthy GFR, renal function (`renal_function`, `renal_function_limits`, `renal_map_of`, taking `simulation=` or `sex=` and refusing a mixed-sex experiment without one), impairment maps (`ScaledRenalMap`, `CHILD_PUGH`, `HEART_FAILURE`), `molecular_weights(definition)`. `create_archive` is the factory of a library model in one call (the licenses, the ODEs, the COMBINE archive with the model figure of `pkpdsim.pbpk.figure`); unit labels which do not wrap (`UNIT_LABELS`, `unit_label`, `axis()`).
  - **Lazy imports.** The names which import sbmlsim are exported lazily, so `import pkpdsim.pbpk` imports no simulator.
- **Annotations** ([#94](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/94)).
  - **Before.** `fluid volume` had no terms, `re`, `rev`, `kiv` and `fov` were marked FIXME.
  - **Now.** UBERON terms for `li`, `he`, `ki`, `lu`, `mu` (skeletal muscle tissue) and `plasma`; `fluid volume` is the extracellular fluid (MeSH) and a version of body fluid (NCIT), `re` part of the multicellular organism, `rev` part of the venous system, `kiv` of the renal vein, `fov` of a vein of the forearm. New keys `br`, `si`, `la`, `sk`, `ad`, `bo`, `go`, the segments of the colon from `caecum` to `rectum` and the tubule segments (`proximal tubule`, `loop of Henle`, `distal tubule`, `collecting duct`). No key changes its meaning.
  - **Models.** A model regenerated with 0.15.0 has the added terms in the annotations of its SBML; the simulations are unchanged.
- **Impairment maps** ([#94](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/94)): `CHILD_PUGH` (the Child-Pugh classes as `CP_severity` 0 to 3, colors `CIRRHOSIS_COLORS`) and `HEART_FAILURE` (the NYHA classes as `HF_severity` 0 to 4, colors `HEART_FAILURE_COLORS`) for the models of the library. The existing maps are unchanged.
- **Sampling site** ([#94](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/94), [#97](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/97)).
  - **Before.** The metadata of a fit mapping did not say where the blood was drawn.
  - **Now.** `pkpdsim.metadata.SamplingSite` (`ARM_VEIN`, `VENOUS`, `ARTERIAL`, `ARTERIALISED`, `HEPATIC_VEIN`, `CAPILLARY`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`) and the field `PKPDMappingMetaData.sampling_site`, appended after the other fields, `None` when no site is stated. `resolved_sampling_site()` gives the stated site, else the site of the tissue: `ARM_VEIN` for plasma, serum and blood, `ARTERIAL` for arterial blood and plasma, `NOT_APPLICABLE` for saliva, urine, feces and bile. A site which contradicts the tissue is a `ValueError`. `to_dict()` and the filters (`metadata_filter(sampling_site=...)`) use the resolved site. `pkpdsim.fit.check_sampling_sites(fit_mappings, sites)` lists the mappings whose site differs from the site of their observable (e.g. `Carm_<s>` is `ARM_VEIN`).
  - **Models.** The overview of the data of a fit has the column `sampling_site`; a subclass of `PKPDMappingMetaData` keeps its fields.
- **Builds per sex** ([#97](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/97)): `pkpdsim.sex.Sex`; `PKPDModel.female_model_path`, `model_paths` (by sex, the male build first) and `model_path_of(sex)`, `model_path` stays the male build. An experiment sets the sex of its subjects (`PKPDSimulationExperiment.sex`, male by default) and the sex of single simulations (`simulation_sexes`); a task runs the build of its sex (`model`, or `model_<sex>` for the other sex, `model_id`, `sex_of`); a sex without a build is a `ValueError` naming the experiment. `check_fit_parameters` checks every build and that the female build has the distributions of the male one, whose draws `--uncertainty` runs on every build; `check_model` checks that the female build lies below the model; a dump of `pkpdsim-verify` records the build of every task. A model without a female build is unchanged.
- **Optimization level of a model** ([#97](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/97)): `SimulatorSettings.jit_optimization` (`"O0"` to `"O3"`, `None` for the level of sbmlsim) is the level at which LLJIT compiles the model for `run_simulation`, the fits, their reports and the identifiability analysis (in the process and in every worker, `pkpdsim.fit.at_level`, `leveled_definitions`), the tests of `pkpdsim.testing` and `pkpdsim-verify dump`. `O0` compiles a model with large initial assignments several times faster (creatinine 4.5 s instead of 24 s). `PKPDModel.load_roadrunner(sex)` loads a build at the level. A model without a level runs as before.
- **`run_fit --start`** ([#97](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/97)): least squares starts from a point instead of samples of the bounds, a json file of the values by parameter id (in the units of the fit parameters) or the id of an adopted or finished fit; with `--runs 1`, not with differential evolution; the record of the fit names the start.
- **Remainders of fitted fractions** ([#97](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/97)).
  - **Before.** A fit could draw fractions whose remainder `1 - a - b - ...` was negative, a product formed at a negative rate.
  - **Now.** `run_fit` refuses the fit before it starts when the upper bounds of the fitted fractions of a remainder and the values of the others sum above 1 in any build (`pkpdsim.fit.remainder_problems`), naming the remainder and the bounds.
  - **Models.** A model whose fitted parameters enter such an initial assignment lowers their upper bounds.
- **Sensitivity** ([#97](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/97)).
  - **Before.** `parameters_from_sbml` loaded the model with roadrunner's MCJIT; a group ran the one model of the model object; a group ran its scan as it was.
  - **Now.** `parameters_from_sbml` reads the values from the model compiled at `O0` (the same values, a tenth of the compile time) and leaves out every parameter, compartment and species with an initial assignment with `exclude_derived=True`. `SensitivityGroup(sex=...)` runs the build of its sex: a global design is drawn once and carried to a female group relative to the values of the table (`build_values`, `design_factors`, `relative_design`), and the table has a column `value_<sex>` (`parameters_to_df(builds=...)`, `write_parameters(builds=...)`). `SensitivityGroup(prepare=...)` is a function `(scan, model_path) -> scan` which gives the scan that runs, e.g. the equilibration of the library; the module imports nothing of the library. For the models of the library: `library_parameters` (the table of the parameters an analysis names, the reference from the sources of the notes, `notes_sources`), `SEVERITIES` and `NonzeroLocalAnalysis` (the local analysis of the parameters whose value is not 0). The observables the models copied: `pk_observables` (the non-compartmental analysis of a concentration and its outputs) and `max_after_dose`.
  - **Models.** A model without `sex` and `prepare` in its groups gets the same tables and results.
- **`pkpdsim-verify`** ([#97](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/97)).
  - **Before.** `compare` matched the rows of two dumps by their times, a time which only one dump had was a difference, so two models or tolerances with other steps of the integrator could not be compared.
  - **Now.** `dump --grid N` outputs every simulation (and every simulation of a dimension of simulations) at `N + 1` equidistant times and the times of its changes, so both dumps of a migration share their times. Without the grid, a row of a time which only one dump has is compared with the other dump interpolated at its time; an array whose interpolation cannot resolve the threshold is reported as unresolved, not as a pass; a row outside of the times of the other dump stays a difference.
- **Experiments** ([#97](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/97)): `across()` (the dimension of the draws of a run over the fitted parameters, `None` without) and `add_function(plot, task_id, y, label, color, linestyle="-")` (a function of the results of a task as a curve, the band of the draws in a run over them).
- **`pkpdsim.distrib`** ([#97](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/97)): the grammar of the distrib formulas of the fitted parameters without a simulator (`parse`, `support`, `DistribFormula`), used by `pkpdsim.uncertainty` and by the library to bound a fitted fraction; `pkpdsim.uncertainty.convert_value` is the public conversion of a number between units.
- **Dependencies**: `sbmlsim>=0.14.1`, whose PEtab export reads petab 0.10.0 (the lock has petab 0.10.0 again) and whose species start at their initial concentration in a compartment changed by a rule; `sbmlode>=0.3.0`, `pymetadata>=0.8.0` (the archive of a library model) and `scipy>=1.18.1` (the confidence intervals, the interpolation of `pkpdsim-verify`, the regional absorption profile) are runtime dependencies; the lock and the action `setup-uv` updated by dependabot ([#95](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/95), [#96](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/96)).
- **API changes.** Against 0.14.1 the changes are additions: the new field `PKPDMappingMetaData.sampling_site` (last, so positional construction is unchanged), `PKPDModel.female_model_path`, `SimulatorSettings.jit_optimization`, the keyword-only `exclude_derived` of `parameters_from_sbml`, `sex` and `prepare` of `SensitivityGroup`, `builds` of `parameters_to_df` and `write_parameters`. `PKPDSimulationExperiment.models()` returns the builds the experiment simulates (`model`, and `model_<sex>` for another sex). The API changes named in #97 (`SensitivityGroup(equilibrate=..., resets=...)` to `prepare=`, the renal functions taking `simulation=` or `sex=`, `interstitial_share` without a default sex) are against #94, which was not released.

## Open

- Sources not read: Rodgers & Rowland 2005/2006 and Schmitt 2008 (the tissue composition is from Coutinho 2024 and Utsey 2020, `f_int` and the Schmitt constants from httk 2.7.4), Malik 2020 (chronic kidney disease acts on the plasma binding and the optional hepatic CYP2D6 and OATP only; hematocrit, kidney volume and flow and the gastrointestinal transit stay at factor 1), Edginton 2008 (taken via Alasmari 2024), the cirrhosis shunt, Bricker 1960; Drozdzik 2018 in its abstract only. The Japanese population is not added, its survey data were not accessible.
- The PBPKO namespace in pymetadata: pymetadata resolves resources only through identifiers.org, which has no PBPKO, so the library writes bioregistry urls; the PBPKO terms which are missing are listed for request in `docs/pbpk/annotation.md`.
- The migration of the remaining models of pkdb_models onto `pkpdsim.pbpk`; the pull requests of the first seven follow this release, and every ported model needs a refit.
- Loading the models of an experiment lazily, which needs changes in sbmlsim: the models are loaded before the simulations are created, so an experiment declares the sex of its simulations (`simulation_sexes`) instead of computing it.
