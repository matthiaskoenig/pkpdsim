# Release notes for pkpdsim 0.17.0

pkpdsim 0.17.0 adds the context of use and the credibility of a model, the first step of the verification, validation and uncertainty quantification of the models of pkdb_models ([pkdb_models#476](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkdb_models/issues/476)). A model declares its contexts of use in a module `credibility.py`, and the new command `run_credibility` assesses every one of them against the adopted fits of the model, following the credibility frame of ASME V&V 40 and ICH M15. The simulations, fits and releases of a model without a declaration are unchanged.

## Changes

- **The declaration of the contexts of use** (`pkpdsim.credibility`, [#109](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/pkpdsim/pull/109)). A `Credibility` holds the contexts of use of a model (`ContextOfUse`): the question, the level on the ladder C1 (describe), C2 (interpolate), C3 (extrapolate with data), C4 (extrapolate without data) and C5 (clinical endpoint), the model influence and the decision consequence with the model risk which follows from them, the quantities of interest, the claimed domain (values of the metadata of the mappings and a statement), the evidence (named selections of fit mappings with the roles calibration, validation and limitation) and the acceptance (fold criteria, GMFE and AFE, with defaults by risk). A declaration is checked when it is created and reports every problem at once. `PKPDModel.credibility` references the declaration like the experiments and the fit definitions.
- **The assessment.** The adopted fits are evaluated, the kinds of their mappings come from their records and must equal the current split of the fit definitions (a refit and a new adoption otherwise). Validation evidence on data which any adopted fit was trained on is an error, the limitations of the model are subtracted from every evidence and never enter an acceptance, and `Unclaimed(within=...)` selects validation data which is computed rather than named, e.g. held-out subjects. The acceptance is decided on the points the criteria can evaluate, the coverage of the claimed domain distinguishes `supported`, `supported_with_extrapolation`, `not_supported` and `insufficient_evidence`, and the credibility factors of V&V 40 are listed with their status. `check_declaration(model)` checks a declaration and the records without simulating.
- **`run_credibility <package>`**, the fifth command of the models, writes `credibility.json`, `contexts.tsv`, `evidence.tsv`, `domain.tsv` and an HTML report; `--from-json` renders a written record again, `--source-commit` names the source commit of a release.
- **Model releases.** `pkpd_release` runs the assessment of a model which declares its contexts of use, the release repository carries it in `credibility/` and its documentation gets a credibility page.
- **Shared named mappings.** `pkpdsim.fit.NamedMappings` and `named_filter` select the fit mappings of the named keys of every study, the copies of the models can import them.
- **Documentation.** The new page "Context of use and credibility" and its API page; the commands, the model object and the release describe `run_credibility`.
