# Physiology, populations and the build

Part of the architecture notes of pkpdsim, see [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md).

**Age and sex (`age.py`, `sex_differences.py`, `curves.py`, `virtual.py`).** The physiology over age is a choice of the build, `PBPKDefinition.age` (default `False`): a build over age has `AGE` as a covariate and every reference value and factor as a curve of it, a build without has the constants of the reference individual of `AGE_REFERENCE`, no `AGE` and the factors of the age as constant 1 (`_Physiology.over_age`, `curve`, `factor`, `constant_factor`; the sex factors as the constants of the adult, `_Impairment._sex_formula`, whose `sex()` runs for every human build while `age()` runs only over age; a laboratory animal over age is refused). The reason is roadrunner: without the cache of the initial value resolvers (sys-bio/roadrunner#1435) every initial assignment is inlined into each reader, so the curves grow the model from 60k to 820k nodes and its compile from 7 to 66 s at O0 (5 s with the cache); the tests which build over age carry the marker `over_age` of `tests/pbpk/conftest.py`, which skips them below roadrunner `IA_CACHE`. The physiology over age (#133) and the sex (#137): every reference value and factor of the age is a `Curve` of `curves.py` (`Interpolation`, `Hill` of the postnatal age in years or of the postmenstrual age in weeks of a birth at term (`pma`, 40 weeks at birth) with `x_min`, `Logarithmic` of the days, `Affine`, `Quotient`, `Product`, `Relative` (exactly 1 at its age: its formula divides by the formula at that age), `Spliced` (checked to meet at the splices); no `max`/`min`, which L3V1 lacks), written once and evaluated as a number (`value`) and as the formula of an initial assignment of `AGE` (`formula`), compared at 22 ages to 1e-12 by `tests/pbpk/test_age.py`. `age.py`: `reference_curves(sex)` the reference values over age (`BW_ref`, `HEIGHT_ref`, `M<o>_ref`, `Vblood_ref`, `CO_ref`): the reference children of ICRP 89 (`database/tables/children_human.toml`, newborn, 1, 5, 10, 15 years) and the adult at `ADULT_AGE` (20 years) linear between, the adult times an aging factor of `age_human.toml` (liver and kidney mass, cardiac output) 1 at `AGE_REFERENCE` (35 years, the adult of ICRP 89 and the default `AGE` of `european_icrp`), so the default individual keeps the values of before; `BSA_ref`, `FFM_ref` the formulas of `BW_ref`/`HEIGHT_ref`; `factor_curves(sex)` the factors `fage_GFR` (Rhodin 2009 maturation of the PMA times the aging of Delanaye 2022/Eriksen 2020 per sex; `GFR_base = GFR_ref*BSA/<adult BSA>*fage_GFR*fimp_GFR`), `fage_HCT` (Scholkmann 2019 neonates, NHANES of `blood_nhanes.toml`, `HCT_ref = HCT*fage_HCT`), `fage_FFM` (Al-Sallami 2015 below 20 years, on `FFM` and `FFM_ref`), `fage_albumin` (Johnson 2006 below 12 years, NHANES) and `fage_agp` (Maharaj 2018) in `fimp_albumin`/`fimp_agp` of the binding; `flow_factors`/`flow_fraction_curves` the flow fractions `fQ<o> = <adult> * factors` which sum to 1 (`fage_Qbr` the brain of the children, Chiron 1992 perfusion times mass over CO; `fage_Qrest` the share of the other organs; `fage_Qli` the liver group (hepatic artery and portal organs, `LIVER_GROUP`) and `fage_Qki` the kidney of the elderly; `fage_Qother` closes the sum, `rest_denominator` subtracts in the order of the formula so the factors are exactly 1 at 35 years); `protein_factor(organ, protein)` the ontogeny of `ontogeny.toml` (PK-Sim database CYPs/UGTs, Hunt 2024 kidney transporters, Prasad 2016 OCT1/OATP1B3/P-gp of the liver; `fage_<o>_<protein>`), `entry_curve` the curve of an entry of a table (`points`, `hill`, `log`, `constant`), `provenance` its `source`, further `sources` and notes. `sex_differences.py` (#137): the female build relative to a man of the same age, body weight and height beyond the ICRP 89 anatomy and FFM of each sex, every factor a curve of `AGE` of `database/tables/sex_human.toml` (1 in the children, puberty ramps and menopause steps linear, a choice), an initial assignment `fsex_<key>` of the female build only (the male build is the reference of a substance and has none, its SBML unchanged): `sex_curves(sex)` with `CARDIAC_OUTPUT` (`fsex_CO` on `CO_base`, 0.914 of the adults: the same cardiac index of the sexes at the same size, form `same_cardiac_index` computed at the ages of the entry by `same_cardiac_index(age)`, the male over the female `physiology.scaled_cardiac_output` at the size between the reference individuals of the age, 1 in the children, Williams 1994, the source of ICRP 89, Carlsson 2012, Middlemiss 2019; tested within 1.5 % from 12 years), `BRAIN_FLOW` (`fsex_Qbr` on `fQbr` and in `fage_Qrest`, 1.257 at 18-45, 1.096 from 65 years; Aanerud 2017, Xing 2017; `flow_fraction_curves` includes it, imported in the function), `ALBUMIN` (`fsex_albumin` in `fimp_albumin`, form `ratio`: the constant NHANES ratio women/men at 35 years, 0.9398, so with the per-sex `fage_albumin` the ratio of NHANES at every age), `GASTRIC_EMPTYING_FED` (`fsex_t_stomach_fed` on `T_st_fed` only, 1.2 from 15 to 45 years; Camilleri 2012, Vasavid 2014) and `COLON_TRANSIT` (`fsex_t_colon` on the transit of every segment of the large intestine, 1.333 from 20 years, ICRP 89), inputs of the intestine read when the build has them (`LumenBuilder._sex_input`); `protein_sex_factor(sex, organ, protein)` the curve of `[protein.<organ>.<protein>]` (CYP3A4 1.2, CYP1A2 0.9, UGT2B17 0.4, UGT2B15 0.75, CYP2A6 1.2 until the menopause; ramps 10 to 16 years), `protein_sex_factors()` the pairs; no factor (documented in `docs/pbpk/physiology.md`, Sex) for AGP, GFR and renal and hepatic flow per BSA, blood volume, body water, kidney and intestine proteins; the equations of the female build are a toy of `tests/docs/test_pbpk_equations.py`; tests `tests/pbpk/test_sex_differences.py`. `organ_activity` gives the input `fimp_<protein>` for an organ and protein with a factor of age or sex (`protein_factors() | protein_sex_factors()`) as well, `fimp_<o>_<protein>` is the product of the conditions, the age and the sex (`_Impairment.age`, `individual_proteins`). The eGFR of the CKD stages stays relative to the adult of 35 years (aging is no CKD); `normal_gfr(sex, age)`, `class_changes`/`severity_values(..., age=)` convert a measured eGFR relative to the normal of the age; `healthy_gfr`, `renal_function`, `renal_function_limits` and `covariate_changes` take `age`. `pkpdsim.impairment.AGE_CLASSES` (ICH E11, ICH E7; `parameter` `AGE`; `class_changes` sets `AGE` and the `BW`/`HEIGHT` of the reference individual of the age). `populations.py`: `Population.anthropometry` per sex an `Anthropometry` (rows of ages with height and log body weight moments and their correlation `rho`, `None` independent, linear between the ages, `age_range`; `at(age)` a `CovariateDistribution`, `sample(ages, rng, truncation)` the truncated bivariate normal), read from `[<sex>.by_age]` (`ln_bw_mean`/`ln_bw_sd` or `bw_mean`/`bw_sd` of a lognormal); the NHANES population files are generated by `scripts/database/nhanes_populations.py` from the public XPT files (DEMO, BMX of 2013-2018, cache `~/.cache/pkpdsim/nhanes`; reference the survey-weighted mean of 20-49 y, kernel moments at 37 ages, pregnant women excluded), `blood_nhanes.toml` by `scripts/database/nhanes_blood.py` (CBC, BIOPRO) and the waist regression of `adipose_human.toml` printed by `scripts/database/nhanes_waist.py` (BMX, `BMXWAIST`, the adults of 20 to 79 years). `virtual.py`: `virtual_population(population, n, ages=(lo, hi) or an age class key, female=, conditions={CP_severity|HF_severity|f_renal_function: value or (lo, hi)}, seed=, truncation=)` a `VirtualPopulation` of `VirtualIndividual`s (`changes()`, `of_sex`, `values(sex)` arrays per id), `PBPKSimulationExperiment.population_dimension(population, sex)` the dimension of a scan over the individuals of a sex (units yr, kg, cm). The physiology over age needs the roadrunner with the cache of the initial value resolvers (sys-bio/roadrunner, branch `cache-initial-assignments` of the fork): without it the curves are inlined into every reader and the compile of a model takes minutes.

**Species (`pkpdsim/species.py`, `physiology.py`, #138).** `Species` (`StrEnum` `human`, `rat`, `mouse`, `pig`; imports nothing of pkpdsim, re-exported from `pkpdsim.pbpk`) is `PBPKDefinition.species` (default `HUMAN`, normalised from text, `combine` requires one): the same definition built on the physiology of a laboratory animal, the model structure and ids unchanged, the taxon of `annotation.TAXA` on the model. `physiology_table(species)` is `physiology_<species>.toml` (the structure of `physiology_human.toml`; rat and mouse male only, from Brown 1997, httk/Davies 1993 and the PK-Sim database; the domestic pig of 30 kg male and female, Lin 2020, the rest of its body assigned to muscle, skin and bone), `segments.intestine_table(species)` `intestine_<species>.toml` (a `Segment` carries its `table`; a species without `transit_time_fed` empties fed as fasted, `_fed_transit`; without an expression profile of a protein the mucosa places it by volume; the bile and `ka` default of the species). `_AnimalPhysiology(_Physiology)` overrides `covariates` (`BW` only), `references` (constants of the reference animal, `b_CO`/`b_GFR` = `ids.CO_EXPONENT`/`GFR_EXPONENT`, 0.75 when the table has none), `body` (`SV_tis = BW/BW_ref`), `mass` (every lean organ `M_ref*SV_tis`), `cardiac_output` and `gfr_base` (allometric); `anatomy` is the source key of the build (`ICRP89` for the human). An animal has no `HEIGHT`, `AGE`, `BSA`, `FFM`, no `fage_`/`fsex_` (the impairment skips `age()` and the age terms), the default population only (else `ValueError`), a sex of its table only; the nephron kidney refuses it. `reference_fractions`, `reference_tissue_volumes`, `reference_hematocrit`, `kp_values` and the Kp checks of `distribution` take `species` (`OrganContext.species`). Docs page `pbpk/species.md` (generated `species-overview`, `species-intestine`, `species-organs-<species>`), tests `tests/pbpk/test_species.py`.

**Circadian rhythms (`circadian.py`, #131).** `PBPKDefinition.circadian` (`Circadian(rhythms=None, clock=8.0)`, `None` without; `combine` requires one) adds `CLOCK` (hr, `units.HOUR` of `EXTRA_UNITS`, the clock time at time 0, an initial value; `PBPKSimulationExperiment.clock_changes(hour)`), the rule `TOD` (clock time modulo 24, `floor`) and per rhythm of `database/tables/circadian_human.toml` the constants `A_circ_<key>`, `phi_circ_<key>` (hr) and the assignment rule `fcirc_<key> = 1 + A*cos(2*pi*(CLOCK + time/60 - phi)/24 hr)` (`circadian_objects`); the factors are dynamic (`circadian_ids` joins the names of `_dynamic`, so every reader becomes a rule, also in the organs). Targets (`PHYSIOLOGY_RHYTHMS`): `GFR` in `fimp_GFR` (A 0.15, 16:30, Koopman 1996), `t_stomach_fed` on `T_st_fed` (A 0.21, 20:00, Goo 1987; an input of the intestine when `fcirc_t_stomach_fed` is in `ctx.top`; the fasted emptying has none), `albumin` in `fimp_albumin` (A 0.05, `default = false`, chosen by key), and `[protein.<o>.<protein>]` in `fimp_<o>_<protein>` (CYP3A4 of the liver A 0.071, 18:50, van Rongen 2015; `organ_activity` includes `protein_rhythms()`); `circadian_keys(definition)` the default rhythms (`Rhythm.default`) or the chosen ones, the proteins only those of a process of the definition. No rhythm (documented): small intestinal and colonic transit, liver and kidney flow, cardiac output (activity), drug renal clearance, CYP1A2. Docs page `pbpk/circadian.md` (generated `circadian-rhythms`, a morning against evening example), tests `tests/pbpk/test_circadian.py`. A periodic model has no steady state: the starts and `equilibrated` use the rhythms frozen at `CLOCK`.

**Pregnancy (`pregnancy.py`, #134).** `PBPKDefinition.pregnancy` (`Pregnancy(ga=0.0, fetus=True)`, `None` without; `combine` requires one) makes the female human build pregnant: `GA` (weeks since the last menstrual period, an initial value as the covariates, `WEEK` of `EXTRA_UNITS`), the factors `fpreg_<key>` of `database/tables/pregnancy_human.toml` (initial assignments, a piecewise linear function of `GA`, 1 at GA 0; the table is generated by `scripts/database/pregnancy_curves.py` from the fits of Dallmann 2017/2018 over the fertilization age `GA - 2` and the in vivo studies of the trimesters, `Factor`, `factors()`, `protein_factors()`) and the fetoplacental unit. The factors enter the products of the impairment (`fimp_GFR`, `fimp_albumin`, `fimp_agp`, `fimp_t_si`, `fimp_HCT`, `fimp_<o>_<protein>`) and `fimp_Vblood` through `pregnancy_physiology_factors` (`physiology_factors() | ...` in `_Top`); every organ flow carries `fpreg_Qorgans = fpreg_CO*(1 - fpreg_upla)` (in the closing-organ group as well) and the kidney `fpreg_Qki` on top, while `fimp_CO` gets the term `fpreg_Qpla = fpreg_CO*fpreg_upla` of the placenta, so `CO` stays the sum of the flows (about 2 % above the meta-analysed CO at term, the kidney keeping its measured flow). `BW` stays the weight before the pregnancy, `BW_total` adds the masses (`mass_fetus`, `mass_placenta`, `mass_amniotic_fluid`, `mass_uterus`, `mass_breasts`). The unit (`fetus=False` leaves it out): the compartments `Vpla` and `Vfet` (the masses at 1 kg/l, floored at 1e-9 l so GA 0 builds), `Qpla = CO_base*fpreg_Qpla`, per substance `Cpla_<s>`, `Cfet_<s>`, `Kp_pla_<s>` and `Kp_fet_<s>` (1), `PS_pla_<s>` (the plasma flow of the placenta by default, a choice) and the reactions `J_pla_in_<s>` (from `Car_<s>`), `J_pla_out_<s>` (to `Cve_<s>`) and the reversible `J_fet_<s>` on the unbound concentrations; `ids.species_of` counts `Cpla`/`Cfet`, so the mass balance of the tests holds. Refused (`_check`): a male build, a laboratory animal and kinetic erythrocytes. Docs page `pbpk/pregnancy.md` (generated `pregnancy-factors`, an example by trimester with the fetal to maternal ratio), tests `tests/pbpk/test_pregnancy.py`; the equations of a pregnant toy are checked by `tests/docs/test_pbpk_equations.py`.

**Adipose tissue, body composition and obesity (`adipose.py`, #136).** The adipose tissue of the library is the remainder of the body weight (`Mad = BW - Mlean - Vblood*rho_blood`), so the body composition needs no equation of its own; `PBPKDefinition.adipose` (`Adipose(effect=None, k_bw=None, obesity=True)`, `None` without; `combine` requires one) adds what the remainder does not give, a choice of the build (a build without it is unchanged). The composition, constants with initial assignments of the top model: `LBW = BW - Mad`, `FM = BW - FFM` (the fat of a body composition measurement, 2 to 4 kg below `Mad`: the lipid is 0.853 of adipose tissue and the compartment also holds the tissues of ICRP 89 the library does not map), `BFF = FM/BW`, `FMI` (Kelly 2009), `f_vat` (the visceral share, the whole-body MRI regression of Shen 2009 over the age of the sex, `visceral_share(sex)`, an `AgeCurve`; 0.112 of the men and 0.056 of the women at 38 years, trebling from 20 to 60 years, held constant below 18 and above 70, the value at 35 years in a build which is not over age), `VAT = f_vat*Mad` and `SAT = Mad - VAT` (masses of the composition, not organs: the visceral depot would drain into the portal vein, 3 to 10 % of the portal inflow, which is the natural extension and is not implemented); `pkpdsim.pbpk.physiology.body_composition(sex, bw, height, age)` (`BodyComposition`) gives the same numbers in python, so the docs table and the tests restate no equation. The waist circumference is the covariate of the visceral depot (#154, `WaistModel`, `waist_model(sex)`): `WAIST_base` is the waist of the population at the sex, body weight, height and age of the individual (the survey-weighted least squares of NHANES 2013-2018 printed by `scripts/database/nhanes_waist.py`, R2 0.92 and 0.89, the intercept shifted by -2.4 and -6.2 cm onto the mean waist of Haueise 2023, so that the prediction and the sensitivity share the measurement site, the WHO midpoint, and the population; a model choice, `[waist.<sex>]` of the table), `f_waist` the covariate (an initial value, default 1, the measured waist over that prediction, `waist_factor(sex, waist, bw, height, age)`), `WAIST = f_waist*WAIST_base`, `WHtR = WAIST/HEIGHT`, and `f_vat` is the share of the age times `exp(k_vat_waist*(WAIST - WAIST_base))` (`[visceral_waist.<sex>]`, the waist term of the multiple regression of Haueise 2023, the whole-trunk MRI of 11,141 participants of the German National Cohort: 2.958 % of the depot per cm in the men and 2.715 % in the women at a fixed age, height and weight, the exponential a model choice which agrees to first order and stays positive; the other three predictors of that regression are the covariates of the build, so only the waist is a residual and the rise of the depot with age is not counted twice). `visceral_composition(sex, bw, height, age, f_waist)` (`VisceralComposition`) gives the waist and the two depots in python. The waist moves adipose tissue between the depots and nothing else: no volume and no flow reads it. The physiology of overweight and obesity, the factors `fobes_<key>` of `database/tables/adipose_human.toml` (`ObesityFactor`, `obesity_factors(sex)`, `obesity_protein_factors(sex)`, `obesity_factor(pid)`; created by `_Impairment.obesity()` and multiplied into the products `fimp_<id>` of `combined()` with the helper `obes(key)`, as the factors of the pregnancy and the rhythms are): each is 1 up to a body mass index of 25 (`BMI_NORMAL`, so a normal weight individual is unchanged) and is the residual of the obese repository of Berton 2022 over what the scaling of the library already does, `[Berton(q, BMI)/library(q, BMI)]/[Berton(q, 25)/library(q, 25)]` at the reference height of the sex and 35 years; `fobes_CO` carries every organ flow (the closing-organ group as well), so `CO` stays the sum of its flows, the flow fractions of the adipose tissue, the muscle, the brain, the skin, the heart, the gut (`FLOW_FACTORS`, stomach, small and large intestine) and the pancreas, `fobes_albumin` and `fobes_agp` (`BINDING_FACTORS`) and the hepatic CYP3A4 (Krogstad 2021, -5 % per +10 % body weight) and CYP2E1 (Ghobadi 2011, Simcyp's bands read as a continuous rise). No factor, documented with the reason: the GFR (`GFR_ref*BSA/BSA_ref` reproduces the measured obese GFR to within 1 %), the hematocrit, the heart rate, the kidney flow fraction, the kidney, heart, skin and muscle masses, the blood volume, the liver mass (the mass of the library is already a lean liver volume, and in the library it also scales the intrinsic clearance), the hepatic artery (Simcyp and Berton disagree on the total hepatic flow, 0 against +22 % at a body mass index of 40; the library keeps the constant fraction and lands at +9 % through the falling gut fraction), the intestinal CYP3A4, OATP1B1, MRP3 and the UGTs (direction only), the tissue composition of adipose (Martin 1994 is six male cadavers; it stays Rodgers and Rowland) and the exchange model (perfusion limited; Levitt 2010 puts diffusion limitation above a log Kow of 6.2, where `SlowPool` or `KpMethod(Mode.PERMEABILITY)` serve). A change of the body weight, for a model with an effect: `f_bw` (an assignment rule of the dimensionless `Formula` of `Adipose.effect`, which unlike the other formulas of a definition may read species and observers), `BW_set = BW0*(1 + f_bw)`, `k_bw` (a half time of 35 weeks by default, the withdrawal limbs of STEP 1 and SURMOUNT-4) and `RateRule(BW, k_bw*(BW_set - BW))` in `KG_PER_MIN`, with `BW_change`, `BW_ratio` and `WAIST_change` (over `WAIST0`, a `StartValue` of the waist; the prediction falls by 0.77 cm per kg in a man against the 0.85 of the trials, so a large weight loss under-predicts the waist by about a tenth); `adipose_dynamic` makes `BW` and `f_bw` dynamic, so the whole physiology, the composition and the obesity factors become assignment rules of the weight, while `BW0` stays a constant whose initial assignment is a `StartValue` (which `_to_rules` keeps, as it keeps a `SpeciesStart`). The fat and lean limbs of a change are those of the fat-free mass of Janmahasatian 2005, 0.68 to 0.73 of the change adipose for an obese individual, which is the measured fat fraction of the DXA substudies (0.74 SURMOUNT-1, 0.709 SD 0.19 over 40 reports) and the Forbes relation; a model which clamps the lean mass gets the cardiac output, the blood volume and the GFR of a weight loss wrong. `pkpdsim.impairment.BMI_CLASSES` are the WHO classes (the class `normal` without a value is the reference individual; `class_changes` sets `BW` at the body mass index of the class and the height of the reference individual of the sex and age), the Asian action points are documented and not a classification. Validity a body mass index of 18.5 to 40. Docs page `pbpk/adipose.md` (generated `adipose-factors`, `adipose-visceral`, `adipose-waist`, `adipose-classes`, an example of a weight loss over a year), tests `tests/pbpk/test_adipose.py`.

**Build (`builder.py`).** `build(definition, output_dir)` (`builder.py`) validates it, composes the comp model (a top model with physiology, pools, substance parameters, sinks, dose states and observers, one submodel per organ, every port and replacement generated from the organ's `Boundary`), writes `<sid>.xml` and flattens it into `<sid>_flat.xml` (`flat_model`: the comp file read once, validated without the units, flattened in memory without the validation libsbml's converter repeats by default, `performValidation` off, and the flat model validated with strict units, since libsbml checks the units of a comp document only in its top model; a build of `definition()` 3.5 s before, 1.4 s after; the data files are parsed once per process, `data._parsed`, `data.load` returns a copy); `PBPKDefinition.combine` couples definitions into one physiology. `build` writes SBML L3V2 (the level of the models of pkdb_models) and every initial assignment carries a name and an SBO term (`annotation.initial_assignment`), so a build logs no sbmlutils warning (`test_build_logs_no_sbmlutils_warning`). The load time is guarded twice: `test_rules_read_a_state` (no assignment rule of the flat model depends on constants only, the cause of the 28.8 s compile) in every run, and `test_load_time` (under 10 s CPU), which skips itself inside pytest-xdist workers and runs as the second command of tox (`pytest -n 0 tests/pbpk/test_builder.py::test_load_time`), so continuous integration measures it serially.

**The parts.** `physiology.py` (ICRP 89 male/female references of `database/tables/physiology_human.toml`, Huisinga 2012 scaling from `BW`/`HEIGHT` relative to the reference individual of `AGE` (`age.py`), everything that depends on parameters only an initial assignment, sex a build choice; `reference_individual(sex, age=35)` gives the body weight, height, healthy GFR (`gfr` in l/min, `GFR_base` of the default individual as `healthy_gfr` gives it, 0.125 and 0.109, the GFR which a PD component that filters the water reads), organ, tissue, cell (`intracellular_volumes`) and lumen (`lumen_volumes`, by segment token) volumes of the reference individual, exported from `pkpdsim.pbpk`, so that no model copies 73 kg, the liver of 2.33 l, its cells of 1.3859 l or the GFR of 0.125 l/min), `populations.py` (reference individuals and covariate distributions, never `distrib`), `organ.py`/`organ_builder.py`/`parts.py`/`distribution.py`/`placement.py` (the standard organ with collapsing, the coefficients, the placement of the chemistry, inhibition rate laws and turnover states; every metabolism and transport of the kidney runs with the tubular capacity `1 - f_tubular + f_tubular*f_renal_function`, `KI__f_tubular` 1, the intact nephron hypothesis; the glomerulus filters the afferent plasma, `fu*GFR*(1 - f_reabsorbed)*Car`, and the outflow of every kidney form lacks the filtrate, `StandardBuilder._filtrate` (the reabsorbed fraction `StandardBuilder._reabsorbed`, constant or saturable), so the filtration clearance is `fu*GFR*(1 - f_reabsorbed)` and a secretion acts on the plasma flow past it; the outflow is valid while `fu*GFR*(1 - f_reabsorbed) < Qki*(1 - HCT)` (`Qki*BP` with erythrocytes in equilibrium, `fu*GFR` with a saturable reabsorption), which the experiments check (`kidney_outflow`); the kidney exposes the net urinary excretion rate `Rurine_<s>`, the fluxes into the urine less a custom reaction from the urine), `variants/` (zonated liver, nephron kidney, segmented intestine (default) and single lumen, the registry), `impairment.py` (`CP_severity`, the `f_renal_function`/`eGFR` stages and `HF_severity` as factor vectors of `database/tables/impairment.toml`, the factors of cirrhosis on the proteins of the liver the consensus of the studies of the database (`ImpairmentFactors`), the switch `CKD_hepatic` for the hepatic proteins in CKD; the binding in CKD per protein: albumin and AGP follow their profile of `[ckd.binding]` (`fimp_albumin_ckd`, `fimp_agp_ckd`, created only when a substance binds the protein with the CKD factor), an unknown protein the binding capacity `plasma_protein`, `Binding(ckd=False)` only for a fu which is no binding measurement or whose binding is measured unchanged in renal impairment), `observers.py` (pool, free, blood, hepatic vein, arm vein `Carm_<s>`, the default fit target; the observers of a definition, `OrganOutput`, the intestine readouts), `sinks.py`, `kp.py` (Rodgers & Rowland), `annotation.py` (SBO and PBPKO on every element), `ids.py` (the one id scheme; every public name is in `__all__` and has a row in the id tables of `docs/pbpk/index.md`, both tested), `values.py`/`data.py` (`Value` with a `source` key of `database/sources.toml`, `ModelChoice` where no publication gives a number).

**Experiments (`experiment.py`).** `PBPKSimulationExperiment`, the base of the experiments of a library model: `healthy_gfr(model_path, *, bw, height, hf_severity, cp_severity)` reads `GFR_base` and returns it as a quantity in ml/min (`renal_function` a dimensionless quantity, both for the changes of a simulation; `REFERENCE_GFR` 101 ml/min, imported from `pkpdsim.pbpk`) (it carries the factors of heart failure and of the hepatorenal reduction of cirrhosis) from a one-minute sbmlsim simulation with the covariates in `preinit_changes`, cached per model file, its modification time and the individual; the classmethods `healthy_gfr`, `renal_function(clearance, ...)`, `renal_function_limits` and `renal_map_of` on the build of the sex of the subjects (`subjects_sex(sex, simulation)`: `simulation=` the key of a simulation, whose sex `sex_of`, a classmethod, resolves from `simulation_sexes`, e.g. the group of women of a study of men; `sex=` a sex; with neither the class attribute `sex`, refused in an experiment whose `simulation_sexes` names another sex, since its subjects have no one sex and the male GFR for a woman would pass silently), `renal_map_of` the renal classes of another sex (`ScaledRenalMap.of_sex`, a map at the healthy GFR of that build, cached per bound map; a map with its own GFR has no other sex, a plain mapping is of every sex); `renal_map` a `ScaledRenalMap` of `RENAL_FUNCTION_GFR`, the classes keep their GFR in ml/min, `preset*101/healthy GFR`, lazy and bound per class as a non-data descriptor (a plain dict of the bound maps, left out when pickled), so importing experiments loads no model and a subclass with another model or another `sex` gets its own GFR (the map of a class is bound to the GFR of its `sex`); `cirrhosis_map` `CHILD_PUGH`, `cardiac_map` `HEART_FAILURE`; `equilibrated` (see the equilibration below); `molecular_weights(definition)` (the `Mr_<s>` of the substances and the parameters `Mr_<x>` of a definition as a read-only mapping of quantities in g/mole, instead of the `MolecularWeights` copied into a model); `PBPKDefinition.parameter_value(sid)` is the number of a constant `ModelParameter` (a constant of a formulation or a dose, which the experiments read instead of a copy); its names are exported from `pkpdsim.pbpk` lazily by a module `__getattr__` (and listed by `__dir__`), so `import pkpdsim.pbpk` imports no sbmlsim, `test_importing_the_library_imports_no_simulator`.

**Kidney validity (`kidney_validity.py`).** The validity of the kidney outflow lives in `kidney_validity.py` (exported lazily as well): `kidney_outflow(model_path, changes)` gives the filtered plasma water (`KidneyOutflow` with `substance=None`, `formula` `GFR` against `Qki*(1 - HCT)`, since the glomeruli filter the water whatever the substances, the tighter bound since `fu <= 1` and `BP >= 1 - HCT`; hctz 6.08 instead of 18.1) and per filtered substance a `KidneyOutflow` (GFR, net filtered plasma flow, plasma flow in ml/min, the `formula` of the net flow, `fu*GFR` with a threshold reabsorption) from the one-minute simulation of `individual_values` (cached per model file, modification time and the changes of the ids the flows depend on, `FlatModel.inputs` from the initial assignments and rules of the flat model, so a dose makes no individual; the model `loaded_model` of `flat_model.py`, compiled once per process at `O0` and shared with the equilibration and `healthy_gfr`; a model without a filtered substance, or without the GFR, `Qki` and `HCT`, is not simulated and has no bound); `simulated_individuals(simulation, model_path)` gives the individuals of a simulation or scan (preinit changes, of a presimulation as well, every simulation of a dimension of simulations, every point of the value dimensions without `at`; not a change during a simulation, also of a `dynamic` id at its start, not a timed dimension, not a scan over models, not the uncertainty draws, all listed in the docs); `PBPKSimulationExperiment.initialize` refuses an individual outside of it, each task on the model it runs (`models()`, `_model_path`), with a message naming experiment, simulation, GFR and plasma flow, `renal_function` refuses a renal function beyond it (naming GFR and plasma flow) or outside a model's `renal_function_range` (`None` by default, only narrows), `renal_function_limits(...)` the derived range (0 to the smallest plasma flow over filtered flow at renal function 1, the water included), `test_kidney_validity.py`.

**Equilibration (`equilibration.py`, `flat_model.py`).** `PBPKSimulationExperiment.equilibrated(simulation_or_scan, resets=, model=, absolute_tolerance=1e-12, relative_tolerance=1e-10, max_time=1e14)` starts a simulation from the drug-free steady state of its condition: the presimulation `SteadyState` of sbmlsim with the changes before the initialization, chosen over a hidden pre-run from a negative start, which leaves a homeostasis of 7 days 1.8 % off after 28 days (`tests/pbpk/test_equilibration.py`); the doses before the initialization (`PODOSE_`/`IVDOSE_`/`SOLDOSE_`/`Ri_`, `DOSE_PREFIXES`) move to a change at the start; the sinks `Aurine_`/`Afeces_`, the intestine readouts which nothing drains (`Aabs_<s>` and `Aapp_<s>`, `GU__Aabs_<s>` and `GU__Aapp_<s>` with the readout `Fa_<s>` alone, since an endogenous substance which reaches the lumen at its baseline is absorbed again) and `resets` are set back at the start, a key of `resets` converted as the zeros (`[sid]` for a concentration species, the quantity of a PEtab condition); it refuses a dose before the initialization and at the start, a dimension over a dose the simulation does not give, a second presimulation, and a state which grows without an outflow and is not set back (`accumulating_states`/`growing_states`, checked once per model file); the `<id>_0` of a delta is set to its value after the equilibration; the species of the drugs are set to exactly 0 at the start, `drug_free_zeros(model_path, set_ids)`, since the residue of the presimulation (1.5e-29 mM in hctz) let pre-dose zero points into the fold metrics: the species of the substances without `baseline_<s>` (by `Mr_<s>`) which start at 0 and which no reaction feeds from a species with a start (a fed reactant, a fed product of a reversible reaction, a source which reads no dose id), minus what the condition, a dimension or a change at the start sets; the direction of a reaction for the zeros is its `reversible` flag, so a `CustomReaction` which can run backwards is written `A <-> B`; no steady state by `max_time` is sbmlsim's `SteadyStateError`; overloads for `Simulation`, `Scan` and the union, so a caller with both needs no `match`. The method calls `equilibrated_simulation(simulation, model_path, resets=, ...)` with the file of its loaded model (an error is noted with the experiment): the one equilibration, also outside an experiment, exported lazily from `pkpdsim.pbpk`; the names (`EQUILIBRATION_TOLERANCES`, `DOSE_PREFIXES`, `accumulating_states`, `growing_states`, `drug_free_zeros`, `equilibrated_simulation`, `Equilibration`) have the one import path `pkpdsim.pbpk.equilibration`. `Equilibration(resets=...)` is the function with which a sensitivity group equilibrates its scans (`SensitivityGroup(prepare=Equilibration(...))`), so the generic analyses import no module of the library. `flat_model.py` is the one reader of a built model for the equilibration and the kidney validity: `flat_model(path)` parses a flat file once per process (a `FlatModel` cached by path and modification time: species, parameters, accumulating states, the drug-free candidates and started species, concentrations, reactions, filtered substances, the flows of the kidney and their inputs), `loaded_model(path)` compiles it once at `O0` with the selections of both (`FlatModel.selections`), and `math_names` is the one walker of the names of a formula.
