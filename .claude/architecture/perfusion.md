# Machine perfusion

Part of the architecture notes of pkpdsim, see [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md).

**Machine perfusion (`perfusion/`).** The library `pkpdsim.perfusion` (pkpdsim#146, docs page `perfusion.md`, API `api/perfusion.md`) builds the SBML of an ex vivo machine perfusion, a circuit with one mounted organ, from a frozen `PerfusionDefinition` (`definition.py`: `sid`, `name`, `species`, the organ, `Circuit` (reservoir, tubing in and out in ml, the pump flow in ml/min, `oxygenator_k`, `oxygenator_k_co2`, `pco2_gas`), `Perfusate` (glucose, lactate, HCO3, K in mM, `o2_target_kpa`, `ph` (within `PH_RANGE` 6 to 8), `carrier` `none` (`rbc` declared and refused by `validate` until a sourced hemoglobin binding exists), `hct`, `tracers`: inert SIds of the perfusate for mass balances and filtration markers, fresh at 0), `temperature` (degC), `core` (`core.CoreParameters`), `creators`, `notes` (markdown appended to the description of the circuit in the notes of the model, the terms of use of a model package); `validate()` the problems, `parameter_value(sid)` a constant of the composed model); the numbers are `Value`s in the units of an experimenter (`units.PU`: ml, ml/min, g, kPa, degC as a kelvin step without offset, µl, µkat/l), the defaults (`Circuit.default`, `Perfusate.default`, `LiverOrgan.default`, `KidneyOrgan.default`, `CoreParameters.default(section)`) the table `database/tables/perfusion.toml` read by `definition.table_value` (sections `oxygen`, `enzymes`, `temperature`, `perfusate`, `circuit`, `acid_base`, `liver_rat`, `kidney_rat`, `core_<organ>` and `core_<organ>_start`, most values `ModelChoice` of the ported model, the enzyme activities placeholders of 1; the vocabulary of the fit mappings `Tissue.PERFUSATE`, `Route.EX_VIVO`, `Dosing.PERFUSION` and the sampling sites `SamplingSite.RESERVOIR` (the default of the perfusate), `INFLOW`, `OUTFLOW` of `metadata.py`; the table is listed in `docs/database.md`, and the citation of `ModelChoice` names both libraries, the PBPK and the machine perfusion library, the references of `tests/pbpk/data/` rebuilt with that text only); the organ is `LiverOrgan(mass, zones=1, profiles={})` (bile, releases ALT, AST, LDH, K), `KidneyOrgan` (filtration and urine, `gfr`, `reabsorption`, `ff_max`; LDH, K) or `GenericOrgan(key, mass)` (no excretion, the fractions of the organ `key` of the physiology table of the species; LDH, K), each a chain of `zones` in series (`_Zonated`, the organ token `li`, `ki` or the key). `ids.py` is the one id scheme: a quantity at a site `<q>_<site>` (sites `res`, `in`, the zones `z<k>`, `out`, the means `pp`/`pc`, the cells `<zone>_cell`, the dead cells `<zone>_dead`, the urine `urine`), `V_<site>`, `Vvas_<z>`, `Vcell_<z>`, `<name>_<z>` for the reactions of the core, `FLOW_<q>_<site>_<next>`, `<q>_fresh`, `pO2_`/`pCO2_`/`pH_<site>`, `<e>_act_<site>`, `f_alive_<z>`, `v_nec_<z>`, `p_<process>_<z>`, `M_`/`rho_`/`V_`/`f_vas_`/`f_cell_<o>`, `GFR_`/`GFR_max_`/`FF_max_`/`gfr_`/`f_reab_<o>`, `Q_pump`, `Q_<site>`, `Q_urine`, `T`, `f_T`, `bile` (an assignment rule of the states `Vbile_<z>`), `bile_rate`, `bile_per_mass` (µl/g), `urine`, `urine_rate`, `VO2`, the shared starts of the cells `<q>_start` (`ids.start`); `PERFUSATE`, `CELL`, `RELEASABLE` (a subset per organ, `perfusate_quantities`/`cell_quantities` drop the enzymes an organ does not release), `FILTERED`. `core.py` is the cellular core of a zone (`zone_objects(zone, cells, vascular, params, profile=, composition=, bile=, released=)`, `CoreObjects`, the constants shared by the zones as ids of the whole model, `core_parameters(params, released=, bile=)` (no bile constants for an organ without bile, no content of an enzyme the organ does not release, and the starts of the cells as the shared constants `<q>_start` of `start_quantities`, which every zone reads by an initial assignment, so a covariate on the starting ATP is one change of `atp_start`), `CoreParameters.problems` (the missing contents and bile constants, a constant or start in another unit than `_SPECS` (name, SBO term and unit) or mM, a quantity of the cells without a start and a start of no quantity, so `validate` refuses what `compose` would fail on), `buffer_objects`/`buffer_parameters`, `perfusate_objects`, `quantity_name`, `PROCESSES`), ported from the liver model of `pkdb_models.misc.machine_perfusion` (PVL) with its TODO fixed and every rate law with its reason in the module docstring: glucose and oxygen import, lactate export with a proton (`LACEX_h_cell` the constant cytosolic protons), glycogenolysis, glycolysis carbon balanced and inhibited by `1/(1 + (|ATP|/Ki)^n)` (defined at ATP 0, no rate divides by a species which can reach 0), LDH reversible, TCA (CO2 into the perfusate), OXPHOS, ATP use, ROS at low oxygen, glutathione peroxidase and reductase with NADPH of the pentose phosphate pathway, necrosis `v_nec = NEC_k*f_T*(1 - (1 - s_atp)*(1 - s_h))` by energy failure or acidosis of the perfusate (the acidosis drives the damage and does not inhibit the metabolism), the dying cells move enzymes and K into the dead cells which release them first order (not scaled by `f_T`), bile volume by ATP in the liver, the bicarbonate buffer `H+ + HCO3- <-> CO2` by mass action at every site and the pH of the free protons; every biological rate is `f_T*f_alive*Vcell` times a rate per litre of cells, a zonal profile the factor `p_<process>_<z> = n*p/sum(p)` (`builder.zone_factors`), a Hill term reads `abs`. `circuit.py`: the sites well stirred, every perfusate quantity flows at `Q_pump*<q>_<site>` (amount conserved, a flow of 0 defined), the oxygenator `OXY_in` towards `alpha_o2*pO2_target` and `OXY_co2_in` towards `alpha_co2*pCO2_gas` (without a gas pCO2 the initial assignment `pCO2_gas = co2_fresh/alpha_co2`, the number `fresh_co2_pressure`, so a changed fresh bicarbonate keeps the fresh perfusate at its pH), the temperature `f_T` (Arrhenius of `Ea`, `R_gas`, `T_ref`) and `alpha_o2` initial assignments of `T` (a change of `T` during a timecourse is not evaluated again), `alpha_co2` held at 37 C, the fresh perfusate `<q>_fresh` the start of every site (`h_fresh` from `pH_fresh`, `co2_fresh` the buffer equilibrium, `o2_fresh` the target), `quantities`, `quantity_label`. `builder.py`: `build(definition, output_dir)` validates, `compose`s and writes `<sid>.xml` (L3V2, no comp, every element named and with SBO, validated with units; `model_ids`, `tracer_ids`); the volumes of the zones `f_vas_<o>*V_<o>/n` and `f_cell_<o>*V_<o>/n` initial assignments of the mass; the single portal inflow of the rat liver (no hepatic artery split); the kidney filters `GFR_ki = min(GFR_max_ki, FF_max_ki*Q_pump)` (a piecewise rule, `GFR_max_ki = gfr_ki*M_ki`, `FF_max_ki` 0.5 a `ModelChoice`), shared by the zones by volume, `FILT_<q>_<z>` the net filtration `(1 - f_reab_ki)*GFR/n*C` (a tracer `GFR/n*C`, the enzymes not filtered), `Q_urine`, the flow out of zone `k` `Q_pump - k/n*Q_urine`, the reservoir volume a rate rule `-Q_urine`, `V_urine` gains it. `observers.py`: `pO2_`, `pCO2_`, `pH_`, `<e>_act_<site>` (`<e>*a_<e>`, µkat/l), the periportal and pericentral means of the first and last third of the zones (`third_weights`), `bile`/`bile_rate`, `bile_per_mass` (`bile/(1000 g/kg * M_<o>)`, µl/g, the unit of the data, `PU.ul_per_g`, `PU.g_per_kg`), `urine`/`urine_rate`, `VO2`. `figure.py` the schematic B (`circuit_figure`, `circuit_svg`, `circuit_cards`, `process_groups`, a long name wraps in the legend) and `anatomy.py` the anatomical panel A, the organ in its circuit (`circuit_anatomy_figure`, the human organ shapes of the anatomy resources for every species), `interactive_circuit(definition, path, figure="anatomy"|"schematic")` their interactive pages (`html_page(..., credit=)` of `pbpk/visual/interactive.py`, the credit `figure_credit(drawn, library=, url=)`: `CREDIT` "anatomy after the anatomograms" for the figures A, `ICON_CREDIT` "organ icons after the anatomograms" for the schematics B, whose icons are the anatomogram shapes, CC BY 4.0; those of `pbpk/visual/interactive.py` name the PBPK library, those of `perfusion/figure.py` the machine perfusion library and its page `LIBRARY`, `.../perfusion/`); the escaping of a card is once (`alt_&lt;site&gt;`); `archive.py` `create_archive(definition, model_output_dir, *, model, copyright, readme=None)` builds and writes the figures, ODE markdown, antimony, licenses and the COMBINE archive with `sbmlsim.release.archive.write_archive`. `experiment.py`: `PerfusionSimulationExperiment(PKPDSimulationExperiment)` with the class attribute `definition` (a class with a definition gets `labels`/`units` from `observable_labels`/`observable_units` unless it sets them, keyed by the selections of sbmlsim: a species by its concentration `[<q>_<site>]`, `[<q>_<z>_cell]` (its plain id is its amount), the urine by its amount `<q>_urine` (mmole; `V_urine` starts at 0, so its concentration is 0/0 at the start), `bile_per_mass` in µl/g, an assignment rule by its id, the means `<q>_pp`, `pO2_<site>`, `VO2`; `TypeError` without a definition), `perfusion_changes(flow, mass, temperature=None)` the preinit changes `Q_pump` (l/min), `M_<o>` (kg) and `T` as its number in degC (sbmlsim converts a quantity of a temperature to kelvin, `Q(37, "degC")` to 310.15, pinned in a test; `initialize` refuses a simulation which sets `T` to a quantity, `temperature_quantities`: before the initialization, the presimulation, a change, a dimension of a scan), `medium_changes(times, fraction, time_unit="min")` one `Change` per time with `medium_change(definition, fraction)`, every perfusate species of the reservoir (the tracers included) `"[X_res]" -> "(1 - f)*[X_res] + f*X_fresh"`, `f` in (0, 1]; the change keeps the reservoir volume (a kidney keeps the volume it has at the change, the urine is not made up); sbmlsim records an output time at a change twice, before then after, and a fit compares a data point at the change with the state before (`data_at_change`), pinned in `tests/perfusion/test_experiment.py`. The names are lazy exports of `pkpdsim.perfusion` (PEP 562). `scripts/visual/docs_figures.py` writes the figures A and B of every fence of `docs/pbpk/*.md` and `docs/perfusion.md` (`PAGES`) creating a `PBPKDefinition` or a `PerfusionDefinition` into the folder of the page (`models_folder`, `FOLDERS`: `docs/pbpk/img/figures/models/` for the PBPK pages, `docs/img/figures/models/` for the perfusion page; `write_pages(docs)` takes the docs or a folder in place of them), the frame paths relative to the page; `tests/docs/test_perfusion_equations.py` checks the ` ```equations ` fences of `docs/perfusion.md` (placeholders `<q>`, `<z>`, `<o>`) against a liver and a kidney of two zones with the parser of `test_pbpk_equations.py`. The limitations of the first version are a list of `docs/perfusion.md` (the rbc carrier, the single portal inflow, `alpha_co2` at 37 C, the human organ shapes, `FF_max_ki`, the placeholders, the living-cell semantics of the dead cells, which keep their metabolites with time constants growing as `1/f_alive`, and the pole of `s/(km + s)` at `s = -km`). Tests in `tests/perfusion/` (the balance of K and the enzymes over the whole circuit of a liver and a kidney with medium changes in `test_experiment.py`; `conftest.py`: `liver_definition`, `kidney_definition`, `generic_definition`, `zone_model`, `load_circuit`, compiled at `O0`).
