# The PBPK definition

Part of the architecture notes of pkpdsim, see [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md).

**`pbpk/`.** The PBPK model library `pkpdsim.pbpk`, from which the PBPK models of pkdb_models are built (the pilots hctz, apixaban and glimepiride and the first group torasemide, inulin, gdeobdtpa and creatinine; docs section `docs/pbpk/`, the modellers' guide, `docs/pbpk/migration.md` the procedure of a migration). A model package writes one `PBPKDefinition` (`definition.py`) with the fields of the paragraphs below; `create_archive` is the whole factory of a model and `build` its build. The organ-keyed fields and the sex may be given as text (`"adipose"`, `"female"`) and are normalised to `Organ`/`Sex` in the dataclasses.

**Substances (`substance.py`, `proteins.py`).** A `Substance` of the definition has the mechanism flags `Binding`, `Erythrocytes`, `Kp`/`KpMethod` (`Mode.TISSUE` default, `PERFUSION`, `PERMEABILITY`, `ftissue`, `ps`; `Kp.value` may be `None` when the overrides cover every built organ the substance enters; `Kp(interstitial=scale)`, the extracellular distribution, gives every organ without an override `Kp_<o>_<s> = fKp_<s> * share`, the interstitial share `f_int*K_int_pls(fu)/(1 - f_vas)` of the reference individual of the sex of the build (a female build the female share), at the fu of the definition (`distribution.interstitial_share(organ, fu, sex)`, the sex without a default, so a female build never reads the male share, `f_int/(1 - f_vas)` is `Fint` of Schmitt 2008 per tissue, so the shares of the sexes differ only by the rounding of the tabulated `f_int`, below 5e-4 relative, exported from `pkpdsim.pbpk`, the lower bound `check_kp(substance, organ, sex)` sets in a full-form organ for the sex of the build, `OrganContext.sex`; the build warns, `KCellFloorWarning` of `warn_kp_floor` (exported from `pkpdsim.pbpk`), where the share at the fu of a CKD stage of the binding profile, `kp_floor_stages` with `impairment.ckd_unbound_fractions`, exceeds the Kp and `K_cell_pls` sits at `K_CELL_FLOOR`: the metabolite M1 of torasemide in the kidney at the end stage, a warning and no refusal; a model accepts it per organ with its reason, `ckd_floor={organ: reason}` of `Kp`/`KpMethod` (`_Exchange`), which silences the warning of that organ and writes the reason and the stages into the notes of its `K_cell_pls_<s>`, `kp_floor_notes`, an empty reason refused by `validate`; `check_kp` and the warning read the Kp of the organ from one function, `_full_form_kp`), the scale a number, a fitted `Value` or a `Formula`, no `fKp_<s>` without it, a `value` with `interstitial` refused by `validate`, `test_kp_interstitial.py` against the stored SBML), `Excretion` (`urine`, the constant `fraction_reabsorbed` `f_reabsorbed_<s>`, or `reabsorption=ThresholdReabsorption(threshold, n)`, the reabsorption of the filtered load up to a renal threshold, the reabsorbed fraction `1/(1 + (fu*C/reab_threshold_<s>)^reab_n_<s>)^(1/reab_n_<s>)` of the unbound concentration of the filtrate in place of `f_reabsorbed_<s>` (then not created), at `Car` in the filtration and at `Cout` in the outflow, at most 1 so it never exceeds the filtration and approaches the transport maximum `GFR*threshold`; the nephron kidney returns the same fraction of `Car` of the flow out of the collecting duct up to the filtered load, `min(GFR*f_tub_cd*Ctub_cd, fu*GFR*Car)*f(Car)`, so a secreted drug is not reabsorbed beyond the filtration; `|C|` in the power keeps it defined for an undershoot below 0; `validate` refuses it without urine, with a `fraction_reabsorbed` other than 0 and with constants not positive or not in mM and dimensionless, `_check_reabsorption`; without it the SBML is unchanged (the nephron kidney against the stored `nephron_reference` of 311895c as well, `nephron_reference_definition` of the conftest), `test_reabsorption.py`, which also checks the sweep of plasma concentrations, the mass balance and the steady state), `Inhibition`, `Induction`, `Stabilisation` (`substance.py`, `proteins.py`; the turnover state `E_<protein>` of `organ_builder.py`, `dE/dt = kdeg*(1 + sum Emax*I/(EC50 + I)) - kdeg*E/(1 + sum I/Kstab) - sum kinact*I/(KI + I)*E`, the constants `Kstab_<protein>_<s>` of a stabilisation, `kdeg_<protein>` of the organ (`[turnover]` of `database/tables/proteins.toml`) or of the protein in every organ, `PBPKDefinition.turnover` (1/hr, or a fitted `Value` in 1/min), refused by `validate` for a protein without a turnover state; `stationary.py` refuses a substance with a generated steady state which changes a turnover state; without either the SBML is unchanged, `test_a_definition_without_turnover_or_stabilisation_is_unchanged` against the stored `turnover_reference`) and the endogenous `baseline` (mM, a number, `Value` or `Formula`, the parameter `baseline_<s>`: every species starts at the equilibrium of its exchange with plasma, `Kbc`/`Kp`/`K_int_pls`/`K_cell_pls`/`K_or`/`K_mu` times the baseline, as initial assignments marked `SpeciesStart`, built after the other elements of an organ (`_defer_start`) and kept as initial assignments under a dynamic parameter; a `Formula` may read top parameters and definition observers, never species, reactions, library observers, organ outputs or what a PD output replaces; without a baseline the SBML is that of f008d56, `test_the_sbml_without_baseline_is_that_of_f008d56` against `tests/pbpk/data/*.xml.gz` with `assert_reference_sbml` and `reference_definition` of `tests/pbpk/conftest.py`).

**Chemistry (`chemistry.py`).** The chemistry of the definition is `Metabolism`, `Transport`, `CustomReaction` with `FirstOrder`/`MM`/`Hill` (`chemistry.py`; the `vmax` and by default the `k` of `FirstOrder` are per litre of organ, `FirstOrder(k, per_volume=False)` a clearance of the whole organ; `RateOf(process, factor)` for a `vmax` or `k` is a ratio of the same constant of another metabolism or transport of the organ, the initial assignment `<p>_Vmax = <p>_Vmax_ratio * <other>_Vmax` over local ids (`OrganFormulaParameter`, assigned in `OrganBuilder.model_definition`), the ratio `ids.rate_ratio` with its provenance, refused by `validate` for a missing process, a custom reaction, another organ, another constant or `per_volume`, a chain of ratios and a factor which is not positive and dimensionless; `Metabolism.products` numbers summing to 1 are the stoichiometry of one reaction (the SBML of 0c538ca, `test_the_chemistry_without_the_options_is_that_of_0c538ca` against `tests/pbpk/data/chemistry_reference*.xml.gz`), a `Value` (fixed or fitted, a constant which a fit or experiment may change, so it needs exactly one remainder `REST`, `Rest`) makes every fraction the constant `f_<p>_<s>` of the organ (`ids.product_fraction`, `REST` the initial assignment `1 - ...`), the reaction consumes the substrate and `<p>_to_<s>` forms each product at `f_<p>_<s> * <p>` (`ids.product_reaction`, `placement.product_reaction`, also in the zones and the mucosa); `validate` refuses fractions whose upper ends (the value and the support of the distribution of the draws of a fitted value, `_fraction_range`) sum above 1, a range below 0, a remainder always 0, two remainders, a `Value` without remainder and a `Formula` (`chemistry.check_products`, with `check_rate_of`, `check_kinetics`, `check_custom_parameters`, `fraction_range` next to the processes; the value checks `number`, `check_positive`, `check_unit`, `unit_text` and `SID` in `values.py`); the protein of a metabolism scales all its products, so branches of different enzymes stay separate metabolisms, tied by `RateOf` where only the ratio is known (`test_branches_of_different_enzymes_stay_separate_metabolisms`); `CustomReaction.parameters` are `ModelParameter`s (a number, a fitted `Value` with its `distrib` and provenance, a `Formula` over top ids read through inputs and over the constants of its organ which are no top ids, another custom parameter of the organ or `Vti`, read by `OrganBuilder._local_reads`; a cycle, a species or an unknown id refused) or sbmlutils `Parameter`s, local ids of the organ, `<ORGAN>__<id>` flat, defined once per organ (`check_custom_parameters`), no id of another element of the organ (`OrganBuilder._custom_clashes`), metaid `meta_<ORGAN>__<id>`, for a sbmlutils `Parameter` only when another organ uses its id), defined once per organ.

**Variants, routes and the lumen (`routes.py`, `variants/lumen_options.py`, `regional.py`).** The definition has its `Variants` and `Route`s (`routes.py`; the depot routes `Route.im`/`Route.sc`/`Route.rectal` (`Depot(ka, fraction, portal, application)` of a model, else the defaults of `database/tables/routes.toml`, the rates 1/hr not verified, the rectal portal fraction 0.45 of de Boer 1979) apply `IMDOSE_`/`SCDOSE_`/`RECDOSE_<s>` with the half-life `ti_<k>_<s>` into the depot `Aim_`/`Asc_`/`Arec_<s>` (a species of the top model in `Vim`/`Vsc`/`Vrec`, `depot_site` once per kind), absorbed with `ka_<k>_<s>`: `f_<k>_<s>` into `Cve_<s>` (rectal: `f_rec_portal_<s>` of it into `Cpo_<s>`), the rest into `Aloss_im_`/`Aloss_sc_<s>` or `Afeces_<s>`, `tests/pbpk/test_routes.py`; `FirstOrderDissolution(k, fed=, release=, enteric=)` with the factors `f_dis_fed_<s>` (read with `FED`) and `f_dis_release_<s>` of a modified release, `DoseDependentDissolution(k, dose50, fed=, enteric=)` with `f_dis_fed_<s>` on its saturating rate, `enteric=True` an enteric-coated tablet without the dissolution `J_po_<s>` in the stomach (it empties undissolved with `T_st_eff`'s formula and dissolves from the duodenum on, the saturation amount `D` of a dose-dependent dissolution without the coated tablet in the stomach; the single lumen refuses it naming the substance), `tests/pbpk/test_intestine.py`, the defaults unchanged in `test_routes.py`; the absorption `ka`/`fabs` of the intestine one value or one per substance, `PerSubstance`, kept as the read-only `FrozenMap` of `values.py`; `SegmentedIntestine(regional=RegionalAbsorption({seg: factor}))` the regional absorption profile (`variants/lumen_options.py`, with the conversions), one or one per substance, a factor `f_ka_<seg>_<s>` of `ka*f_area` per absorbing segment or one shared factor of the group `colon` (caecum to rectum, the parameter `f_ka_colon_<s>`, of which `f_ka_colon` is the shorthand: the same model; its notes name the source of a sourced `Value`, else that a model fits it), no generic default (Tannergren 2009: the colonic absorption depends on the permeability), two factors of one segment refused; `regional.py` derives a profile with the model from a release study: `release_auc_ratios` the AUC of a solution released in a segment over the oral solution, `derive_regional_absorption` the factors of the reported ratios, one per release segment, `regional_key` the segment or the group `colon`, brentq in turns, iterated with the refit by the model; `changes` the condition of the study as initial values, taken back with a reset of all values at the end; the segmented intestine gives the transit time of the stomach of the condition, `(T_st + FED*(T_st_fed - T_st))*fimp_t_stomach`, to the top model as the constant output `T_st_eff` (`ids.T_STOMACH`), which a formula of the top model reads (a conversion in the stomach); `SegmentedIntestine(conversions=[LumenConversion(substrate, product, k, segments)])` a first order conversion in the lumen segments, `kconv_<s>_<m>` (1/hr) of the top model and `J_conv_<seg>_<s>_<m>`, one mole per mole, the product gets lumen species, a substrate which never reaches the lumen refused; `SingleLumen` refuses both with a message; without them the SBML is unchanged, `test_lumen.py`).

(constant `OrganOutput`s), a saturable systemic organ the root `Css_<o>_<s>` which that organ reads), explicit `starts` override; the formulas read each deep quantity least (roadrunner before the cache of its initial value resolvers inlined every initial assignment a start reads, `docs/pbpk/substances.md`); `build` warns (`stationary.check_built`, `SteadyStateWarning`) for a pinned substance whose venous pool moves at the start (`IMBALANCE_LIMIT`, the imbalance in mmole/min, the model loaded once at O0 inside the function); a linear rate law is a positive clearance (a negation is refused); `tests/pbpk/test_stationary.py` checks every variant to 1e-12 over a day (densely in the first hour) and the endogenous starts without declarations against `endogenous_reference_definition` built at 87a5e79; `steady_state.py` gives the steady state of the equations of the library as formulas for the starts (`plasma_flow`, `tissue_volume`, `tissue_exchange`, `organ_plasma`, `organ_tissue`, `renal_filtration` (and `renal_clearance`, with `arterial=` the net filtrate of a saturable reabsorption at the arterial plasma, the baseline by default), `kidney_plasma` (the arterial plasma for a saturable reabsorption without another process of the kidney, `NotImplementedError` with one or at an elimination) with a first order `clearance` and a saturable `secretion=Saturable(vmax, km)` (the positive root of the quadratic) at a given `arterial` plasma or a given renal elimination `eliminated`, `kidney_arterial` (with `eliminated` `(E + R*C)/F`, the kidney plasma read once: every start reads the baseline, and roadrunner compiles a copy of the expanded formula into each), `renal_clearance`, `liver_plasma`, `arm_vein(substance, preset, outflows)`;
