# The model and run layer

Part of the architecture notes of pkpdsim, see [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md).

**`model.py`.** `PKPDModel` is a `sbmlsim.project.Project` (a frozen pydantic model, `extra="forbid"`) and describes a model once, in the `__init__.py` of its package. The keywords of the model packages of pkdb_models are mapped onto the fields of the project by a before validator, only for keyword input (`PKPDModel(**data)` takes any keyword, so ty accepts the old keywords): `path` is `root` (the property `path`), `model_path` and `female_model_path` are the `variants` `male` and `female` (the values of `pkpdsim.sex.Sex`, the male build first; a variant which is no sex or a female first is a `ValueError`; the file of the male build as the female one is a `ValueError`), `data_paths` is `data_path` (the property `data_paths`, a tuple), `fit_definitions` is `fits` (the fit definitions of the project), `fits` as a sequence of fit ids is `adopted_ids` (the adopted fits; `adopted_fits()` maps them by subset, two fits of one subset raise), and a `FigureSettings` of pkpdsim as `figures` is split into `figure_settings` (`sbmlsim_settings()`) and `figures` (`outputs()`, a `FigureOutputs` of the formats and `savefig_dpi`), a plain sbmlsim `FigureSettings` goes to `figure_settings`; a keyword and its field together are a `ValueError`. `package`, `factory_dir` (the directory of the male build by default) and `fits_dir` (`factory_dir.parent / "fits"` by default) are narrowed to be always set, derived in the before validator from the raw values, relative ones relative to `root` as the fields resolve them; the factory directory lies inside the root (sbmlsim). `replace(**fields)` gives a changed copy by the names of the fields (`Project.replace`); with other `variants` or another `root` it derives the directories again when they are the derived ones and not given (as the dataclass did), a `FigureSettings` as `figures` replaces `figure_settings` too. `model_path` is the property of `Project` (the male build), `variant_path(variant)` any build. A model needs its build and its factory. `PKPDModel` keeps what is pkpdsim's: `archive_path`, `results_path` and the result directories as properties (`results_dir`, `simulation_dir`, `uncertainty_dir`, `fit_dir`, `report_dir`, `identifiability_dir`, `credibility_dir`, below the package, which phase 3 of the consolidation removes), `reduced_selections`, `save_results`, `fit_identifiability`, the `ExtraAction`s of `run_simulation` and `extra_arguments` (references checked when the model is created, an action which repeats an action of `RUN_ACTIONS` or another is a `ValueError`), `adopted_ids` (with `adopted_fits()` from them; `title` and `fit_records()` are those of `Project`), `model_paths` (`Sex` to build), `female_model_path`, `model_path_of(sex)` (a `ValueError` naming the model and `female_model_path` for a sex without build), `load_roadrunner(sex)` (a build at the level of the model, the loader of the tests of a model), `load_credibility()` (a `TypeError` for a reference which is no `sbmlsim.credibility.Credibility`), `credibility_settings` (`CREDIBILITY_SETTINGS`, see `credibility/`) and the known failures of the battery checked against `BATTERY_CHECKS` of `sbmlsim.credibility.verification` and `PK_CHECKS` of `credibility/battery.py`. The default simulator of a model is `DEFAULT_SIMULATOR`, `SimulatorSettings(absolute_tolerance=1e-10, relative_tolerance=1e-10)` of sbmlsim; the processes belong to the run, `RUN_WORKERS` (1, serial, so a script without the guard `if __name__ == "__main__":` runs) is the default of `run_experiments` and `run_simulation_experiments`, which keep their signatures of the model packages (`experiment_classes`, `output_dir`, the result a `Path`; they override `Project.run_experiments`, `ty: ignore[invalid-method-override]`) and gain `n_workers`. The fit definitions of `load_fit_definitions()` carry the level of `SimulatorSettings.jit_optimization` (`FitDefinition.jit_optimization` of sbmlsim), so the problems of the fits, their reports, the identifiability analysis, `start_fits` and `pkpdsim-verify fits` load their models at it in every worker. `SimulatorSettings`, `CheckSettings`, `resolve`, `check_reference`, `normalize_formats` and the release settings (`sbmlsim.release.settings`) are sbmlsim's (a model sets their framework, core libraries, release hook and archive, see `release/`), `fit_subset` and `FIT_ID` are `sbmlsim.fit.result`'s and `adopted_record` is `Project.adopted_record`; pkpdsim re-exports none of them. `sex.py` holds the enum `Sex` alone, imported by the model object and by the library, which re-exports it, so that neither imports the other. `pkpdsim.model` imports `sbmlsim.plot` and `sbmlsim.project` at runtime, because `FigureSettings` derives from sbmlsim's. The model has no entry points of its own, the commands of `cli.py` resolve it by its package.

**The run of the experiments.** `PKPDModel.run_experiments` and `run_simulation_experiments` call `Project.run_experiments(self, ...)` of sbmlsim (`_run`) with the settings of the model: the simulator settings of the model or `simulator=`, `n_workers` the pool of the experiments (`None` by default, a worker per four experiments, at most one per CPU) and `scan_workers` the processes of the scans (`RUN_WORKERS`, 1, serial, by default: the default of pkpdsim before step 4b of the consolidation, restored in step 5b; `None` takes `n_workers`, sbmlsim's meaning), `save_results` of the model unless given, `reduced_selections` of the model, the figure settings and formats of the project; a relative output directory is below `simulation_dir`, with `uncertainty` (`sbmlsim.experiment.draws.UncertaintySettings`) below `uncertainty_dir`, `run_simulation_experiments` collects the figures (`collect_figures=True`, `sbmlsim.project.FIGURES_DIR`, `_figures`) and returns their directory, and the messages are shown on the rich console (`log.enable_rich_logging()`). A run which has a failed experiment or figure writes the report and collects the figures first and then raises `sbmlsim.experiment.ExperimentRunError` (sbmlsim). The `run.py` of pkpdsim (`create_simulator`, which is `SimulatorSettings.simulator(n_workers)` of sbmlsim, `COLLECTED_FORMATS`) was removed in step 4b of the consolidation.

**`cli.py`.** The commands as functions of a `PKPDModel`, wrappers of the run layer of sbmlsim since step 5b of the consolidation: they keep their options and defaults, build the spec of a kind of `python -m sbmlsim.run` and run it with `sbmlsim.run.execute` (`_execute`, a `ConfigurationError` of the plan is a usage error, exit 2) on the project `<package>:MODEL` (`project_reference`, a `ValueError` for a model which is not the model object of its package, so tests make a copy the model object with the fixture `as_model` of `tests/conftest.py`, which monkeypatches `MODEL` of the package). A kind needs an empty output and writes `run.json`, `spec.json`, `summary.json` and `progress.jsonl` into it, so a command runs it into the folder of its result below its old directory through a staged swap (`_staged`: the run into `.<name>.<uuid>` next to it, the result folder of the kind moved up inside it (`_moved_up`), an earlier result renamed aside, the staging renamed into place, the earlier removed; a failed run or plan removes the staging and keeps the earlier result, the stagings a killed run left are swept and logged (`_sweep`), an `OSError` of the move-up or the swap is an exit-1 message naming the `.old` folder when it is not restored, one of the run propagates; the identifiability report's `spec.json` is rewritten to name its input after the swap, `_relocated_input`; `_overlaps` also compares with `os.path.samefile`), refuses an output which is, contains or lies inside an input, the adopted fits, the factory or a build, or contains the package or its data (`_check_out`, usage error), plans a spec first (`_execute`: a plan error exits 2, a `ConfigurationError` of the run 1), so the files are where they were: `run_command` (actions `factory`, `simulate`, `all`, `list_experiments` and the extra actions; `simulate` is the kind `simulate` into `simulation/<group>` or `custom_selection` (`simulation_uncertainty/` with `--uncertainty`, the field `uncertainty`), unknown names warned and left out (the kind refuses them), the figures collected afterwards (`collect_figure_files`), `--open` opens `index.html`; `list_experiments` is `sbmlsim.run.list_analyses`; `factory` builds in place with `create_models` (library), `--rebuild` (new) runs the kind `factory` into `results/factory` and exits with 1 for a changed output; the extra actions are called as before with the results directory (library: the results are no empty output); `--workers` (`run_workers`): `model` the kind with `min(available_cpus(), ceil(n / EXPERIMENTS_PER_WORKER))` workers (serial below), a number as given, `auto` `Project.run_experiments(n_workers=None)` of the library (the kind resolves its workers), while an extra action gets `parse_workers` (`model` is `RUN_WORKERS`); a failed run collects its failures, the remaining actions run and the command prints them and exits with 1), `fit_command` (returns the records directory by fit id; the options of `sbmlsim.fit.cli.fit_cli` plus the identifiability options and `--start JSON_OR_FIT_ID`; the kind `fit` into `<output_dir>/<fit id>` with `opid` the id, `report`, `record_adopted`, `identifiability`, `profile`, `fisher`, the start resolved to a path for a finished fit of the output (`_start`), the records moved up from `<fit id>/<fit id>`; `--name` ignored with a warning; `--strategy single` keeps the old library path (`_fit_each`, `sbmlsim.fit.cli.run_fit`); `--adopt` checks a plain `FIT_ID` with `parameters.json` in the output first, runs the kind `adopt` into a temporary directory and `adopt_fit`s its record into `fits_dir`; a failed fit moves up the records it wrote (`finally`); `--cross-validation` runs `sbmlsim.credibility.cross_validate` of the library and stores the result as `cross_validation.json` in the record of the adopted fit in `fits_dir`, where the assessment reads it), `report_command` (the kind `report` into `<output_dir>/<name or report>`, `--adopted` per adopted fit into `<output_dir>/<fit id>`; several files or a file which is no `parameters.json` go to `report_cli`), `identifiability_command` (the kind `identifiability` into `<name>/analysis` and the kind `report` of it into `<name>/report`, the report moved up into `<name>`; a file which is no `parameters.json` goes to `identifiability_cli`) and `credibility_command` (the kind `credibility` into the folder `--output_dir`, `results/credibility` by default, through the staged swap with the outputs of the kind moved up from `credibility/` (`CREDIBILITY_DIR`); `_check_out` refuses an output of its inputs, `_check_credibility_out` a file or a directory with other files than an assessment (`credibility.json`, exit 1, as `write_outputs` did); `--from-json` is the input `record` (a record may be written again into its own directory); `fates.tsv` is the table of the hook, which sbmlsim's report writes without rows also when the battery did not run, so the outputs are the ones of pkpdsim 0.19; the options of an assessment with a record and the options of the uncertainty and the variability without them stay usage errors of the parser, an invalid setting is a plan error of the kind (exit 2); an error of the declaration, the evidence, the records or the lock (`LookupError`, `ValueError`, `TypeError`, `OSError`) exits 1; `--lock` moves the lock the kind writes into its output to `sbmlsim.credibility.lock.lock_path(model)`, the folder of the model). The console scripts (`run_simulation_main`, `run_fit_main`, `run_report_main`, `run_identifiability_main`, `run_credibility_main`, in `[project.scripts]`) all go through `main(name, argv)` and `COMMANDS`: the first argument is the dotted model package, `resolve_model` imports it and takes its `MODEL` (`ModelNotFoundError`, a usage error with exit 2, for a name which is no dotted package, a package or parent which is not found, no `MODEL`, a `MODEL` which is no `PKPDModel` or of another package; any other import error propagates with its traceback), a fit command needs fit definitions, the rest of the arguments goes to the command of the model, whose `prog` is `<command> <package>`; `--help` without a model prints the usage of the command (docs page `commands.md`). `--profile` after the package runs the command under sbmlsim's profiler, `sbmlsim.run.profiling.run_profiled([command, package, ...], profile_dir(model, command), module="pkpdsim.cli")`, into `profiling/<command>_<time>/` of the results of the model; the `__main__` of `cli` records the exit code with `sbmlsim.run.profiling.record_exit_code`; before 3.15 a usage error. `PKPDModel.extra_actions` are also the `actions` of the project (`ExtraActionFunction`, a frozen dataclass with the reference, which calls the old function with a namespace of the defaults of `extra_arguments` and the arguments of the spec), so the kind `action` runs them; giving `actions` directly is a `ValueError`; an argument of the spec which is no option of the parser of `extra_arguments` is a `ValueError`, a text is converted by the type of its option.

**`fit.py`.** What the fits of the models share beyond sbmlsim: `default_fit_settings` (`data_at_change` is sbmlsim's `DataAtChangeType.BEFORE`, the state before a change), `mapping_metadata` (the check of the metadata type, `PKPDMappingMetaData` by default, `TypeError`), `metadata_filter(metadata_type=PKPDMappingMetaData, /, *, excluded=None, **allowed)` (a typed `def` calling `sbmlsim.fit.filters.metadata_filter` with the PK metadata, so the calls of the models keep their signature; the class is positional-only and passed only for a local extension) and `check_sampling_sites(fit_mappings, sites)`, which checks the resolved sampling site of every mapping against a table `{observable id: SamplingSite}` and returns one problem per mismatch or unknown observable (a model test asserts it is empty). `PKPDMappingMetaData.filter_value` (the hook of `sbmlsim.fit.objects.MappingMetaData`) gives a filter the resolved sampling site. Everything else of a fit is sbmlsim's, imported from there (no re-exports): the filters `MetadataFilter`, `ObservableFilter`, `observable_filter`, `observable_id`, `NamedFilter`, `named_filter`, `NamedMappings`, `study`, `named` (`sbmlsim.fit.filters`), `select_mappings(project, ...)` (`sbmlsim.fit.helpers`), `FIT_ID`, `fit_subset`, `fit_date` (`sbmlsim.fit.result`), `start_point`, `with_start`, `fixed_values`, `current_fixed_fits`, `recorded_fixed_fits`, `FIXED_FITS`, `remainder_problems` (`sbmlsim.fit.project_fits`; `fit_command` refuses a fit whose remainders `1 - a - b` of a build can be negative at the upper bounds, `parser.error`) and `report_context`, `records_context` (`sbmlsim.fit.cli`, the title is `Project.title`).

**`metadata.py`.** The one vocabulary of the fit mappings of all models, 13 `StrEnum`s: `Tissue`, `Route`, `Dosing`, `Fasting`, `ApplicationForm`, `DataType`, `PKPDData`, `Health`, `Coadministration`, `Genotype`, `Phenotype`, `AdministrationSite` and `SamplingSite` (the site of a blood sample, the observers of `pkpdsim.pbpk`), with the conventional members `HEALTHY`, `NONE` (nothing coadministered) and `NR` (not reported); a member has one name and one value in every model, a study which needs a new member adds it here, and a member whose meaning is not plain from its value is defined by a comment. The values are readable text (no `_`, a genotype starts with its gene) and API as much as the names: models look members up by value (`Genotype(f"CYP2C9 {genotype}")`, `Route(route)`). `PKPDMappingMetaData` is the keyword-only dataclass of `MappingMetaData` with 13 fields: `tissue`, `route`, `application_form` (default `NR`), `dosing`, `fasting` (`NR`), `health`, `coadministration` (`NONE`), `genotype`, `phenotype`, `administration_site`, `data`, `data_type` (all `NR`), `sampling_site` (`None` by default, what a study states; read the site with `resolved_sampling_site()`, never from the field: it follows the tissue without a statement by the table `_DEFAULT_SITES`, plasma, serum and blood `ARM_VEIN`, arterial blood and plasma `ARTERIAL`, saliva, urine, feces, feces and urine, bile and none `NOT_APPLICABLE`; `__post_init__` refuses a blood site on a tissue without a blood sample, `NOT_APPLICABLE` on a blood tissue and a site other than `ARTERIAL` on an arterial tissue, and `tests/test_metadata.py` classifies every member of `Tissue`, so a new member fails until it is in the table; `to_dict`, the filters and `check_sampling_sites` read the resolved site; appended last so the subclasses of the models stay valid); `to_dict` gives member names in the order of the fields. A model uses the class as it is and extends it only for a facet no other model has, with a subclass in its `fitting/mapping_collections.py` which adds fields and neither redefines a field nor repeats a vocabulary (e.g. a diplotype of the subjects).

**`experiment.py`.** `PKPDSimulationExperiment`, the base of the experiments of a model: bound to the model object by `pkpd_model` (a `TypeError` without), `selections`, `Mr`, `labels`, `units`, `sex` (the sex of the subjects, whose build the simulations run, `Sex.MALE` by default; a group whose majority is women runs the female build) and `simulation_sexes` (the key of a simulation to its sex where it differs from `sex`, declared and not computed, since the runner loads the models before `simulations()`); provides `models` (the build of `sex` as `model`, the build of another sex of `simulation_sexes` as `model_<sex>`, `model_id(sex)`, only the builds it simulates; a sex the model object has no build of is a `ValueError` naming the experiment), `tasks` (from the simulations `initialize` created, each on the build of `sex_of(key)`, a classmethod since the sexes are declared; a key of `simulation_sexes` which is no simulation is a `ValueError`), `default_changes` (none), `data`, `load_dataframe`, `load_dataframes_by_substance`, `figures_by_sid`, the method `to_sid` and the impairment and PK maps as class attributes. The method `to_sid` gives the SId of `sbmlsim.experiment.to_sid(text)` and raises a `ValueError` naming both texts when two texts of an experiment give one SId. `data_units` (class attribute, name in the data to the definition `<name> = <expression>`) defines the units of the data which sbmlsim lacks (an international unit) with `sbmlsim.units.define_unit(definition, ureg)` (raises a `ValueError` for a name which is defined with another value, since sbmlsim's registry is one per process and ignores a redefinition, so the name carries the model and the substance, `IU_ramipril_renin`, and for an expression pint cannot evaluate) in `initialize`, before the data are read, so a refused definition fails its experiment and not the run (sbmlsim's runner creates the experiments outside of its error handling; a loader called without `initialize` defines them on its first call), and the loaders rename them in the unit columns with `sbmlsim.data.rename_units` (the unit alone or after a metric prefix; `DataSet.from_df` of sbmlsim gives `gmean` the unit of its row and `gsd` `dimensionless`); `data_unit_names()` (public, read-only mapping, defines the units on its first call) gives the names for a table which a model reads itself (apixaban's scan of the parameters). `add_doses(times, doses, model="model")` returns the `Change` of repeated doses which add to their dose states (`"PODOSE_x + <dose in the unit of the state in the loaded model>"`), so the rest of earlier doses stays (pkdb_models#105); `ValueError` for a model which is not loaded. `uncertainty` and `across()` (the dimension of the draws of a run over them, `None` without) are those of sbmlsim's `SimulationExperiment`, whose `initialize` runs the experiment over the draws, and `add_function(plot, task_id, y, label, color, linestyle="-")` a function of the results of a task as a curve of width 2.0 (`linestyle=None` gives the curve no line style) or, over the draws, the band of the run (the copies of inulin and gdeobdtpa and the `_across` of five scans).

**`pk.py`.** `PK_LABELS`, `PK_UNITS`, `nca_frame(result)` (a row per point: its position, every parameter with `<name>_unit`, `nca_flags`) and `scan_nca(res, key, *, dose, route, substance, lloq_fraction=None, options=None)`: the non-compartmental analysis of pkpdutils of a timecourse at every point of a scan as one batch (`ScanResult.to_timecourses`, `pkpdutils.nca`, the per-sample `lloq` relative to the maximum of every point), the parameters of `nca_single` per point to 1e-12, about 40 times faster for 200 points (pkdb_models replaces its per point loops of `nca_single`).

**`sensitivity.py`.** The helpers of the points of a scan (`single_dimension`, `scan_dimensions`, `point_timecourse`, `point_band`, `state_after_changes`, `start_values`, `end_values`) are `sbmlsim.result.scan`. The runner of the sensitivity scripts of the models and the table of their parameters are sbmlsim's (`sbmlsim.sensitivity.runner`: `SensitivityRunner`, `SensitivityGroup`, `SensitivityOutput`, `LocalAnalysis`, `SamplingAnalysis`, `FastAnalysis`, `SobolAnalysis`, `CommandLine`, `run_subfolders`, `sample_size` with `SBMLSIM_SENSITIVITY_N`, `SEED`; `sbmlsim.sensitivity.parameters`: `parameters_from_sbml`, `set_bounds`, `set_relative_bounds`, `parameters_to_df`, `write_parameters`, `distributions`, `free_parameters`, `build_values`, `design_factors`, `relative_design`, `ParameterType`, `SensitivityParameter`), imported from there, no re-exports: a script builds `SensitivityRunner(project=MODEL, ..., out=MODEL.results_dir / "sensitivity")` (a `PKPDModel` is the project, its builds per sex the variants, `SensitivityGroup(variant=Sex.FEMALE)`, its `simulator` the tolerances) and runs it with `main` (`--workers`, `--out`) or `run(names, workers=)`; a model may list its runners in the project field `sensitivities` (the kind `sensitivity` of `sbmlsim.run`). `pkpdsim.sensitivity` keeps what is PK or of the PBPK library: `pk_observables(prefix, selection, outputs, *, dose, route, options, per_dose_mass, mr, pk_id)` (the `PK` analysis `pk_<prefix>` and a `Formula` `<prefix>_<key>` per output, an output of `per_dose_mass` divided by `at(<mr>, 0)`, which it requires; the `observables` of a runner, a list or a function building them at the start of a run, an output may name `<pk_id>.<key>` directly) and `max_after_dose(time, values)` (the maximum of one selection from time 0 on, the function of a `Custom`); for the models of the PBPK library `library_parameters(model_path, ids, fit_ids=, scaling_ids=, references=, relative=0.15)` (the table of the parameters an analysis names: fitted and other parameters `value*(1 +- relative)`, scaling factors `1 +- relative`, the severities of `SEVERITIES` (`CP_severity` 3, `HF_severity` 4) from 0 to `relative*top`, the reference from the sources of the notes, `notes_sources`) and `NonzeroLocalAnalysis` (the `LocalAnalysis` of sbmlsim of the parameters whose value is not 0, overriding `varied`; a severity at 0 has no relative change). It imports nothing of `pkpdsim.pbpk` (`tests/pbpk/test_equilibration.py`); a library model equilibrates the points of a design with `SensitivityGroup(prepare=pkpdsim.pbpk.Equilibration(resets=...))`.

**Uncertainty of the fitted parameters.** The bands of the predictions of a model over the uncertainty of its fitted parameters (docs page `uncertainty.md`); the module `uncertainty.py` of pkpdsim was removed in step 4b of the consolidation, the run over the draws is sbmlsim's `sbmlsim.experiment.draws`. The records of the adopted fits are sbmlsim's (`sbmlsim.fit.records`): `adopt_fit(fit_dir, fits_dir, force=)` (`run_fit <package> --adopt <fit id> [--force]`) copies the record of a finished fit into `PKPDModel.fits_dir` (`fits_dir`, default `factory_dir.parent / "fits"`, tracked with the model); `PKPDModel.adopted_ids` lists the adopted fits (`adopted_fits` by subset) and `Project.fit_records()` reads them (`AdoptedRecords` of `AdoptedRecord`s, a `ParameterUncertainty` per fitted parameter, `AdoptedRecords.parameter(pid, unit=)` the kwargs of a factory `Parameter`); `parameter_distributions(sbml)` and `uncertainty_dimension(sbml, draws, seed)` are those of `sbmlsim.simulation.sampling`, `UNCERTAINTY_DIMENSION` of `sbmlsim.distrib` (light, `pkpdsim.pbpk` reads it). `run_simulation <package> --uncertainty [--draws 100] [--seed 1234] [--quantiles 0.05 0.95]` (`sbmlsim.experiment.draws.UncertaintySettings`) draws once per run (`UncertaintyRun.create`, in `Project.run_experiments`), passes the run as the setting `uncertainty` of the runner (`SimulationExperiment.settings`) and writes into `results_dir / "simulation_uncertainty"` (`PKPDModel.uncertainty_dir` for a relative directory); the `initialize` of sbmlsim's `SimulationExperiment` calls `apply_uncertainty` (every simulation a `Scan` over the draws, a scan with the draws appended, a target which the simulation or a dimension sets is left out of its draws and a simulation without draws runs once; every curve of a task over the draws becomes a `Band` with the name and colour of the curve, a curve of more than one `over` dimension raises) and checks the figures again; `SimulationExperiment.uncertainty` is the run or `None` for the figures an experiment draws itself, with `point_band` and `scan_dimensions` of `sbmlsim.result.scan`. `sbmlsim.experiment.draws.check_fit_parameters(model)` (in `sbmlsim.project_checks.check_model`) fails a parameter of an adopted fit which is missing in a build of the model (every variant, the problem names the variant `'female'` and its file when there are two), has another value (rtol 1e-12), no distribution or (in the female build) another distribution than in the default variant, the male build: the draws are those of the male build (`UncertaintyRun.create`) and run on every build.

**The reports of the adopted fits.** `run_report <package> --adopted [-o DIR] [--no-mapping-figures]` is intercepted in `report_command` (`_adopted_reports`) and runs the kind `report` of sbmlsim with `adopted` once per fit of `model.adopted_ids` (its subset from the id), into `output_dir / <fit id>` (`model.report_dir` by default), the report of the kind moved up into it; one adopted fit per subset (`adopted_fits()`). The tests share the adopted toy fit of `tests/conftest.py` (`toy_fit`, `fits_dir`).

**`credibility/`.** The evidence and the battery of the credibility of a PBPK model (docs page `credibility.md`). The credibility itself is `sbmlsim.credibility` since step 7 of the consolidation (the declaration `Credibility`/`ContextOfUse`, the selection, the lock, the records, the assessment, the verification with the open `Check` ids, the generic battery, the uncertainty, the variability, the cross-validation, the report and the scorecard; pkpdsim re-exports none of them, a declaration imports from `sbmlsim.credibility`); pkpdsim keeps what is specific to the models of pkdb_models, registered by every `PKPDModel` through `credibility_settings` (`model.CREDIBILITY_SETTINGS`, references so a model package imports none of it: `metadata_type` `pkpdsim.metadata:PKPDMappingMetaData`, `domain_fields` `model.DOMAIN_FIELDS` (tissue, route, application form, dosing, fasting, health, coadministration, genotype, phenotype), the `extensions` ratios, temporal, inputs and studies in this order (the order of the acceptance rows, the factor statements and the blocks of the report) and the `battery` `PK_BATTERY`; a model may still set `battery_settings`, e.g. `{"steady_state": False}`). The four modules are `sbmlsim.credibility.extensions.EvidenceExtension`s (contract in the module docstring of `sbmlsim/credibility/extensions.py`), each with an instance (`RATIOS`, `TEMPORAL`, `INPUTS`, `STUDIES`, together `EXTENSIONS` of `__init__.py`) and the HTML of its blocks rendered from the macros of `resources/templates/credibility_extensions.html` (`blocks.render_macro`, the environment of the reports of sbmlsim with the filter `number`): `ratios.py` (step 3: `Ratio(observable, numerator, denominator, summary=Summary.AUC)` is the `Pairing` of a ratio quantity, which the extension claims (`claims`); `GuestAcceptance(minimum, delta=1.25)` is the setting `ContextOfUse.extensions["ratios"]` (it has `problems()`), else `DEFAULT_RATIO_ACCEPTANCE` of the risk, 50/67/80 % (`ratio_rule`); `pairings` per fit and study of the decisive points, a study with two denominators a problem of the selection (`selection_problems`) and a `SelectionError` of the assessment; `summarize`, `guest_limit`, `ratio_pairs` (the metadata of the numerator by the fields of the domain of the project, `prepare`); `assess_context` for a COU with a `Ratio` only: the section `{"ratios": [pairs, NaN as None]}`, the rule as an acceptance row with the extension `ratios` (label `ratio pairs`), which joins the verdict, the verification and the uncertainty (`acceptance` on other `IPRED`), the evaluable pairs whose numerator lies in the claimed domain as the points of the quantity, the factor relevance of the quantities (`1 of 1 ratio pair within the limits of Guest et al. (>= 80 %), 1 of 1 ratio quantity covered.`); the block of the pairs after the acceptance, `ratios.tsv`, and `pool(contexts, {"field": ...})` the qualification (`QUALIFICATION_COLUMNS`, the field `health` by default; `pkpdsim-verify scorecard` calls `sbmlsim.credibility.scorecard.pool(records, RATIOS, {"field": options.field})`)), `temporal.py` (`Chronology` and `chronology(project)` from the study records of the data paths and the dates of the adopted fits; `temporal_record` for a COU decided on validation data, the section `{"temporal": {...}}`, the factor output comparison after the core statement (`temporal_statement`), the block after the acceptance, `temporal.tsv`, the scorecard columns `temporal_points` and `temporal_met` with the regression of `temporal_met` from True), `inputs.py` (`input_checks(project, fits, covariates)`, the covariates of the declaration or `DEFAULT_COVARIATES` `{"BW": "weight", "HEIGHT": "height"}` when it declares `None` (an empty mapping checks none); the checks are the section `{"inputs": [...]}` of the assessment (`inputs.tsv`), `inputs_record` of the decisive mappings the section of a COU (`input_ranges.tsv`, the block after the uncertainty), its statement before the core one in the factor equivalency of inputs when a decisive mapping is checked, the scorecard column `inputs_differ`) and `studies.py` (`data_paths`, `study_folders`, `study_data`: the section `{"studies": [...]}` of the assessment, `studies.tsv` and the section Study data of the report, `report_section`). `battery.py` is the `BatteryHook` of the battery of sbmlsim (`PKBattery`, `PK_BATTERY`, name `pk`): `FlatBuild.read` (the conventions `Mr_<s>`, the dose states `IVDOSE_`/`PODOSE_`/`SOLDOSE_`, `Ri_<s>`, the sinks, the dose chains, the flows with a parameter `battery_flow_<r>` each, with `pbpk.flat_model.accumulating`/`math_names`) is what the hook reads of a variant, its SBML the one the battery simulates, `dose_cases` the cases (`DOSE` 10 mg of every dose, an infusion over `INFUSION` 60 min with its stop, repeated by the second engine, and the small doses of the superposition, `<dose> small at 0`, ..., not repeated); `settings()` `HORIZON` 2880 min on `STEPS` 288, rtol 1e-10, atol 1e-16, `steady_state=True`, `engines=("amici",)`; `not_applicable(check, data)` leaves a build without a substance (no `Mr_<s>`, e.g. the toy models of the tests) out of the steady state with the reason of 0.19 (non-negativity, the cross simulator and the plausibility run on every build, ruling of round 2); `report_tables({})` gives `fates.tsv` without rows, which sbmlsim's report writes also without a battery; `contribute` the checks `mass_balance`, `zero_dose` and `superposition` (`PK_CHECKS`, registered with `register_check`, factor numerical code verification; not applicable without a substance with a molecular weight, the mass balance and the superposition without a dose) and the section `fates` (`Fate`, `fates.tsv`, the table after the battery); `order` the order of pkpdsim 0.19 (mass balance, non-negativity, zero dose, steady state, superposition, cross simulator, plausibility, rebuild). The generic checks (non-negativity and the steady state on every model, the cross simulator through sbmlsim's engines with a compile deadline, plausibility, the rebuild through `Project.create_models`) are sbmlsim's. `run_credibility` (`cli.credibility_command`) runs the kind `credibility` (see `cli.py`). `tests/data/credibility/toy-0.19.json` and `toy-uncertainty-0.19.json` are records of the toy credibility model written by pkpdsim 0.19; `tests/test_credibility_assessment.py` checks that the assessment by sbmlsim with the extensions gives their verdicts and evidence.

**`database/`.** The parameter database `pkpdsim.database` (docs page `database.md`), the numbers of the PBPK library apart from the code which uses them; it imports no other module of pkpdsim, the library reads it (`pkpdsim.pbpk.data`) and holds no numbers of its own. `sources.toml` (every publication by key, `ModelChoice`; `tables.sources`, `tables.cite`), `tables/*.toml` (the tables of the library, `tables/populations/`; `tables.load(name)`, `tables.load_folder(name)`, `tables.entry(name, section, key)` an `Entry` with the value, unit, source, notes and the `Uncertainty` a number states, `sd`, `se`, `cv`, `n`, `ci`; `pkpdsim.pbpk.data.value` makes it a `Value` whose uncertainty is a distrib uncertainty of the SD, SE, CV, sample size and CI, no distribution, `sbml_uncertainty`) and `studies/<source>/` (`studies.py`: `study.toml` with the tissue (`liver`, `body` for the quantities of the subjects), the control group (optional, a study without gives levels and no ratios), `via` (the source of a review whose table is read, `Li2015`), the groups, `Group` with `condition` (`CONDITIONS`), `child_pugh`, `etiology` (`ETIOLOGIES`) and an `assumption`, and the groups with a class of their condition (`CONDITIONS` control, cirrhosis, ckd, heart_failure; `child_pugh` A-C, `ckd_stage` of `CKD_STAGES` mild, moderate, severe, end stage, dialysis, `nyha` I-IV, each only on its condition, a group of several classes has none and gives levels of the condition only), the measurements of `KINDS` `protein`, `mrna` and `physiology`, one TSV file of records each, `Record` per entity (HGNC symbol, or a key of `QUANTITIES` for `physiology`, `Quantity(description, unit)`: every record of a quantity is in its unit, another printed unit is converted on entry with the printed numbers in the note, a record in another unit is a `ValueError`) and group with its `Summary` as printed (`n`, `n_detected`, `mean`, `sd`, `se`, `cv_percent`, `median`, `q1`, `q3`, `min`, `max`, `ci_low`/`ci_high` of the median, a `unit` column per row); a record whose statistics disagree, `Summary.inconsistencies`, keeps them and has a note, `check()` lists one without, the tests keep it empty; `studies()`, `records(kind, entity, tissue)`). `integration.py` integrates the studies: `log_location` (the log median with its SE: of the median CI, else of the quartiles `(ln q3 - ln q1)/1.349`, else of the log-normal SD `sqrt(ln(1 + CV^2))` of mean and SD with `sqrt(pi/2)*sigma/sqrt(n)`, else the geometric mean of mean and SD, the SD of a group with the SE alone `se*sqrt(n)`; a group without a spread takes the median `sigma` of its measurement, then of its kind, `imputed`; a median or mean of 0 is excluded with the reason, `Exclusion`), `study_estimate` (the group of the condition without an etiology or `mixed`, else the strata pooled by inverse variance, minus the control: `Estimate`; a comparison with a group of q1 or median 0 or under 75 % detected, `zero_inflated`, takes the ratio of the arithmetic means, `mean_location`, one with q3 0 or under 25 % detected, `mostly_undetected`, is excluded), `random_effects` (DerSimonian and Laird, `tau^2`, Q, I^2), `consensus(entity, condition, kind=, tissue=)` (cached, `Consensus` with `value`, `ci`, `prediction_se` `sqrt(se^2 + tau^2)`, `sources`, `text()`), `factor(entity, condition, kinds=("protein", "mrna"))` (the mRNA only without a protein abundance), `entities` and `level(entity, condition, kind="physiology", tissue="body")` (`Level`, `LevelEstimate`: the mean with its SE or SD/sqrt(n), or the median with the SE of its quartiles, the groups of a study by inverse variance, the studies by DerSimonian and Laird on the linear scale, e.g. the MELD of a class). The studies: Drozdzik 2021 (Tables S2 and S3, protein and mRNA of the CYPs and UGTs by etiology and Child-Pugh class), Drozdzik 2020 (the transporters of the same cohorts, digitized from the box plots of Figures 1, 2, 5 and 6 with `scripts/database/digitize_boxplot.py` and the configurations of `scripts/database/drozdzik2020/`, the images of `pdfimages` not in the repository; the means checked against the text in the tests), Wang 2016 (the transporters of the livers of Prasad 2018 per gram of liver, Excel box plots of Figures 2 and 3, `scripts/database/wang2016/` with the options `dark`, `min_run`, `thick` and `box_fraction`), El-Khateeb 2021 (Table S8, 51 enzymes and transporters by class) and Prasad 2018 (Table 3, end-stage alcoholic and HCV cirrhosis, class C assumed); the physiology of the classes from the primary studies which Li 2015 tabulate (`via`, albumin, hematocrit, cardiac index, portal and hepatic arterial flow and perfusion, liver volume) and the open-access cohorts with the MELD by class (Mesropyan 2022, Hong 2025, Kim 2020, Yang 2020, Xu 2026); CKD by stage (hemoglobin, albumin, AGP, ASL perfusion, ERPF, kidney volume and length, gastric emptying; 19 studies, the controlled ones Lin 2017, Panjeta 2017, Zakiyanov 2013, Huang 2017, Zhang X 2024, Brown 2020, Li LP 2017, ...) and heart failure (10 studies: by NYHA class without controls, Aquilani 2017, Yamabe 1992, or of several classes against controls, Myers 1948, Mokotoff 1948, Jung 2021, Muller 1992, Zelis 1968, Mason 1964, Carlsson 2012); the CKD factors on the hematocrit (consensus of `hemoglobin`), the kidney flow (`renal_cortical_perfusion`) and the albumin of the binding are consensus entries, the in vivo ontogeny of the hepatic CYP3A4 and CYP1A2 is digitized from Figure 1 of Upreti 2016 with `scripts/database/digitize_curve.py` and `scripts/database/upreti2016/figure1.json` (the equations of their Table 1 contradict their own figure, see the notes of the entries); the heart failure factors are the consensus of `hepatic_blood_flow_per_bsa`, `renal_blood_flow_per_bsa` and `forearm_blood_flow`, i.e. Leithe 1984 (Figure 1 digitized with `scripts/database/digitize_barchart.py` and `scripts/database/leithe1984/`; its subgroups of the cardiac index taken as NYHA II-IV, NYHA I interpolated); the CKD gastric/small intestinal transit are Malik 2020 (Table 2), the kidney volume stays 1 (the processes of the kidney are per litre and scale with the renal function already, a shrinking kidney would count the nephron loss twice; Malik's regression in the notes), the cirrhosis factors without consensus cite Edginton 2008 Table I (read); the liver tissue `functional_liver` (`f_functional`, tissue volume and capacity of the liver) is the consensus of `liver_volume_per_bw`, since the protein factors are per amount of (cirrhotic) tissue and the hepatocyte count of Edginton would count the loss twice; the AGP row of the CKD binding is the consensus of Docci 1985 (by serum creatinine) and Zakiyanov 2013 (end stage only, earlier stages interpolated; Schmulenson 2018 in the notes); `tables/comparisons.toml` holds the Simcyp cirrhosis parameters of Johnson 2010 (Table III) for the generated comparison `impairment-child-pugh-comparison`, no factor reads it. `tables/adipose_human.toml` holds the adipose tissue and the physiology of obesity (`pkpdsim.pbpk.adipose`): the visceral share of Shen 2009 over age, the waist circumference of the covariates (`waist`, NHANES 2013-2018 anchored on Haueise 2023) with the sensitivity of the visceral depot to it (`visceral_waist`, Haueise 2023), the rate of a change of the body weight and the residual factors of overweight and obesity over the body mass index from the repository of Berton 2022, with the Simcyp parameters of Ghobadi 2011 as the alternative in the notes. The factors of cirrhosis on the proteins of the liver of the library are their consensus per class (`pkpdsim.pbpk.impairment.child_pugh_protein`, `child_pugh_proteins`), an entry of any section of the table (`child_pugh`, `ckd`, `ckd.binding`, `heart_failure`) may name the quantity whose consensus it is (`consensus = "albumin"`, `class_factors(section, key)` with `_SECTIONS` mapping a section to its classes and the field of the groups; the other factors keep their values), four significant digits, a class without a study interpolated (a model choice, `_cp_factors`); the MELD maps onto `CP_severity` piecewise linearly through `meld_anchors()` (healthy `MELD_HEALTHY` 6.43, the smallest score of the formula, and the consensus MELD of each class), `cp_severity_of_meld` (clamped to 0 to 3) and `meld_of_cp_severity`; the docs tables `impairment-meld` and `database-physiology`. Tests in `tests/test_database.py`.

**`impairment.py`, `pk.py`.** The clinical classifications `CHILD_PUGH_CLASSES`, `KDIGO_GFR_CLASSES` (G1-G5 with G3a/G3b, the value of a class its midpoint eGFR, G5 at 7.5, G1 `None` the renal function 1) and `NYHA_CLASSES` and `BMI_CLASSES` (the WHO classes of the body mass index, `BMI`; `Classification` of `ImpairmentClass` with key, label, color, definition, value of the library parameter and limits; `CLASSIFICATIONS`), which `PBPKSimulationExperiment.class_changes(classification, key)` (`CP_severity`/`HF_severity`, or `f_renal_function` = eGFR over `normal_gfr` of the build), `class_simulations(simulation, classification, prefix=)` and `add_classes(plot, classification, prefix=, yid=)` scan and draw; the severities are continuous and the conditions multiply (`fimp_<id>`, hepatorenal and cardiorenal combinations, the CKD factors on the intrinsic eGFR only so the decline in the Child-Pugh and NYHA data is not counted twice): `severity_values`/`severity_changes(cp_severity= or meld=, egfr= or renal_function=, hf_severity=)` give a point, `impairment_dimension(id, ...)` a sbmlsim dimension whose severities change together (two dimensions a grid), `Classification.color_of(value)` the interpolated color; the generated heat maps `combinations-figure`; docs page `pbpk/classes.md` with the definitions (Child-Pugh score, MELD, KDIGO, FDA/EMA stages, NYHA, ACC/AHA) and the generated figures of the classes and of the factors over the severity (`classes-figure`, `factors-*` of `scripts/pbpk_tables.py`, inline SVG). The presets of the renal function (`RENAL_FUNCTION`, `RENAL_FUNCTION_GFR`, `RENAL_FUNCTION_90`), the cirrhosis and the cardiac function with their colors, and `PK_LABELS`/`PK_UNITS`; the values are those of the models, the floats bit-identical. `CHILD_PUGH` and `HEART_FAILURE` (with `HEART_FAILURE_COLORS`) map the classes onto the severities `CP_severity` and `HF_severity` of the models built on the PBPK library (`pkpdsim.pbpk.impairment`).

**`verify.py`.** The command `pkpdsim-verify` is a thin wrapper of `python -m sbmlsim.run verify` (`sbmlsim.verify`, which holds the subcommands `dump`, `compare`, `ranges`, `fits`, `definitions`, `settings`, `png`, `sbml`, `scorecard`, the dump with its `builds`, `versions`, `--grid`, the renames and the exit codes 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4), `verify(args)` translates the arguments and runs it: `--model PACKAGE` is `--project PACKAGE:MODEL` (`MODEL_ATTRIBUTE`, a package without `MODEL` exits with 4, a configuration error; `dump`, `fits`, `definitions` and `settings` still require `--root` and `--model`), `scorecard` adds `--extensions pkpdsim.credibility:EXTENSIONS --pool ratios` and `--field health` unless given. `PKPDSimulationExperiment.variant_of(simulation)` is the value of its sex (the hook of `SimulationExperiment`), so a dump records the sex of the build of every task as `builds`, and the versions of a dump are sbmlsim and the `core_libraries` of the release settings of the model (pkpdsim, sbmlsim, sbmlutils). `RatiosExtension.pool_lines` words the qualification (the rows of the model `all`) as the share of the ratio pairs within the limits of Guest et al. The migration helpers for sbmlsim 0.8 figures (`align_figures`) and `tests/data/figures-sbmlsim-0.8.5.json` are gone. Tests: `tests/test_verify.py` (the wrapper on the toy packages) and the scorecard in `tests/test_cli_credibility.py`.

**`release/`.** The release of a model repository (`hctz-model`) from a model package of pkdb_models is sbmlsim's since step 8 of the consolidation (`sbmlsim.release`: the kind `release` of its run layer builds a release into a clone of the release repository in an out, `python -m sbmlsim.release publish` publishes it); pkpdsim keeps what is pharmacokinetic and its command (docs page `release.md`). `release/hook.py` is the release hook of pkpdsim, `PKReleaseHook` and its instance `HOOK`: `templates` is `pkpdsim/resources/release` with the three parts of the page of the credibility which the page of sbmlsim includes (`docs/credibility-acceptance.md.j2`, the ratio pairs and the temporal validation after the acceptance table of a context, `docs/credibility-uncertainty.md.j2`, the inputs between the parameter uncertainty and the variability of a context, as in 0.19, and `docs/credibility-sections.md.j2`, the study data of the adopted fits after the sections of the core), `credibility` gives the page the sections `ratios`, `temporal` and `inputs` of every context and the `studies` of the assessment and keeps in the acceptance table the rows of the core followed by the rule of the ratio pairs as `(ratio pairs)` (the rules of the other extensions have tables of their own, as in 0.19), `data_study` names a file of `data/<substance>/<study>/...` of the package `<substance>/<study>`, the names of the release notes of 0.19, and the release notes have no sections of pkpdsim (`notes_sections` is the default). The release settings of a model are a `PKPDReleaseSettings` (`model.py`), the frozen keyword-only dataclass `ReleaseSettings` of sbmlsim with the defaults of pkpdsim, `framework="pkpdsim"`, `core_libraries=("pkpdsim", "sbmlsim", "sbmlutils")`, `library_owner="matthiaskoenig"` and `hook="pkpdsim.release.hook:HOOK"` (a reference, so `import pkpdsim.model` imports no release), so a field the package gives keeps its value, also `framework="sbmlsim"` (a dataclass does not record which fields were given, the defaults do); the before validator of `PKPDModel` (`_release`) refuses the release settings of sbmlsim themselves (a `ValueError`) and sets `archive` to `archive_path` relative to `root` (none without it, a `ValueError` outside of the root). `release/cli.py` is `pkpd_release PACKAGE --version X.Y.Z [--repo PATH] [--out DIR] [--no-publish] [--pkpdsim-path PATH] [--workers N]` (`release(model, ...)` returns the release repository of the build, `<out>/repository`), a wrapper of the kind and of the publishing: it checks the version, `--pkpdsim-path` only with `--no-publish`, the release settings, the model object of the package (`pkpdsim.cli.project_reference`), a clean source and, when publishing, the archive and the tools (`check_tools`) before the build; the checkout `--repo` (by default the sibling of the source repository named like the release repository) is only read, the kind clones it (`repository` of the spec; the repository on GitHub when it is missing, no checkout is created), the out is `--out` or `default_out`, `<checkout>-release-<version>` next to it (`-local` appended for a build with `--pkpdsim-path`, `LOCAL_SUFFIX`, so a review with a local pkpdsim and the release do not share an out), and the kind runs with `resume=True`, so a second build of the same spec builds again into it, while an out of another build (another version, checkout or local pkpdsim, or files of no build) is refused before the run with "remove `<out>` or pass --out" (`_check_out`: `sbmlsim.run.check_out` with `resume=True`, its `OutInUse` worded by its `reason`, `_OUT_IN_USE`, instead of the message of the run layer about spec files); a missing checkout is logged at `INFO` (the GitHub fallback); the refusals of the clone (on another branch, diverged, stale commits) and of a checkout with unpushed commits are sbmlsim's, which say what to do (remove the out, push the commits), and a clone behind `origin/main` is fast-forwarded by sbmlsim; the build is `sbmlsim.release.workflow.execute_release` with the runner of `release`, a publishing run skips it when `sbmlsim.release.workflow.publishable` finds the build of the release in the out (the release commit of the version with its tag missing or at `HEAD`, or the unchanged build of the source commit with a publishable lock, as 0.19), then `sbmlsim.release.workflow.publish_release` publishes and a clean checkout on `main` is fast-forwarded to the release (`_fast_forward`, a warning otherwise). A refusal of the kind (`ConfigurationError`) and a failed stage (`ReleaseError`) exit with 1. The commands run through the `runner` of `release` (`sbmlsim.release.source.run` by default), which the tests pass a fake for (`tests/test_release_cli.py`, with the fixtures `git_env`, `source_repo_model`, `remote_and_clone`, `release_settings` of `conftest.py`, which point `sbmlsim.release.source.clone_url` at a bare remote, and `tests/data/release/uv.lock`, a source lock with pkpdsim 0.8.0); `tests/test_release_hook.py` checks the settings, the studies of the release notes and the PK parts of the credibility page rendered by `sbmlsim.release.docs.write_docs`. No network, docker or gh.
